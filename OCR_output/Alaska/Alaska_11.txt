REAFFIRMATION OF COOPERATION
BETWEEN
THE PROVINCE OF ALBERTA
("ALBERTA")
AND
THE STATE OF ALASKA
("ALASKA")
Thel Province of Alberta and the State of Alaska ("the Participants") recognize the mutual benefits
of their collaboration and common commitment in the pursuit of economic progress and
prosperity.
Thel Participants have already established the Alaska-Alberta Bilateral Council to engage in areas
of mutual interest.
Thel Participants desire to affirm and to expand upon the Alberta-Alaska Bilateral Council, signed
between. Alberta and Alaska on May 17, 2004.
Thel Participants have arrived at the following mutual understanding:
Thel Participants intend to continue to support efforts to build on their collaboration and
shared interests.
Thel Participants will deepen their cooperation with respect to transportation, market
access, energy and environmental issues.
Thel Participants have the intention to extend their collaboration to discussions between
each jurisdiction to advance the aims of the Alberta-Alaska Bilateral Council.
THE PARTICIPANTS REAFFIRM their commitment to grow and develop the Alberta-Alaska
relationship surrounding areas of mutual interest, and will endeavor to work collaboratively.
This Reaffirmation mayl be signed in counterparts, in which case the counterparts together shall
constitute one fully-signed Reaffirmation.
