DECLARATION OF COOPERATION BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF
SONORA, THROUGH THE DEPARTMENT OF INFRASTRUCTURE AND URBAN
DEVELOPMENT AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF ARIZONA, THOUGH THE
DEPARTMENTO TRANSPORTATION, FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A COOPERATIVE AND
COLLABORATIVE INITIATIVE AND THE PARTICIPATION IN THE SAFETY CORRIDOR
PROGRAM FOR THE CORRIDOR BETWEEN THE BORDER, AT LUKEVILLE-SONOYTA AND
PUERTO PENASCO, SONORA.



Declaration of cooperation between the Government of the State of Sonora, through the
Department of Infrastructure and Urban Development (SIDUR) and the Government of the
State of Arizona, though the Department Transportation (ADOT), for the establishment of a
cooperative and collaborative initiative and the participation in the Safety Corridor
Program between Lukeville/Sonoyta and Puerto Penasco, Sonora.
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the States of Arizona and Sonora share a border of 568 kilometers (353 miles)
and have 6 ports of entry that connect: the two states;
WHEREAS, the States of Arizona and Sonora have a long history of collaboration in cultural,
social, entrepreneurial and shared history, founded on a common entrepreneurial spirit;
while the Arizona-Sonora region is a vibrant binational economy that has experienced an
important level of growth and diversification;
WHEREAS, Arizona and Sonora are strategically situated as ideal gateways for their
respective domestic markets;
WHEREAS, the tourism destination of Puerto Penasco, Sonora, has become a major
destination for tourists from Arizona that want to visit Sonora beaches;
WHEREAS, thousands of Arizonans travel along this corridor throughout the year and in
greater numbers during key vacation periods;
WHEREAS, due to increased flows there has been an increase in accidents and road safety
concerns;
WHEREAS, ADOT has implemented several safety programs and initiatives to enhance the
safety of Arizona roads and highways;
WHEREAS, Arizona and Sonora are committed to the safety of the traveling public on their
respective roads and highways.



THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
FIRST - That ADOT and SIDUR agree to establish a prevention and information campaign
based on safe driving practices, to be called Safety Corridor Program, to be deployed in the
corridor connecting Lukeville/Sonoyta with Puerto Penasco, Sonora.
SECOND - That ADOT and SIDUR, along with other relevant safety driven organizations in
both states, will share information on best practices on road and highway safety programs
and offer reciprocal training opportunities for safety personnel.
THIRD - That ADOT and SIDUR, will create an informational campaign for the traveling
public and distribute information throughout the Safety Corridor between
Lukeville/Sonoyta and Puerto Penasco, Sonora.
FOURTH - That ADOT and SIDUR will enhance Safety Corridor Program outreach efforts
during key vacation periods such as the long weekends associated with Memorial Day, the
4th of July, and Labor Day.
This statement is signed on Friday, December 1 of the year two thousand and seventeen, within
the framework of the Plenary Session between the Arizona-Mexico Commission and the Sonora-
Arizona Commission, held in Puerto Penasco, Sonora.
This Declaration of Cooperation is intended to memorialize the Participants' commitment to
explore ways to promote collaboration between them, but it is non-binding and does not create any
legal or binding obligation between the states. Nothing in this Declaration of Cooperation should
be read to conflict with the laws and regulations of the United States of America or the United
Mexican States (or any state or local jurisdiction), or with any relevant international agreements.
There are no third-party beneficiaries to this Declaration of Cooperation.
The Arizona Department of Transportation and the Department of Infrastructure and Urban
Development of Sonora may terminate its participation under this Declaration of Cooperation at
any time through a written notice of termination. Nonetheless, the Arizona Department of
Transportation and the Department of Infrastructure and Urban Development of Sonora should
endeavor to give the other party 30 days' written notice ofits intention to terminate this Declaration
of Cooperation. The termination should not preclude the completion of any cooperative activities
agreed upon while the Declaration of Cooperation was valid. Each state will be responsible for its
own expenses in the implementation of this Declaration of Cooperation.
