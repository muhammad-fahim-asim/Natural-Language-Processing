DECLARATION OF COOPERATION BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF
SONORA, THROUGH THE DEPARTMENT OF INFRASTRUCTURE AND URBAN
DEVELOPMENT AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF ARIZONA, THROUGH THE
DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION, FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A COOPERATIVE
AND COLLABORATIVE ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT INITIATIVE AND PARTICIPATION IN
THE DEVELOPMENT OF A. SAFETY COMMERCIAL LOGISTICAL CORRIDOR PROGRAM.



Declaration of Cooperation between the Government of the State of Sonora, through the
Department of Infrastructure and Urban Development (SIDUR), and the Government of the
State of Arizona, though the Arizona Department Transportation (ADOT), for the
establishment of a cooperative and collaborative economic development initiative and
participation in the development of a Safety Commercial Logistical Corridor Program.
SIDUR and ADOT are collectively the "Participants."
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the States of Arizona and Sonora share a border of 568 kilometers (353 miles)
and have 6 ports of entry that connect the two states;
WHEREAS, the States of Arizona and Sonora have a long history of collaboration in cultural,
social, entrepreneurial and shared history, founded on a common entrepreneurial spirit;
WHEREAS, the Arizona-Sonora region is a vibrant binational economy that has experienced
significant growth and diversification;
WHEREAS, Arizona and Sonora are strategically situated as ideal gateways for their
respective domestic markets;
WHEREAS, both states are working together to create the Arizona-Mexico Corridor, a state-
of-the-art binational infrastructure corridor that is an ideal location for investment, job
creation and tourism;
WHEREAS, both states are committed to enhancing the safety of the traveling public;
WHEREAS, both states are committed to fostering a corridor that contributes to the safe
and secure movement of people and goods and enhances the competitiveness of companies
that invest in the region;
WHEREAS, both states recognize the intrinsic relationship between modern transportation
infrastructure systems and regional economic development;
WHEREAS, the advancement and development of innovative transport mechanisms
facilitate supply chain mobility and enhance the competitiveness of the region;
WHEREAS, Arizona and Sonora have jointly worked to promote and implement truck
driver training programs that have positively impacted compliance with safety
requirements and enhanced the safety of roads and highways in the region;
WHEREAS, Sonora and Arizona have advocated for and worked together to establish the
Safety Corridor between Lukeville and Puerto Penasco, Sonora with great results for the
safety of the traveling public;

WHEREAS, the deployment of smart technologies can contribute to the efficient and safe
flow of trucks and their cargo along the Arizona-Mexico Corridor;
That the use ofi information technology in freight transport is increasingly important and
useful in attracting investment, contributing to security throughout the logistics chain; and
WHEREAS, Arizona and Sonora have worked to create a border environment recognized
for its modern facilities and innovative processes, and a corridor that enhances the
competitiveness of the region and the companies that operate in it; and
WHEREAS, the Arizona Commerce Authority and the Secretary of Economy of the State of
Sonora, have been important partners for economic development in the region.
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
FIRST - That Arizona, through the Arizona Department of Transportation, and Sonora,
through the Department of Infrastructure and Urban Development, will endeavor to
continue to promote and offer technical certification programs that support truck drivers'
and trucking companies' compliance with road safety standards and regulations;
SECOND - That Arizona and Sonora will encourage the deployment ofinnovative systems
and technologies that contribute to the safety of the Arizona-Mexico Corridor and that
espouse the principles promoted by the Smart Borders Initiative in an effort to enhance the
competitiveness of the Arizona-Mexico Corridor;
THIRD - That Arizona and Sonora will promote the benefits of these efforts with the goal of
attracting new investments to the Arizona-Sonora Megaregion; and
FOURTH - That Arizona and Sonora will endeavor to share non-confidential information
with each other in order for Sonora to create the Safety Commercial Logistical Corridor
Program in an effort to enhance the efficient and safe flow oftrucks and supply chains
between both states.
This Memorandum of Understanding will become effective as of the date ofi its signature
and will remain in force for a period of five (5) years, extendable for periods of equal
duration.
This Declaration of Cooperation is signed on Friday, November 30 of the year two thousand
and eighteen, within the framework of the Plenary Session between the Arizona-Mexico
Commission and the Sonora-Arizona Commission, held in Hermosillo, Sonora.
This Declaration of Cooperation is intended to memorialize the Participants' commitment to
explore ways to promote collaboration between them, but it is non-binding and does not
create any legal or binding obligation between the states. Nothing in this Declaration of
