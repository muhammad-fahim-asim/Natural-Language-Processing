LETTER OF INTENT BETWEEN THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE
REPUBLIC OF THE UNITED MEXICAN STATES AND THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY
GENERAL OF THE STATE OF ARIZONA OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
The Office of the Attorney General of the Republic of the United Mexican States and the Office
of the Attorney General of the State of Arizona of the United States of America, hereinafter
referred as the Participants",and
CONSIDERING
That the Office of the Attorney General of the Republic of the United Mexican States and the
Office of the Attorney General of the State of Arizona of the United States of America share
common values and a vision of prosperity and security;
That the Participants seek to create and keep strategic and collaboration links;
That the collaboration between the Participants has as purpose to join efforts for increasing the
efficiency of the investigation and prosecution of crimes;
That the effective collaboration between law enforcement institutions is essential for fighting
transnational criminal organizations;
That the current international conditions and the challenges to fight organized crime, have
widened the Participants' role, whose primary objective is to guarantee justice, security, respect
to human rights and freedom for all their nationals within their jurisdictions;
That the crimes off trafficking and smuggling of persons, trafficking in firearms, money laundering
or operations with resources of illicit proceeding, drug trafficking or crimes against health, and
child pornography crimes committed in the Internet, transcend international borders, thus an
effective cooperation between the Participants is required;
That the Participants are dedicated to enhance binational cooperation, as expressed in the
International Border Conference, celebrated in Scottsdale, Arizona, United States of America,
on March 1st, 2016, organized by the Office of the Attorney General of Arizona, the Southwest
Border Anti-Money Laundering Alliance and the Conference of Western Attorneys General of
the United States (CWAG);
That the Office of the Attorney General of the Republic of the United Mexican States and the
Office of the Attorney General of Arizona of the United States of America wish to make joint
efforts and projects that will improve the bilateral cooperation and the exchange of practices for
optimizing the investigation and prosecution of crimes;

DEIAR RIPOBLICA
That the law enforcement authorities of the United Mexican States are in transition from a mixed
inquisitorial and written system, towards an oral and accusatory system, that will enhance the
use of scientific evidence in criminal prosecutions;
That the criminal adjudication process in the United States of America is based on an accusatory
criminal procedural system, that bases its criminal investigations on scientific evidence;
That a strong relationship between both institutions benefits the actions that promote the Rule
of Law and the mutual cooperation;
That the collaboration between the Participants shall be in accordance with its laws, international
legal instruments and domestic regulations.
As aforementioned, the Participants intend to:
I.
Share legal and technical knowledge through workshops, seminars and study tours;
II.
Exchange information on best practices in the area of criminal investigations;
III. Offer training exchange programs between the Participants, that operate the criminal
justice system;
IV. Share information and new technologies, about equipment, software and hardware
programs on law enforcement matters;
V. Exchange information, knowledge and provide training for investigating and
prosecuting trafficking and smuggling of persons, trafficking in firearms, drug
trafficking or crimes against health, money laundering or operations with resources of
illicit proceedings, and Internet crimes, especially the ones committed against
children;
VI. Work towards the establishment of a cooperation for learning investigation techniques
with regard to the criminal act or findings, procedures, protocols and strategies;
VII. Facilitate the exchange of personnel between the Participants, to promote the
continuous training;
VIII. Cooperate in all reasonable and necessary ways as permitted by the regulations of
each of the Participants.

PROCURADURIAC DEL LAI KETUBLICA GENERAL
The Attorney General of Arizona has the intention to:
I.
Promote academic activities for the benefit of the personnel of both Participants, such
as: training programs, continuous education, specialization, seminars, workshops,
meetings, workshops and other academic activities that can developed in a presential
manner or via videoconference;
II. Allow Arizona prosecutors to provide training in the United Mexican States, related to
the investigation and prosecution of criminal cases, as well as for the preparation for
the criminal trial;
III. Train public prosecutors of the Federation of the United Mexican States, in the
investigation of pornography crimes, committed against persons under eighteen years
old or those that do not have the capacity to understand the meaning of the act, or
those that do not have the capacity to resist them, and crimes committed against the
free development of personality, particularly those perpetrated through the Internet;
IV. Allow Arizona prosecutors to train, in the United Mexican States, Mexican public
prosecutors of the Federation, on preparation of criminal investigations, cross-
examinations, legal argumentation and interviews with victims and witnesses;
V. Share information, investigation tactics and techniques, on cases of trafficking and
smuggling of persons;
This Letter of Intent is signed on May 16th, 2016, in Mexico City, in two originals, in English and
Spanish, both texts being equally authentic, and will end on March 1st, 2021.
