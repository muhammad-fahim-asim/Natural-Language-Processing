Confidentiality Agreement for the 2020 meetings of the
College of Supervisors of
Reinsurance Group of America, Incorporated
The Missouri Department of Commerce and Insurance (DCI) and the Authorities
listed in Schedule A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference (herein referred
to collectively as the "Authorities") enter into this agreement for the purpose of exchanging
confidential supervisory information pertaining to the Reinsurance Group of America,
Incorporated (RGA), and its regulated/non-regulated affiliates, for any telephone conference
call(s) or meeting of the College of RGA Supervisors during 2020. This agreement also
applies to confidential supervisory information shared in preparation for, or as an immediate
result of, all 2020 telephone conference call(s) and the 2020 meeting of the College of RGA
Supervisors. Confidential supervisory information in connection with the 2020 meeting of
the College of RGA Supervisors means any supervisory information whether written or
spoken, and regardless of its format.
All confidential supervisory information furnished under this agreement remains the
property of the party that provided the information. The sharing of confidential supervisory
information in connection with the Supervisory College shall not be deemed a waiver of any
privilege or claim of confidentiality by any party to this agreement.
Authorities listed in Schedule A who are signatories to the IAIS MMOU on
Cooperation and Information Exchange will share information obtained in connection with
the 2020 meeting of the College of RGA Supervisors pursuant to the terms of the IAIS
MMOU. The Authorities who sign Schedule A (herein referred to as "Signatory Authorities")
each confirm: (1) their agreement to use any confidential supervisory information received
in connection with the Supervisory College only for lawful supervisory purposes; (2) their
authority and intent, under the relevant laws, rules and regulations of their respective
jurisdiction, to protect from disclosure and otherwise preserve the confidential and/or
privileged nature of any confidential supervisory information received in connection with the
Supervisory College; and (3) their authority to share confidential supervisory information
pertaining to Reinsurance Group of America, Incorporated with each of the Supervisors of
the Group.
The Signatory Authorities shall not disclose confidential supervisory information
obtained in connection with the 2020 meeting of the College of RGA Supervisors unless
required to do so by applicable law. Prior to disclosing any information, the Signatory
Authority will notify the Authority that provided the information of the request for disclosure.
If the Authority that provided the information does not consent to disclosure of the
information, the Signatory Authority will use all reasonable legal means to protect the
confidentiality of the information, and will consent to an application by the Authority that
provided the information to intervene in any action for the purpose of asserting a claim of
confidentiality.
