MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN THE FILM & DIGITAL
MEDIA PROGRAM OF THE STATE OF ARIZONA, THROUGH THE ARIZONA
COMMERCE AUTHORITY, AND THE FILM OFFICE OF THE STATE OF
SONORA, THROUGH SONORA TOURISM, TO JOIN EFFORTS IN MARKETING
FILM PRODUCTION IN THE ARIZONA-SONORA REGION.



Memorandum of Understanding between the Film & Digital Media Program of the
State of Arizona (the "AZFDMP") through the Arizona Commerce Authority, and the
Film Office of the State of Sonora, through Sonora Tourism, to join efforts in
marketing Film & Digital Media Production in the Arizona-Sonora region.
RECITALS
WHEREAS the states of Arizona and Sonora share an innovative, exemplary
bilateral relationship. This relationship and partnership are made possible thanks to
a history of trust and collaboration between Arizona and Sonora that has developed
solid results; and,
WHEREAS the states of Arizona and Sonora share a goal of expanding film & digital
media production and increasing related jobs and industry activity through promotion
and development; and,
WHEREAS in August 2017, the AZFDMP offered to list Sonoran beaches and
resorts on its website; and,
WHEREAS in November 2017, images of Sonoran beaches and resorts became
universally accessible on the AZFDMP website; and,
WHEREAS in May 2019, the AZFDMP added the category of Associated Film
Offices" on the AZFDMP website, which included the State of Sonora Film Office;
and,
WHEREAS the foregoing evidence a consistent and continually improving
relationship of collaboration, cooperation, and support between the AZFDMP and
the Film Office of the State of Sonora;
NOW, THEREFORE, THE FILM & DIGITAL MEDIA PROGRAM OF THE STATE
OF ARIZONA AND FILM OFFICE OF THE STATE OF SONORA RESOLVE AS
FOLLOWS:
1. The Film & Digital Media Program of the State of Arizona and the Film
Office of the State of Sonora will collaboratively promote strategies aimed at
increasing film & digital media production in the Arizona-Sonora Region.
2. The Film & Digital Media Program of the State of Arizona and the Film
Office of the State of Sonora will mutually identify current and future marketing
activities that will promote binational film & digital media production. This may
include but may not be limited to advertising opportunities, participation in
trade shows, and engagement with broader stakeholder groups through
familiarization trips.



3. The Film & Digital Media Program of the State of Arizona and the Film
Office of the State of Sonora will work together to develop a mutually
agreeable and mutually beneficial public relations campaign, which will be
presented to media outlets in Mexico, the U.S., and around the world,
highlighting opportunities and successes in cross border film & digital media
production.
4. The Arizona Commerce Authority and the Sonora Tourism will support
efforts from the film commissions in Arizona and Sonora to promote bi-
national film, TV, and digital media productions.
This Memorandum of Understanding is intended to memorialize the participants'
commitment to explore ways to promote collaboration, but it is nonbinding and
creates no present or future legal obligation among any of the participants, Nothing
in this Memorandum of Understanding should be read to conflict with the laws and
regulations of the United States of America or the United Mexican States (or any
state or local jurisdiction), or with any relevant international agreements. There are
no third-party beneficiaries to this Memorandum of Understanding.
This Memorandum of Understanding will be effective on the date of its signature and
will be valid for 5 years. Either the Arizona Commerce Authority or Sonora Tourism
may terminate its participation under this Memorandum of Understanding at any
time; provided, however, that the terminating party should endeavor to give the other
party 30 days' notice ofi its intention to terminate this Memorandum of Understanding.
The termination should not preclude the completion of any cooperative activities
agreed upon while the Memorandum of Understanding was valid. Each state will be
responsible for its own expenses in the implementation of this Memorandum of
Understanding.
This Memorandum of Understanding is signed on Wednesday, June 26, 2019, within
the framework of the Panel Discussion on Bi-National Film Marketing, held at the
Arizona-Mexico Commission 60th Anniversary Summit, held in Phoenix, Arizona.
