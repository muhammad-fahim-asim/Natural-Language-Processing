DECLARATION OF COOPERATION BETWEEN THE STATE OF SONORA OF THE UNITED MEXICAN
STATES, THROUGH THE DEPARTMENT OF THE ECONOMY, THE DEPARTMENT OF
INFRASTRUCTURE AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT, AND THE TOURISM DEVELOPMENT
COMMISSION, AND THE STATE OF ARIZONA OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA, THROUGH
THE ARIZONA COMMERCE AUTHORITY, THE DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION, AND THE
ARIZONA OFFICE OF TOURISM, TO CATALYZE AND PROMOTE THE CRUISE SHIP PORT,ALSO
KNOWN AS THE "HOME PORT" OF PUERTO PENASCO, SONORA



Declaration of Cooperation between the State of Sonora of the United Mexican States, through the
Department of the Economy, the Department of Infrastructure and Urban Development, and the Tourism
Development Commission, and the State of Arizona of the United States of America, through the Arizona
Commerce Authority, the Department of Transportation, and the Arizona Office of Tourism (collectively
"Participants"), to Catalyze and Promote the Cruise Ship Port, also known as the "Home Port" of Puerto
Penasco, Sonora.
RECITALS
WHEREAS the States of Sonora and Arizona share a strong relationship of binational cooperation in
various economic and social areas through the mechanisms of the Sonora-Arizona Commission and the
Arizona-Mexico Commission; the States of Sonora and Arizona share an innovative, exemplary bilateral
relationship, thanks to a history of trust and collaboration that has developed tangible results, strengthened
both states in accordance with their mission to improve the quality of life in the region, and led to the
development of actions and programs of mutual interest.
WHEREAS there is great interest by the States of Sonora and Arizona in promoting economic development
and tourism, as well as taking advantage of the geographic location and the existing connectivity in the
region with the objective of improving quality of life in the region;
WHEREAS currently under construction is a maritime cruise ship terminal, named Home Port of Puerto
Penasco, Sonora, located in the northwestern part of the State of Sonora, at a distance of 100 kilometers
(62 miles) from Sonoyta-Lukville Port of Entry;
WHEREAS Puerto Penasco is a tourism destination for many Arizonans;
WHEREAS the Lukeville-Sonoyta Port of Entry is the principal border crossing for Arizona visitors to Puerto
Penasco;
WHEREAS Arizona, in a unique partnership with the Puerto Penasco Visitors and Conventions Bureau,
and its federal partners, have invested $2.5 million to construct two additional inspection lanes at the
Sonoyta-Lukeville Port of Entry;
WHEREAS, in two-way traffic, more than 1.8 million people crossed at the Sonoyta-Lukeville port of entry
in 2016;
WHEREAS Puerto Penasco has an international airport, 340 kilometers (215 miles) away from Sky Harbor
Airport in Phoenix, with an influx of more than 40 million passengers per year;
WHEREAS the construction of the Home Port is over 50% complete, and this is a priority project for the
government of the State of Sonora because of the economic impact it will have on the Sonora-Arizona
region;
WHEREAS in 2015, more than 474 thousand Arizonans took cruises, while in California the total for the
same year and mode of transportation reached approximately 1.24 million;
2



WHEREAS the launch of operations of the cruise terminal is necessary because it represents an important
tourism and economic focal point for the development of both States; and
WHEREAS currently Puerto Penasco has sustainable hotel infrastructure to host the cruise travelers and it
also has capacity for expansion.
Based on the aforementioned considerations, the government of the State of Sonora and the government
of the State of Arizona pledge the following:
ACTIONS
1. The governments of the States of Arizona and Sonora will undertake good faith efforts to exchange
non-confidential information regarding the development and promotion of the "Home Port" project
at Puerto Penasco, Sonora;
2. The governments of the States of Arizona and Sonora will continue their efforts to advance projects
that modernize and facilitate the flow of tourism at the Sonoyta-Lukeville Port of Entry;
3. The governments of the States of Arizona and Sonora will serve as a source of information in order
to educate both state and federal government agencies on the importance of the Home Port
project;
4. The governments of the States of Arizona and Sonora will continue good faith efforts to encourage
the participation of the private sector, where applicable, in the development of the Home Port;
5. The governments of the States of Arizona and Sonora will promote supply chain opportunities for
the cruise ships, including goods and services coming from the State of Arizona; and
6. The State of Sonora will continue its efforts to complete the "Home Port" project.
This statement is signed on Friday, June 2 of the year two thousand and seventeen, within the framework
of the Plenary Session between the Arizona-Mexico Commission and the Sonora-Arizona Commission,
held in Scottsdale, Arizona.
This Declaration of Cooperation is intended to memorialize the Participants' commitment to explore ways to
promote collaboration between them, but it is non-binding and does not create any legal or binding
obligation between the States. Nothing in this Declaration of Cooperation should be read to conflict with
the laws and regulations off the United States of America or the United Mexican States (or any state or local
jurisdiction), or with any relevant international agreements. There are no third-party beneficiaries to this
Declaration of Cooperation.
This Declaration of Cooperation will be effective on the date of its signature and will be valid for 5 years.
The State of Arizona or the State of Sonora may terminate its participation under this Declaration of
Cooperation at any time through a written notice of termination. Nonetheless, the State of Arizona or the
State of Sonora should endeavor to give the other party 30 days written notice of its intention to terminate
this Declaration of Cooperation. The termination should not preclude the completion of any cooperative
