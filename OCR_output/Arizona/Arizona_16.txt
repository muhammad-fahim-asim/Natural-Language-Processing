CONSIDERATIONS
1.- The State of Sonora's Development Plan 2016-2021 states the following in Challenge 12: "Encourage the
improvement of environmental management foreseen in Strategy 12.1. Line of action 12.1.2; Generate, in
coordination with the municipalities, an environmental planning system based on reliable environmental
parameters, allowing the generation of joint strategies to improve air quality, water quality, solid waste
management, green area expansion, among others.
2.- The objectives of "CEDES" include formulating, implementing and evaluating the environmental policy of the
state, creating efforts for joint and coordinated implementation of actions to improve and protect the environment,
and to disseminate and promote culture and ecological values, as the actions that will be implemented by both
parties, in accordance with the bases of this collaboration agreement. It also has among its responsibilities the
freedom to agree with Federal Government agencies, Federal Entities and State Municipalities, as well as with
the social and private sectors, joint and coordinated implementation of environmental protection actions; in
accordance with Article 3, Section X of Law No. 162, which creates the Comision de Ecologia y Desarrollo
Sustentable del Estado de Sonora (Commission on Ecology and Sustainable Development of the State of
Sonora/CEDES).
3.- "ADEQ" wishes to establish a temporary air quality monitoring system in the municipality of San Luis Rio
Colorado, in the State of Sonora, an activity that it wishes to develop in conjunction with "CEDES" and "THE
MUNICIPALITY".
4.- The ozone (03), nitrogen oxides (NOy) and carbon monoxide (CO) monitoring equipment is designed to
facilitate accomplishment of the following objectives:
A. Provide information to elected officials and the general public regarding 03, NOy and CO pollution;
B. Track short-term and long-term trends;
C. Assess source contributions to 03, NOy and CO pollution;
D. Evaluate the effectiveness of air pollution control strategies;
E. Monitor both the diurnal and nocturnal concentrations of ozone.
F. Evaluate NOy and CO pollutants as 03 precursors to understand 03 formation in the San Luis and
Yuma Basin
5.- "ADEQ" maintains a network of ambient air monitors throughout the state of Arizona to characterize and
quantify air pollutants. These monitoring data are needed to provide policymakers and the public with



information, track short and long-term trends, assess source contributions, and better evaluate the effectiveness
of air pollution control strategies.

CLAUSES
1. - Objective: The parties agree that this specific collaboration agreement will aim at the installation and
operation of a temporary air quality monitoring system, which will be operated by "ADEQ", being the main
objective of the program to monitor 03, NOy and CO concentrations in the Municipality of San Luis Rio Colorado,
Sonora, and once the results are obtained, they shall be reported on a daily basis to the United States
Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), and can be downloaded by "CEDES" and "THE MUNICIPALITY", as
integral parts of this Agreement, and to the general public, if required. The physical characteristics of the
equipment are described in detail in ANNEX I, which is an integral part of this Collaboration Agreement.
2. - Use of Site and Site Maintenance: In order to carry out this project, "THE MUNICIPALITY" will provide a
plot of land belonging to Operador Municipal de Agua Potable y Alcantarillado y Saneamiento (OOMAPAS) of
San Luis Rio Colorado, Sonora, that has been made available on loan at no cost, to "THE MUNICIPALITY",
located in Lot 12 at Calle 15 and Ave. Carranza, San Luis Rio Colorado, Sonora. "THE MUNICIPALITY" will be
fully responsible for all expenses generated by taxes and all other expenses for said plot. Likewise, "THE
MUNICIPALITY" will be in charge of incurring all expenses for electricity and water, or for any maintenance
needs of the lot not pertaining to equipment owned by "ADEQ"
3.- Sampling Schedule: Maintenance and service of the 03, NOy and CO equipment shall be conducted every
two (2) weeks by "ADEQ" personnel, using technical protocols specified by the instrument manufacturers, the
State of Arizona and the EPA; the resulting data shall be integrated into hourly averages. These maintenance



trips will be conducted during the day, during normal working hours. However, extra trips may be taken in case
of equipment malfunctions.
4. - Notification of On-Site Visits: For regularly scheduled visits, "ADEQ" shall notify "CEDES" of dates with
at least one week advance notice, so that "CEDES" technicians can participate in these visits. Likewise, for
those visits that are conducted to address equipment malfunctions, "ADEQ" shall notify "CEDES" with at least
24 hours advance notice.
5. - Project Review and Site Visits: "ADEQ" shall be responsible for the execution, implementation and
operation of this project, involving the temporary monitoring of 03, NOy and CO. Likewise, it will also be
responsible for observing due progress of the monitoring station at all times. To that end, it will have free right to
access the premises where the equipment is located, as many times as necessary, without the need to give
advance notice either to "THE MUNICIPALITY" or to "CEDES", except in the case of scheduled visits for some
of the reasons already described in previous clauses.
6. - Reports and Access to Information: "ADEQ" shall submit hourly preliminary data to EPA's AirNow-Tech
website (www.airnowtech.org) for current 03, NOy and CO conditions. Subject to statutory confidentiality
requirements of "ADEQ", the parties to this Agreement shall have full, complete and equal access to data
available through AirNow-Tech and information generated as a result of this Agreement, on a no-charge basis.
Title to all documents, reports, data and other materials prepared as a result of this Agreement shall rest with
"ADEQ". All parties shall have full and complete rights to reproduce, duplicate, disclose, perform and otherwise
use all information prepared as a result of this Agreement, with provision that all reproduction, duplication,
disclosures and literature shall contain acknowledgment to "ADEQ".
Information: all information and data provided that gives rises to and is related to this agreement, is considered public information and
may be made available to the public with previous authorization of the parties, except for information that is considered of restricted
access, as defined in Chapter Seven of the Law of Transparency and Access to Public Information ofthe State of Sonora, which,
at no time, the parties mayl have such information and documentation, nor make it known to any third parties.

Informacion de la Calidad del Aire (Air Quality Information System/'SINAICA").
B- Provide maintenance to the monitoring station in case "ADEQ" cannot perform it and requests it.
C- Act as a link between "THE MUNCIPALITY" and "ADEQ", to settle disputes and work together.

10. - Rights and obligations: None of the parties may assign nor transfer all or part of the rights and obligations
of this agreement, nor those derived from it.
11.- TERM: The validity of this agreement will take effect upon the signing of this collaboration agreement and
until July 31, 2021. This agreement may be terminated by any of the parties, at any time, with or without cause,
after giving thirty (30) days written notice to its counterparts; in this case, the parties will take the necessary
measures to ensure that the actions that have been initiated are carried out until their complete conclusion.
12. - Modifications to the Agreement: The parties agree that in case of any modification to this agreement, it
will be made by means of a written document signed by all parties, which will be integrated to this agreement
and it will only modify the point in question.
13.- Exclusive obligations and responsibilities: The parties are committed to promote and make sure that
they carry out their functions with the necessary discretion and confidentiality, regarding the information
generated in the development of the activities that they agree to specifically, to ensure the rights of the population



benefiting from it, and the personnel assigned to it, as well as to avoid the filtration and handling of information
for any other purpose.
Likewise, this obligation extends to the persons that each of the parties authorizes and contracts with, in order
to carry out the activities related to the fulfillment of this Agreement.
14. - Controversies: this agreement is in good faith and any disputes that arise will be resolved by common
agreement by the signatory parties, through their corresponding instances.
15.- "THE PARTIES" agree that, by virtue of the nature of the present agreement, there is no labor relationship
between the parties and they will be mutually released from any liability that may arise in the area of work and
social security.
Each party will be responsible for its own staff that is involved in the activities foreseen in this Agreement of
Collaboration, and staff will be under the absolute direction and dependence of the Institution to which they
report.
