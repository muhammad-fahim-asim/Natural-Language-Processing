Article 1 Purpose and Areas for Collaboration
The Mie Prefecture Department of Employment and Economic Affairs (DEEA) and the
Washington State Department of Commerce (Commerce) agree to promote joint initiatives for the
advancement of technology and the expansion of trade between the Parties.
The aerospace, life sciences industries are designated as high priority sectors for cooperation.

Article 2 Terms of this MOU
(1) This MOU shall come into force once signed by the Parties and it shall remain in effect for
an initial period of three years from that date.
(2) Provided that none of the Parties give written notice of withdrawal three months prior to
the expiry of this MOU as outlined in (3), the effective period of this MOU shall be
automatically extended by an additional 12 month periods each subsequent year.
(3) The Parties are entitled to withdraw from this MOU by providing written notice three
months prior to the date of withdrawal.

Article 3 Confidentiality
The Parties shall not disclose or divulge to any third party information obtained through
collaboration based on this MOU that has been designated as confidential by either party for the
entirety of this MOU's effective duration.

Article 4 Miscellaneous
This MOU is a statement of intent to foster genuine and mutually beneficial co-operation. No
portion of this MOU shall be construed as creating any de facto company or any other kind of
legally binding relationship between the parties, with the exception of the confidentiality agreement
outlined under Article 3.
