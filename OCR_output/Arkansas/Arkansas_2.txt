Dear Sir:
As of December 1, 1997 the State of Arkansas, through the
Department of Finance and Administration, Office of Driver
Services, will no longer require German citizens residing here in
Arkansas to take a practical or written drivers' test. A valid
German license will be recognized and exchanged for a Arkansas
drivers license for the normal conversion fee that anyone else in
the United States would be paying upon receiving a license here.
In return, we understand that United States citizens from Arkansas
residing in Germany will be similarly exempt from the German
practical and written drivers' license tests.
If any further action is necessary in order to establish this
agreement of reciprocity between our licensing office and yours,
please let me know so that we can take any further steps that are
required.
