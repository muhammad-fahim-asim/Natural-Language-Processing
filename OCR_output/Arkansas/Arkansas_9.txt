1. Group framework
The Bermuda Monetary Authority has determined that it is the Group Supervisor of the Group in
accordance with the provisions of the Insurance Act 1978 of Bermuda (the "Act"). In this connection,
the Authority has determined that a Multilateral Memorandum of Understanding ("MMOU") is
required to be made among all relevant supervisory authorities to clarify the respective roles and
requirements of such authorities, in order to achieve effective regulation and supervision in relation
to the operations of the Group; and to make provision for confidentiality rules regarding the sharing
and exchange of Group information and the information of its members.
The Authority is a signatory to the International Association of Insurance Supervisors ("IAIS") MMOU
and accordingly this MMOU incorporates certain IAIS principles and standards in particular, the IAIS
principles on group wide supervision.
2. Definitions
For the purposes of this MMOU, unless the context states otherwise:
The "BMA" or the "Authority" means the Bermuda Monetary Authority.
The "Group" is described in paragraph 4.1 of this MMOU and is comprised of the ultimate parent and
all its subsidiaries, including regulated and unregulated entities.
"Group Supervisor" means the "BMA" or the "Authority".
"Competent Authority" means a regulatory authority that is empowered by law to supervise
Insurers.
"Insurer" means every insurance company that is a member of the Group and includes all insurance
branches of the Group.
"Supervisors" mean the Competent Authorities of the Group listed in Appendix 2 of this MMOU.
"Supervisory College" is the forum referred to in this MMOU, which shall be used to effectively
facilitate cooperation and information sharing amongst the Group Supervisor and the Supervisors in
relation to all supervisory and regulatory matters pertaining to the Group.
"Emergency Situation" means any situation or event that could materially affect or impair the
financial or operational condition of an Insurer or the Group; or substantially affect the public
interest of the jurisdiction of a Supervisor and accordingly, must be addressed in an expedited or
urgent manner.
3. Objectives and principles of cooperation
1. The following principles shall apply to this MMOU:
(a) The MMOU may be amended, if necessary, to reflect significant changes to the Group. It
may also be amended, if needed, to remain compatible with the IAIS principles and
standards or insurance standards promulgated by other industry bodies. Amendments

to this MMOU shall be made in writing and agreed to by all Supervisors who are
signatories to this MMOU.
(b) The provisions of this MMOU do not create legally binding obligations; and do not modify
or supersede any jurisdictional law; or create any direct or indirect enforceable rights
which may be enforced by any signatory to it. This MMOU does not affect the provisions
under other multilateral or bilateral agreements.
(c) The proposals of this MMOU are without prejudice to stricter provisions applicable to the
cooperation between all Supervisors.
(d) The Authority shall facilitate and lead cooperation amongst the Supervisors in relation to
matters of the Group as Group Supervisor. This MMOU may also provide a framework
for bilateral cooperation, when necessary, and for relevant matters.
2. The objectives of this MMOU are to:
(a) Facilitate group-wide supervision, entailing in particular but not limited to, an
assessment of the Group's: (1) financial situation, (2) compliance with the rules on
solvency, (3) risk concentration and intra-group transactions, and (4) system of
governance. The Supervisors may facilitate the gathering and analysis of available
information on risk exposure, solvency and governance of entities in the Group. The
Supervisors intend to assist the BMA in its functions as Group Supervisor; and may
participate in the prudential assessment of systemic risks applicable to the Group when
required by the BMA.
(b) Enhance solo supervision of insurance entities by the Supervisors. The solo supervision
of Insurers is the sole responsibility of the relevant Supervisor, and nothing in this MMOU
derogates from their rights to take all measures deemed necessary in relation to the
supervision of Insurers they respectively license, authorise and regulate. The gathering
and sharing of information, assessments and discussions by the Supervisors is intended
to facilitate the exchange of useful information among them, and to prevent the
duplication of certain supervisory and regulatory obligations and is intended to allow for
more efficient and effective supervision of Insurers in the Group by each Supervisor.
(c) Provide a permanent forum for cooperation, to (1) build relationships between the
Supervisors, (2) coordinate supervisory activities and (3) engender greater cohesiveness
in cooperation between Supervisors. This MMOU is an on-going mechanism that forms
an integral part of the Group supervisory process, contributing to the ongoing protection
of pollcyholders' interests and fostering beller communicalion relevant lo supervision by
the Supervisors in a financial crisis (until such time the Supervisors determine otherwise).
(d) Foster a better understanding of supervisory practices and more efficient and effective
supervision. There may be significant differences in the supervisory requirements of the
Supervisors, due to the specific features of each domestic insurance market, which are
better understood by the relevant Supervisor. As Supervisors work together through the
Supervisory College, the intent is to attain a greater understanding of the Group's
structure and the nature of its solo and group risks.

Description of the Group and identity of the Supervisors
4.1 Description and structure of the Group
The parent company of the Group is Allied World Assurance Company Holdings, AG which
has its registered office in Lindenstrasse 8 BAAR, ZUG 6340, Switzerland. The Group, through
its wholly-owned subsidiaries, provides property and casualty insurance and reinsurance on a
worldwide basis. A summary of the Allied World Group Structure is attached as Appendix 1.
4.2 Identity of the Supervisors
1. This MMOU will take effect between thet following Supervisors listed in Appendix 2.
2. The Supervisors will designate individuals ("Contacts") who will represent them at meetings
of the Supervisory College and will assist in performing the duties and carrying out the
functions of the relevant Supervisor that are described in this MMOU. A list of Contacts is
attached as Appendix 3. It will be updated by the Authority on a regular basis.
5. Confidentiality
1. The Supervisors, particularly those who are not signatories to the IAIS MMOU, confirm that
they have made available to the BMA as Group Supervisor a copy of their relevant
jurisdictional laws or regulations which impose a requirement to keep all regulatory
information disclosed to them confidential (the "Legislation"). The Supervisors confirm that
they have been provided with copies of the Legislation by the BMA, if requested by them,
and that they have had the opportunity to review, if required, the Legislation for equivalence
with their own legal confidentiality obligations.
2. A Supervisor will notify the BMA of any changes to its Legislation relating to confidentiality of
information; which applies or may apply to any information which has been disclosed to it in
the Supervisory College; and which has or may potentially affect, the previous positive
assessment by the other Supervisors of such Legislation. Any copies of the amended
Legislation shall be provided to the BMA who will disseminate such Legislation and other
information for review to the Supervisors as soon as reasonably practicable. Should a
Supervisor consider that such amendment(s) alter any approvals previously provided by it, it
will notify the BMA who will inform the other Supervisors. The BMA will endeavour to find
an appropriate solution and will consult with the Supervisors in this regard.
3. Further to the confidentiality obligations arising out of the Supervisor's Legislation or from
any other legal obligations, the Supervisors confirm that Confidential Information (as defined
in 5.4) shared between them will be used only for the lawful supervision of the Group.
4. The Supervisors will endeavour to maintain the confidentiality of all information
("Confidential Information") received to the extent permitted by their respective Legislation.
Should a Supervisor receive a request or demand ("Receiving Supervisor") for Confidential
Information from any person or entity that is not a member of the Supervisory College (i.e., a

third party); the Receiving Supervisor will as soon as practicable, inform the Supervisor that
supplied the information ("Disclosing Supervisor") and request its consent to the disclosure.
The Disclosing Supervisor may impose conditions on its consent and these conditions will be
imposed by the Receiving Supervisor releasing the information to the third party. If written
consent is not received or denied, the Receiving Supervisor will use all lawful means available
to it to resist disclosure of the Confidential Information. If the Receiving Supervisor is not
successful in ensuring that the Confidential Information is kept confidential in the hands of
the third party, it will afford the Disclosing Supervisor any available opportunity to take
whatever action it deems appropriate to preserve, protect and maintain the confidential
nature of its information. Where the Receiving Supervisor is under a legal compulsion to
disclose the information to the third party, it will inform both the Disclosing Supervisor and
the BMA of the proposed disclosure. The BMA, in conjunction with the Disclosing Supervisor
will determine next: steps, provided, however, that nothing herein will prevent the Receiving
Supervisor in possession of Confidential Information from complying with an order
compelling disclosure: as required byi its Legislation.
5. All Confidential Information shared hereunder by a Disclosing Supervisor belongs to and
remains the property of such Disclosing Supervisor. The Receiving Supervisor will take all
actions reasonably necessary to preserve, protect and maintain the confidentiality of such
information and any privileges associated therewith.
6. The Supervisors will endeavour to ensure that all persons dealing with, or having access to,
such Confidential Information, have a need for such information consistent with the
purposes of this MMoU, and are bound by the obligation of confidentiality.
6. Responsibilities of the Supervisors
1. The BMA is responsible for the coordination and overalt supervision of the Group in
accordance with Bermuda law. The BMA will coordinate the gathering of relevant or
essential information, especially those for going concern and Emergency Situations, including
the dissemination of information which is of importance for the solo supervisory obligations
of the Supervisors, and send the information to relevant Supervisors subject to the terms of
this MMOU.
2. All Supervisors agree to the terms and conditions of the MMOU and, where they are not
signatories to the IAIS MMoll, agree to provide their Legislation to the BMA in order to
comply with paragraph 5.
3. The Supervisors confirm that they are authorized to share Confidential Information with each
other in accordance with, and to the extent allowed under their respective jurisdictional laws
and agree to share information with other Supervisors, in accordance with the terms of this
MMOU, in the Supervisory College if required, from the date of commencement of this
MMOU. The Supervisors confirm that they are authorised in accordance with their
Legislation to protect from disclosure and otherwise maintain the confidential or privileged
nature of any Confidential Information shared on this MMOU.

4. The Supervisors confirm that they are able and willing to administer their obligations arising
under this MMOU accordingly.
5. Supervisors will endeavour to contribute to the gathering of information by disclosing, to the
extent permitted by the Legislation, any information that they consider may be relevant to
the other Supervisors so as to facilitate supervision of the Group.
6. The BMA, as the Group Supervisor, shall schedule meetings, coordinate the activities of the
Supervisory College and propose changes where required regarding the function of the
Supervisory College. The BMA shall keep the Supervisors informed of proposed Supervisory
College meetings to be convened and of all matters relevant to the Group; and shall advise
all participating Supervisors of the main issues to be discussed prior to each meeting. The
BMA shall also inform the Supervisors of actions planned during meetings and outcomes of
action taken in respect thereof.
7. The activities and findings of the Supervisory College shall not impact in any way the
individual regulatory responsibilities of each Supervisor required in accordance with its
jurisdictional laws.
7.
Cooperation in going concern situations
7.1 The Supervisory College
1. The organisation of the Supervisory College reflects the activities and the legal structure of
the Group and the risks to which iti is or might be exposed.
2. Other forums in which Supervisors may participate, (for instance regional colleges if
appropriate), may be utilised by the BMA and the Supervisors to cooperate and disclose
information relating to the Group. Such forums may be proposed by the BMA or the
Supervisors on a permanent basis or in light of specific circumstances. A list of such forums
will be maintained by the BMA and all Supervisors shall be consulted on or informed of any
changes as necessary. All forums created are expected to report on their duties and functions
to thel BMA and to a group meeting of the Supervisory College when necessary.
3. The BMA may extend an invitation to other Competent Authorities and relevant banking or
securities regulators ("Third Party Regulators") to participate in aspects of any meeting of a
Supervisory College; with the proviso that the Third Party Regulators have supervisory
functions regarding the Group and have confirmed to the BMA and the Supervisors that the
Legislation to which they are subject are equivalent to the legal confidentiality obligations of
the Supervisors prior to the meeting, and the Supervisors have had the opportunity to review
the same and to conclude arrangements on confidentiality and professional secrecy with the
relevant Third Party Regulators, if necessary, and therefore agreed in advance to the Third
Party Regulators' attendance and participation.

7.2 Exchange of information among Supervisors
1. The BMA shall centralise and disseminate information to the Supervisors. However, a
Supervisor may exchange information bilaterally with another Supervisor in relation to
activities of the Group subject to the terms of this MMOU if it deems more appropriate or
necessary. In such case, both Supervisors will endeavour, to the extent possible, to keep the
BMA informed of the exchange of information in writing.
2. The Supervisors may, to the extent permitted byl law:
(a) on request, share with each other relevant information concerning an Insurer in the
Group which may allow or facilitate Group supervision;
(b) on their own initiative, share any information which they are of the view is essential
for the other Supervisors to have in order to carry out their jurisdictional duties and
functions.
3. Where a Supervisor receives information ("Receiving Supervisor") from another in response
to a specific request, and where the BMA receives information provided without a specific
request, the Receiving Supervisor and the BMA may disclose that information to other
Supervisors subject to the terms of this MMOU and subject to any conditions placed on
disclosure by the Disclosing Supervisor. The Receiving Supervisor must not share
Confidential Information with any Third Party Regulator which is not a member of the
Supervisory College unless it has first obtained the consent of the Disclosing Supervisor.
4. Requests for information should, to the extent possible, be responded to within a reasonable
time but no later than 20 business days from the date of the request.
5. Information that Supervisors may exchange between themselves include (but is not limited
to): (1) any granting or withdrawal of authorisations; (2) changes in the membership of the
board of directors; (3) solvency concerns or problems concerning a member in the Group
(including financial problems which could lead to the drafting of financing schemes or the
winding-up of an Insurer of the Group, the declaration of emergency settlements, the
freezing of assets); (4) the granting of approvals or licenses to an Insurer in order to allow a
major acquisition leading to a qualified participation in another Insurer or financial entity; (5)
any major acquisition by an Insurer in the Group; and (6) any enforcement action taken by a
regulator against an Insurer in the Group.
6. Confidential Information will be shared via secure communication channels available.
7.3 Task sharing, delegation, joint activities, specialised group, joint on-site inspections
1. The BMA may share or delegate tasks to the Supervisors, who with the knowledge of the
BMA and subject to their mutual consent, may share and delegate tasks to one another in
compliance with their respective jurisdictional laws and regulatory requirements.

2. Joint activities between Supervisors may be organised on a voluntary basis, in order for the
BMA or a Supervisor to comply with applicable jurisdictional laws and regulatory
requirements.
3. The BMA and the Supervisors may establish working groups or execute joint on-site
inspections, as permitted by jurisdictional laws and regulatory requirements, comprising
various competent authorities, working on a voluntary basis to review specific matters of
interest relating to an Insurer or to the Group.
8. Cooperation in emergency: situations
1. The Supervisors are responsible for assessing whether a crisis situation is affecting the
Insurers under their supervision.
2. The Crisis Management Plan, attached as Appendix 4 and forms part of this MMOU, provides
a definition of a crisis situation, as well as the required cooperation in the case of a crisis.
The Supervisors intend to follow the principles and procedures stipulated in the Crisis
Management Plan.
3. The Supervisors will endeavour to cooperate closely, whenever necessary and in accordance
with their jurisdictional law, with other relevant authorities (e.g. central banks) involved in
the crisis management process.
9.
Final provisions
1. The Supervisors will make their best efforts to guarantee the security of the information
transmitted by implementing secured and quick means of transmission (e.g. through a
secured sharing platform). All Confidential Information shared under this MMOU will be
stored in secure communication channels.
2. A Supervisor may terminate its participation in this MMOU at any time by giving at least 30
days prior notice in writing to the BMA. The BMA will inform all other Supervisors without
delay. The retiring Supervisor may continue to cooperate and exchange information
according to this MMOU during the 30 days' notice period until its actual retirement date.
Retirement by a Supervisor will not in any way affect:
(a) the rights or obligations of any Supervisor with respect to Confidential Information
previously received or provided under this MMOU;
(b) any privileges associated with such information.
3. The Supervisors shall revise and update this MMOU when necessary to appropriately reflect
major changes in the overall structure of the Group (or any Insurer) and the risks undertaken
by it or any Insurer; changes to the laws governing any Supervisor (including organization
changes of supervisory authorities or creation of new authorities) or changes to their
cooperation procedures.
4. Any amendment to this MMOU requires the consent of all Supervisors in writing.



For Official Use Only
5. Before a new Competent Authority (an "Applicant Competent Authority") joins the
Supervisory College, the agreement in writing of all Supervisors is required.
6. If an Applicant Competent Authority is a signatory to the IAIS MMOU, it will not be required
to submit to a further review of the confidentiality obligations imposed on it under its
Legislation by the BMA.
7. The BMA confirms that where it has entered into a bilateral MoU with a Competent
Authority (or an Applicant Competent Authority) it will not require a further review of that
Competent Authority's legal confidentiality framework in order to be satisfied (in accordance
with Bermuda law) that such Competent Authority is subject to and adequately administers,
an equivalent confidentiality framework.
8. If an Applicant Competent Authority is not a signatory to the IAIS MMOU, it will be required
to submit to a review of the legal confidentiality framework imposed on it under its
Legislation by the BMA and the Supervisors (if such Supervisors require a review).
9. If all Supervisors agree to the application of the Applicant Competent Authority, the
signatory page of the MMOU and Appendix 2 will be amended by adding the signature of the
new Competent Authority.
10. This MMOU will come into force between the Supervisors from the date each Supervisor
signs ("date of commencement").
