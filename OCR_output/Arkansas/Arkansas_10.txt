RECIPROCITY ARRANGEMENT
This informal Arrangement is made between the State of Arkansas, represented by the Lieutenant
Governor, and thel French Authorities, represented by the Consul General of France in Houston, to facilitate
the ease with which citizens from Arkansas living in France and French citizens residing in Arkansas can
obtain drivers licenses.
It is agreed in this Arrangement that an Arkansas citizen residing in France and having an Arkansas driver's
license will be able to exchange that license for al French driver's license at the nominal conversion fee
without being required to take the knowledge and skills tests and without further driver's education.
Correspondingly, French citizens residing in Arkansas having a valid French driver's s license will be able to
convert that license to an Arkansas driver's license without being required to take the knowledge and skills
tests and without further driver's education at the nominal conversion fee.
Arkansas and France agree to provide samples of driver's licenses currently in circulation as of the date of
this Arrangement and any versions, which may be introduced at a later date.
This Arrangement applies only to drivers age 18. and older and does not apply to drivers making application
to operate a commercial motor vehicle or motorcycle.
This Arrangement is effective upon the: signature of both parties and can be revoked by either party at any
time, for any reason with three months from notice to implement this decision.
