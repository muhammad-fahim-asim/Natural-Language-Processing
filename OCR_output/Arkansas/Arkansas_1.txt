AGREEMENT ON COOPERATION
Between
THE STATE OF ARKANSAS, THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
And
DONG NAI PROVINCE PEOPLE'S COMMITTEE, THE SOCIALIST
REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM
For the 2015-2020 period
Dong Nai Province People's Committee, the Socialist Republic of Vietnam
represented by Mr. Dinh Quoc Thai, Chairman and the State of Arkansas, the United
States of America, represented by Mr. Asa Hutchinson - Governor (hereinafter refered
to as "Parties");
In accordance with the framework of the July 25th, 2013 Joint Statement by the
United States and Vietnam on the Comprehensive Partnership which intended to
contribute to the peace, stability, co-operation and prosperity for each country; based on
the principles of equality and mutual benefit corresponding with the Law of each
country, and International treaty and agreements that both countries are obligated to;
Whereas the two Parties with their needs and capability desire to cooperate in order
to promote economic development in the fields of trade, high-tech agriculture, services,
healthcare, education, science and technology, industrial production; as well as the
introduction of tourism, culture exchange, and people-to-people relations between Dong
Nai Province, Vietnam and the State of Arkansas, the United States in the 2015 - 2020
period and the years after;
The two Parties hereby agree to sign the "Agreement on Cooperation" as follows:
Article 1. PROMOTION OF COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT
Trade and services: The two governments shall encourage and support
enterprises in activites to promote export-import, trade promotion, business
environment research, and increase awareness (through trade shows, exhibitions,
conferences, and marketing programs); support each other's enterprises in seeking
opportunities and implementing investment activities (open branches and representative
offices); introduce Dong Nai enterprises to local companies and trade organizations in
Arkansas; support Arkansas enterprises making investment in the fields of warehousing
and logisitics to make the most the potential in Dong Nai Province, in accordance with
the Law of each country.
Cooperation in high-tech agriculture, industrial production, healthcare,
education, science and technology: The two parties shall discuss to develop mutually
beneficial initiatives for each field in accordance with comparative advantages,
strengths and the needs for development of each party.
- For facilitating this framework agreement, the two Parties shall negotiate and
implement feasible procedures, including:
+ Effectively promoting enterprises and investors from both sides; establishing
1



the supporting mechanism for mutual cooperation; facilitating exchange of
information;
+ Increasing effectiveness in administrative procedures.
+ Supporting interaction and exchange of people to increase mutual
understanding.
Article 2. ENHANCING PROMOTION AND DEVELOPMENT OF
TOURISM,
CULTURAL
EXCHANGE
AND
PEOPLE-TO-PEOPLE
RELATIONS
The two Parties shall combine the activities of tourism development and promotion,
cultural exchange, and people-to-people relations together with the economic
development for further exchange and mutual understanding between peoples in the two
regions.
Article 3. COOPERATION METHOD
1. Both Parties shall appoint the Lead Representatives and Contact Agencies for
carrying out the contents agreed in this document.
The State of Arkansas appoints the Executive Director of the Arkansas Economic
Development Commission to directly supervise the contents of this agreement with
Dong Nai, and assigns the Arkansas Economic Development Commission to be the
Contact Agency.
Dong Nai Province People's Committee appoints 01 Vice Chairman of People's
Committee in charge of Foreign Economic Affairs to directly supervise the contents of
this agreement with Arkansas, and assigns Dong Nai Department of External Relations
to be the Contact Agency in cooperation with Department of Industry and Trade,
Department of Planning and Investment to carry out the agreement.
2. The two Parties agree to form the Dong Nai - Arkansas Working Unit which
helps formulate and implement an Action Plan based on the principles of the Agreement
on Cooperation of the 2015 - 2020 period and the years after. This Working Unit shall
consistently perform the cooperation strategy in this agreement for a period of 5 years
from the date of signature. The two Parties shall regularly review the execution of the
Action Plan based on this agreement. After 5 years, based on the achievement of
cooperation, the two Parties shall assess the possibility for further cooperation aiming at
economic development stimulation between the State of Arkansas and Dong Nai
Province.
3. The two Parties shall visit each other when needed to discuss issues of mutual
concern and share experiences about specific program(s) in every field of cooperation to
ensure the mutual development direction. Each party shall actively arrange necessary
personnel, time, and funds to implement responsibily during each year of the 5-year
program.
Article 4. DISPUTE SETTLEMENT
In the implementation of this agreement, if any dispute arises due to a difference of
opinion, the disupute shall be settled amicably by the two Parties.
This Agreement is established for facilitating economic development cooperation
