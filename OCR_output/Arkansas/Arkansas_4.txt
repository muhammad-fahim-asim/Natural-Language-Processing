MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN
THE ARKANSAS DEPARTMENT OF FINANCE & ADMINISTRATION
AND
THE NATIONAL POLICE AGENCY
OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOREA
REGARDING THE MUTUAL RECOGNITION OF
DRIVER'S LICENSES
The Arkansas Department of Finance & Administration (hereinafter the "DFA") and the National
Police Agency ofthe Republic of Korea (hereinafter the "NPA")(hereinafter jointly referred to as the
"Sides"),
Acknowledging that their respective governments have established comparable knowledge and
skills requirements which must be demonstrated by a driver's license applicant under
driving
standards for testing knowledge and driving skills prior to a driver's license being issued; comparable
Desiring to streamline the exchange of valid non-commercial driver's licenses for an applicant from
Arkansas living in the Republic of Korea and an applicant from the Republic of Korea living in Arkansas
and;
Intending to set forth the principles and procedures for achieving the aforementioned objective without
creating legal obligation,
Have reached the following understanding:
Paragraph 1
a.
The DFA will allow a person aged 18 or over, lawfully present in the United States and
residing in Arkansas who possesses a valid, unexpired Korean driver's license (Class 1
Large Driver's License, Class 1 Ordinary Driver's License, Class 1 Special Driver's
License or Class 2 Ordinary Driver's License), to exchange that license for an Arkansas
noncommercial (Class D) Operator's License without a vehicle group designation or
endorsement." The applicant will be required to pay all requisite fees and pass an eyesight
test, but will not be required to submit to a driving skills test or knowledge test.
b.
The NPA will allow a person aged 18 or over, residing in the Republic of Korea who
possesses a valid, unexpired Arkansas Operator's License, to exchange that license for a
Class 2 Ordinary Driver's License after paying all requisite fees and passing a physical
(aptitude) test, without being required to submit to a driving skills test or knowledge test.
C.
The Sides may require a driving skills test and/or medical documentation attesting to the
physical or mental capabilities of applicants prior to granting a license in situations where
the Side carrying out the exchange has reason to believe that the individual is not
qualified to safely operate a motor vehicle. All other legal or policy requirements in
Arkansas and/or the Republic of Korea will apply.



d.
Each Side will require all applicants for an exchange of driver's license to
ofi identity and lawful presence as required to comply with the laws and regulations present proof ofits
country and/or state, and will apply the same application standards, rules, and
in licensing decisions for exchange as it would for any resident ofthe country regulations and/or
state, including the suspension, revocation or restriction of a license already issued, as
well as the reexamination of a driver previously granted a license.
e.
The DFA may require an applicant to present an International Driving Permit or a
notarized translation issued by a Korean Consulate General to assist in the translation of
the Korean driver's license. In addition, the DFA may require an applicant to provide a
translation into the English language of any documents presented as identification or
proof of lawful presence.
f.
The NPA may require an applicant to present an apostilled certificate, which confirms the
validity or authenticity ofthe driver's license issued by the government ofthe State of
Arkansas.
Paragraph 2
a.
The Sides will provide to each other samples oftheir relevant driver's licenses in
circulation at the date ofthe coming into effect ofthis Memorandum of Understanding
(hereinafter the "MOU") and any versions that may be introduced in the future.
b.
In case of doubts related to the validity or authenticity of a driver's license submitted for
exchange, with the consent of the holder of the driver's license for release ofthis
information, the Sides may verify with each other that the applicant has a valid driver's
license issued by the respective agency. Ifconsent is withheld, an exchange may not
occur.
C.
The Side requested to verify the validity or authenticity of an applicant' 's driver's license
will promptly respond to the request for verification.
Paragraph 3
a.
This MOU is subject to the relevant laws, rules, and regulations in force in the State of
Arkansas and in the Republic of Korea.
b.
Both Sides will cooperate in implementing this MOU.
C.
Both Sides will respect all State and Federal privacy laws with regard to any information
obtained when exchanging a driver's license, including the Driver's Privacy Protection
Act, 18 U.S.C. S 2721, et seq., and the Korean Personal Information Protection Law.
d.
Communication between the Sides under this MOU will be in English and in writing, and

Paragraph 4
a.
This MOU supersedes any and all prior arrangements, if any, between the Sides.
b.

resolved through discussions between the Sides
C.
Any modifications to this MOU will be in writing and approved by both Sides.
d.
This MOU becomes effective when signed by the last signatory hereto and may be
terminated by either Side at will at any time with a thirty (30) day written notice, except
in the case of the misuse of data or a security breach, in which case each Side may
immediately terminate this MOU.
