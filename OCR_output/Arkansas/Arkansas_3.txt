Memorandum of Understanding
Between Dông Nai Province of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam
And the State of Arkansas of the United States of America
In order to further friendly diplomatic relations, enhance cultural and historic understanding and
cooperation, and to promote economic development and international trade between the State of
Arkansas of the United States of America and the Dông Nai Province of the Socialist Republic of
Vietnam, and in accordance with the principles of the 1995 Joint Statement on the Establishment
of Diplomatic Relations between the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the United States of
America, the parties have resolved to enter into this Memorandum of Understanding hereby
establishing a Sister State relationship between the parties in the fields of trade and economic
cooperation, agriculture, manufacturing, education, culture, science, health and technology.
Recognizing the mutual interests of the State of Arkansas and the Dông Nai Province, and the
many opportunities for cooperation in these fields and others, it is the parties' objective to build a
long-term relationship, promote a strong economic exchange, and to develop a joint program by
which regular contact is maintained between the leaders of the governments of both parties,
including their relevant departments, ministries and agencies, to facilitate meaningful dialogue to
accomplish the objectives set forth herein, including the increase in commercial trade between the
parties, for the mutual benefit and prosperity of the parties and their peoples.
This Memorandum ofUnderstanding shall become effective immediately upon its signing by both
parties and is written in duplicate in the languages of English and Vietnamese, both texts being
equally authentic.
Agreed upon in Little Rock, Arkansas, the United States of America, the day of April, 2015.
