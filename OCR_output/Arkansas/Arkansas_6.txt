MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) FOR ESTABLISHING AN
AGRICULTURAL DEVELOPMENT PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN
THE ARKANSAS AGRICULTURE DEPARTMENT, USA
AND
GHANA MINISTRY OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE, THE REPUBLIC OF GHANA
The Arkansas Agriculture Department, State of Arkansas of the United States of
America and the Ghana Ministry of Food and Agriculture of the Republic of Ghana
have agreed, on the basis of friendly cooperation, equality and mutual benefit, to
continue to develop relationships to promote and broaden agricultural
development, cooperation, and cultural exchanges between the two sides.
The two sides will identify themes, common to both, that can generate new
initiatives to foster and encourage science and technological innovation,
agriculture, trade, commerce, cultural, and environmental activities
between the parties. The long-term goal is to strengthen the bond for
agricultural development and to enhance partnership between the
government of Arkansas and Ghana.
Regular contacts shall be maintained between the leaders of the two sides
to facilitate consultations on the exchanges and cooperation as well as
matters of common concern.
This Agreement shall be officially established and shall become effective
from the date of signature. This Agreement shall be in full force and effective
for a period of 5 years. However, the Republic of Ghana and the State of
Arkansas may terminate this Agreement at any time during the Agreement
period by providing 30 days' written notice.



On this date June 13 in the year of 2018, we do hereby set our signatures to affirm
and witness to make this MOU the official document by which all aforementioned
activities and subsequent activities shall be implemented.
