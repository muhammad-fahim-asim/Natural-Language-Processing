COOPERATION AGREEMENT
BETWEEN
THE NEW YORK STATE ENERGY RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY
AND
THE DANISH MINISTRY OF ENERGY, UTILITIES AND CLIMATE
ON THE DEVELOPMENT AND PROCUREMENT OF OFFSHORE WIND ENERGY



The New York State Energy Research and Development Authority ("NYSERDA") and the Danish Ministry
of Energy, Utilities and Climate, including the Danish Energy Agency ("The Danish Ministry of Energy,
Utilities and Climate") (each, a "Party" and collectively, "the Parties") seek to enhance cooperation by
establishing a framework to encourage the exchange of knowledge and information between the Parties
and to develop innovative solutions for the development and procurement of offshore wind energy. In
recognition of the Parties' collaborative relationship and mutual interest in advancing the availability and
use of offshore wind energy, the Parties have reached the following Cooperation Agreement
("Agreement"):
ARTICLE I- OBJECTIVES
1. The Parties' objectives for this Agreement are to enhance cooperation and to establish a
framework for exchanging information and knowledge to help facilitate the development and
procurement of offshore wind energy. This framework shall include:
a. The exchange of information and commentary between the Parties regarding the
development of offshore wind energy, including but not limited to infrastructure,
transmission, supply chain, and workforce development;
b. The exchange of information and commentary between the Parties regarding the
procurement of offshore wind energy, including but not limited to procurement
mechanisms, requests for proposals, contract provisions, guarantees, penalties, and
award criteria;
C. Regular communication between the Parties regarding their experiences, research, and
learnings;
d. Trips to Denmark and New York;
e. Meetings with relevant governmental agencies and stakeholders, to the extent feasible;
and
f The planning and attending of joint events and seminars by the Parties.
2. The implementation of this Agreement and any activity hereunder shall be in accordance with
the Parties' respective applicable laws, regulations, rules, procedures, programs, and policies.
ARTICLE I - RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTIES
Each Party will:
1. Provide one or more representatives who will draft a Work Plan, commencing in October 2018
and continuing to the termination date of this Agreement (Article II, paragraph 1). The Work
Plan shall include, at a minimum: meetings and/or workshops between the Parties and relevant
governmental entities and stakeholders; a study trip to Denmark; bilateral knowledge exchange



meetings; and an industry seminar focused on infrastructure, supply chain, and workforce
development.
2. Provide one or more representatives to form a Working Group that will be responsible for
coordination and project supervision. The Working Group will be expected to conduct regular
meetings (at least twice per year) to discuss key tasks, objectives, and information exchange.
3. Share information relevant to the objectives of this Agreement as set forth in Article I
4. Engage in specific tasks to effectuate the objectives of this Agreement as set forth in Article I
including but not limited to participating in knowledge exchange meetings and industry
seminars.
5. Upon request, review and comment on drafts of proposed research proposals, orders, rules, or
other documents provided by the other Party.
6. To the extent either Party provides to the other Party information that the providing Party deems
confidential, the receiving Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of such information
pursuant to applicable lawl and the following conditions:
a. The providing Party will identify any confidential information to the receiving Party, and
explain the basis for such claimed confidentiality;
b. Each Party will protect from release information the other Party has identified as
'confidential" to the extent permitted by law;
C. Each Party will specify to the other its confidential information management procedures,
including procedures for sharing confidential data with the other Party;
d. Each Party will identify to the other Party a list of individuals who will have access to
confidential information;
e. Neither Party will share third party material that is deemed proprietary or otherwise
protected from disclosure by law2 and

ARTICLE II - GENERAL PROVISIONS
1. This Agreement shall come into force as of the date of full execution by both Parties below and,
unless terminated pursuant to subsection (b) below, shall expire on December 31, 2019 unless
extended or terminated prior to that date.
a. The Parties may agree to extend the term of this Agreement upon mutual written
agreement.
b. Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, upon 30 days'
written notice to the other Party.
2. This Agreement may not be amended or modified except by the written consent of the Parties
hereto.
3. This Agreement is not legally binding on the Parties and is non-exclusive; each Party reserves the
right to enter into similar Agreements with any other third party.
4. This Agreement does not supersede any other written agreements between the Parties hereto.
5. Each Party shall be responsible for its own expenses incurred in order to effectuate the objectives
of and responsibilities set forth in this Agreement. However, upon written agreement by both
Parties, an alternative funding mechanism may be used to cover the costs of particular activities
undertaken in connection with this Agreement.
6. Neither Party shall use the name, trademark, or logo of the other Party for any purpose, whether
in relation to any advertisement or any other form of publicity, without the prior written consent
of the other Party.

Extension of Cooperative Agreement
Reference is made to that Cooperation Agreement by and between the New York State Energy Research and
Development Authority (NYSERDA) and the Danish Ministry off Energy, Utilities and Climate ("the Parties") on
the Development and Procurement of Offshore Wind Energy (Agreement), executed by Denmark's Minister of
Energy, Utilities and Climate, Lars Christian Lilleholt, and NYSERDA President and CEO Alicia Barton on
September 9, 2018.
WHEREAS, the parties entered the Agreement for the purpose of enhancing cooperation and establishing a
framework to encourage and promote the exchange, between the Parties, ofl knowledge and information
regarding offshore wind energy, and to develop innovations and procurement of offshore wind energy; and
WHEREAS, Article III, Section 1 of the Agreement provides that the Agreement will terminate on December 31,
2019, unless extended upon the mutual written agreement of the Parties; and
WHEREAS, the Parties wish and intend to extend and continue the valuable cooperation and exchange of
knowledge and information made possible by the Agreement; and
NOW and THEREFORE, by signing below NYSERDA and the Danish Ministry of Energy, Utilities and
Climate hereby agree and do extend the Agreement termination date to December 31, 2022.
