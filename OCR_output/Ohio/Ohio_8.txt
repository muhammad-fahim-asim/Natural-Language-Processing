JOINT DECLARATION FOR ESTABLISHMENT
OF SISTERIOOD EBLATIONSHE
In accordance with the principles of equality and mutual
benefit, the State of Ohio of the United States of America
and Gyeongsang Bugdo of the Republic of Korea have
decided to establish a state-province - relationship. The two
parties will carry out multi-form and wide-range exchan-
ges and cooperation in the fields of economics, trade, science,
technology, education, and culture.
Working under the guidance of the official declaration of
friendship, the two parties have agreed to.cooperate on the
following:
1. To promote better understanding, friendship, and
goodwill among the people of the State of Ohio and
Gyeongsang Bugdo.
2. To promote better understanding of administration and
culture between the State of Ohio and Gyeongsang Bugdo.
3. To promote exchange of visits by business groups and
the exchange of information concerning economics
and trade opportunities between the State of Ohio and
Gyeongsang Bugdo.
4. Any other matters which will bring benefit to both part-
ies will be considered and solved through the cooperation
between the State of Ohio and Gyeongsang Bugdo
In witness whereof the governor. of the State of Ohio
and the governor of Gyeongsang Bugdo have executed and
proclaimed this agreement.
