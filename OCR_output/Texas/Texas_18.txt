Letter of Intent for the Establishment of the Friendly Exchange Relations
Between
The State of Texas, USA and Tianjin Municipal People's Government, PRC
In accordance with the joint communiqué governing the establishment of diplomatic
relations between the United States of America and the People's Republic of China, the State
of Texas and Tianjin Municipality, desiring to strengthen cooperation, to deepen
understanding and to promote friendship, believe it necessary to establish the friendly
exchange relations, and jointly agree upon the signing of this Letter of Intent for the
establishment of the friendly exchange relations between The State of Texas and Tianjin
Municipality through amicable negotiation.
1. In accord with this Letter of Intent and based upon the spirit of equality and mutual
benefit, both parties will actively encourage and promote the expansion of cooperation
and exchange in the areas of trade, culture, education, science, health, sports and tourism
and to broaden the areas for exchange.
2. The government of the two sides shall support exchange in the above-mentioned areas as
well as facilitate the exchange of delegation visits between the two signatories. Base on
the continual communication, the State of Texas and Tianjin Municipality will sign the
formal agreement of friendly exchange and cooperation relations in the opportune
occasion through friendly negotiation.
3. For the purpose of maintaining close contacts between the two signatories, the Texas
Economic Development and the Foreign Affairs Office of Tianjin Municipal People's
Government are appointed respectively as the liaison offices between the governments of
the two signatories.
4. This agreement is written in duplicate in the English and the Chinese languages, both texts
being equally authentic.
