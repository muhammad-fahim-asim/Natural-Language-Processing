MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN
THE STATE OF TEXAS
AND
THE REPUBLIC OF BULGARIA
The State of Texas of the United States of America and the Republic of Bulgaria, desiring to
strengthen cooperation, to deepen understanding and to promote friendship, believe it necessary to
establish friendly relations and jointly agree upon the signing of this Memorandum ofUnderstanding
for the Establishment of Friendly Relations between the State of Texas and the Republic of Bulgaria.
I. In accord with this Memorandum of Understanding and based upon goodwill, the spirit of
equality and mutual benefit, both parties will actively encourage and promote the
expansion of cooperation and exchange in the areas of trade, investment, tourism, science,
and sport. Both parties will facilitate the spread of information about the corresponding
partner thus helping to develop better mutual knowledge and people-to-people contacts.
II. For the purpose of maintaining close contacts between the two signatories, the Office of
the Secretary of State of the State ofTexas and the Embassy of the Republic of Bulgaria in
the United States of America are appointed respectively as the liaisons between the two
signatories.
III. The governments of the two sides shall support exchange in the above-mentioned areas as
well as facilitate the exchange of delegation visits between the two signatories.
This Memorandum of Understanding is signed by the two parties in expression of their willingness
to work together toward mutually beneficial endeavors and does not commit either party to any
legally binding terms, conditions and obligations. This Memorandum of Understanding shall be
valid until declared otherwise in writing by either party. The Memorandum of Understanding is
written in duplicate in the English and the Bulgarian languages, both texts being equally authentic.
The Memorandum of Understanding shall enter into force on the date of its signature.
This Memorandum of Understanding is signed on the 4th of May, 2004 in Austin, Texas, USA.
