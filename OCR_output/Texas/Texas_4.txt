ALLIANCE
between the SECRETARIAT OF ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT
of the STATE OF NUEVO LEON
and the TEXAS DEPARTMENT OF ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT
WHEREAS, the objectives ofthe Secretariat ofEconomic Development ofthe State ofNuevo Leon
(hereinafter SEDEC) are stated in the Nuevo Leon Public Administration Organic Law, Ley
Organica para la Administracion Publica para el. Estado de Nuevo Leon, sections 4,11,16, and 21
VIII, XII, XIII; and
WHEREAS, the aims and objectives of SEDEC include the promotion and stimulation of
international trade and commerce and the fostering ofmutual commercial exchanges and alliances;
and
WHEREAS, an objective ofthe Nuevo Leon Development State Plan "Plan Estatal de Desarrollo
1997-2003"is to foster the exportation ofproducts and services and to attract foreign investmentand
new technologies to the State ofNuevo Leon; and
WHEREAS, thej powers and duties ofthe Texas Department ofEconomic Development (hereinafter
Department) are codified at Texas Government Code, Chapter 481; and
WHEREAS, the duties ofthe Department include the promotion ofinternational trade and tourism
in Texas;
NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved that the two departments will work cooperatively to increase
business and tourism between the State ofNuevo Leon and the State ofTexas, and toward that goal
agree to use best efforts to advance such programs and initiatives of mutual benefit and concem as
may be identified from time to time, including, as follows:
*
Maintain open dialogue and communication
*
Promote and develop airline connections

Organize meetings and trade missions
*
Initiate a regional strategic plan to strengthen economic development along our common
border
*
Share technological advances and experience in lake management and fishing
*
Promote the establishment of cross-border trade corridors for the shipment of goods
*
Develop trade corridors between the State ofNuevo Leon and the State of Texas
*
Establishjoint programs orientedtowards developing suppliers for thein-bond(maquiladora)
industry



Exchange statistics on border crossings
*
Share information to increase workforce productivity and micro and small business
development
*
Strengthen educational exchanges in areas related to economic development
*
Support the development of the marketing plan for the Two Nation Vacation Program and
the efforts of the Frontera Norte Program
*
Support a joint study of the winter tourism market
*
Support the exchange ofinformation on tourism market initiatives
*
Promote cultural exchanges, particularly of literature, music, and sports
Promote the exchangeoftourism) products such as ecotourism, health, adventure, fishing, and
hunting tourism
*
Promote the establishment ofi information booths at international crossings

Provide each other with information relating to companies and individuals who wish to
conduct commercial transactions
*
Advise each other of business opportunities, including opportunities for licensing,
distributor, and representation agreements; joint ventures; investments; acquisitions; and
other transactions of interest
*
Provide assistance and advice when visiting each other's state
This document is not intended to be legally binding upon the parties, but represents a memorandum
ofmutual understanding, alliance, and cooperation.
IN ACCORD HEREWITH, witness our hands effective this 11 day of December, 2000.
