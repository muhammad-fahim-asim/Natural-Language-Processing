Memorandum of Cooperation
Between
Chinese Provinces
And
The State of Texas
Of the United States of America
The Ministry of Commerce of the People's Republic of China, with Tianjin, Fujian,
Shandong, Jiangsu, Sichuan and the State of Texas (the Parties) recognizing the
importance of developing and expanding ties of cooperation through business, trade,
investment, tourism, education, research and development, have decided to establish
"China Provinces and State of Texas Joint Working Group" (hereinafter referred to as the
"Working Group").
The Parties acknowledge the importance of commercial, scientific and cultural
connections to their respective economies. They undertake to expand existing links by
exploring mutually beneficial collaborative opportunities with a goal of increasing the
exchange ofinformation and increasing trade and investment flows.
Consequently, the Parties have: reached the following understanding of cooperation:
to explore the potential for enhancing business networks, alliances and
collaborative projects between their respective government agencies, industry
organizations, and educational and research institutes that will aim to deliver
benefits to the public good of Chinese Provinces and the State ofTexas; and
to support ongoing staff collaboration to increase knowledge, enhance expertise
and develop best practices in relation to economic and industry development,
policy development, training and project approval.
The forms of joint initiatives and collaborations between the Parties may include but are
not limited to:
regular trade and investment delegations;
research projects between academic institutions;
development of partnerships exploring joint industry and economic development
initiatives;
collaborative industry-related training initiatives;
collaboration between government agencies and the private sectors of Chinese
Provinces and US State of Texas to increase industry-related competency levels
and experience; and
promotion of industry-related events.
The Parties respectively designate the China International Contractors Association
(CHINCA) and Office of the Governor of Texas, Economic Development and Tourism
Division as representatives of the business community to join the Working Group and
assume the functions and responsibilities of the Working Group secretariat for daily
communication, contact, information exchange, business project promotion and business
activities organization.



STATE OF TEXAS
OFFICE OF THE GOVERNOR
This Memorandum of Coopcration:
will commence on the date of the final execution;
may be modified by agreement in writing by the Parties;
can be terminated by either Party with written notice;
is a statement of intent of cooperation between the Parties, but is not intended as a
legally binding document and does not create legal obligations.
Any dispute arising out of the interpretation or implementation of this Memorandum of
Cooperation shall be settled amicably by consultation and negotiation in good faith
between the Parties.
