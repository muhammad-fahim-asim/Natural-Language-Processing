Arrangement
between the Texas Department of Public Safety
and the National Police Agency of the Republic of Korea
on the Mutual Recognition of Driver's Licenses
The Texas Department of Public Safety (the "Department") and the National Police
Agency of the Republic of Korea (the "NPA") (hereinafter jointly referred to as the
"Participants");
Desiring to facilitate the ease with which persons from Texas living in the Republic
of Korea ("Korea") and persons from Korea living in Texas can obtain a driver's
license; and
Acknowledging that each Participant has established comparable knowledge and
driving skills requirements in which an applicant must demonstrate, under comparable
testing standards, knowledge and driving skills before being issued a non-commercial
driver's license;
Have reached the following arrangement:
1. (a) The Department may allow a person who is aged eighteen (18) or older, lawfully
present in the United States, and residing in Texas, who possesses a valid non-
commercial Korean driver's license (First Class Special Driver's License, First Class
Large Driver's License, First Class Ordinary Driver's License, Second Class Ordinary
Driver's License) to exchange that license for a Texas Class C driver's license for a
nominal fee after passing an eyesight test, without, however, being required to take any
knowledge or skills tests. All other requirements in Texas law and policy will apply.
(b) The NPA may allow a person who is aged eighteen (18) or older residing in
Korea who possesses a valid, unexpired Class C Texas driver's license may exchange

2. The Participants may verify with each other that the applicant has a valid driver's
licence issued by the respective agency and will provide to each other samples of
driver's licenses in circulation at the date of the coming into effect of this Arrangement
and any versions which may be introduced in the future. The samples of driver's
licenses and requests for verification will be sent to the addresses listed in Annex 1.
3. (a) The Department will send Korean driver's licenses exchanged for Texas driver's
licenses to the address listed in Annex 2.
(b) The NPA will send Texas driver's licenses exchanged for Korean driver's licenses
to the address listed in Annex 2.
4. The Department may require an applicant to provide a translation of any documents
presented as identification into the English language.
5. This Arrangement is subject to the laws and regulations in force in the Republic of
Korea and the State of Texas.
6. Any changes to this Arrangement will be in writing and approved by both
Participants.
