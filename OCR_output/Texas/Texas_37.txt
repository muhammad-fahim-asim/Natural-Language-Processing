Memorandum of Understanding
On Trade and Mutual Economic Co-operation
Between
Texas Office of the Governor, Economic Development & Tourism
(OOGEDT)
And the
Fujian Department of Foreign Trade and Economic Cooperation
(Fujian DOFTEC)
Whereas Texas OOGEDT and Fujian DOFTEC, in recognition ofincreased economic
Cooperation have established an enduring and positive relationship based on many
mutual interests; and
Whereas OOGEDT and Fuian (DOFTEC), wish to build on the experience of the Texas
Business & Investment Mission to China during the month of June 2007; and
Whereas OOGEDT and Fujian DOFTEC, recoguize:
The dramatic increase in the value of trade between global partners;
The overall importance of trade and investment relationships between China and the
United States, and between Fujian and Texas;
The increasing need for interrelationship in a global economy;
The advantages to be derived from the strengthening and broadening of mutually
beneficial trade and investment relations;
- The value of close consultations and co-operation on trade issues and the need to ensure
open lines of communication and sharing of publicly available information; and
The importance ofworking together in matters of international trade significance.
Therefore, Texas OOGEDT and Fujiang DODTEC intend to work co-operatively to the
fullest possible extend consistent with law, existing treaties, and privacy requirements of
their respective state or prince and nation to:
Promote and facilitate bilateral trade;
Co-ordinate activities in international development;
Promote the ongoing relationship
Therefore, according to their respective activities, Texas OOGEDT and Fujian DOFTEC
intend to work co-operatively to the fullest possible extend consistent with law, existing
treaties, and privacy requirements of their respective state or province and nation to:
A. The commercial promotion, on an international level, of products, services, and
technologies of Texas and Fujian DOFTEC; and



B. The development and delivery of international projects, initiatives and consulting
service for exporters and companies ready for export.
To carry out these objectives, the parties agree to promote the expansion oftrade and
business relation between Texas and Fujian DOFTEC. The parties will work together
to identify target sectors for joint initiatives, including but not limited to: oil and gas
exploration, petroleum refining, petrochemicals production, high-technology, bio-
technology, medical field, agricultural business, and service industries.
It is the intention of parties to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between
Texas OOGEDT and Fujian DOFTEC. This Memorandum ofUnderstanding between
the two parties does not represent a commitment by either party to any terms,
conditions and obligations, but does represent a willingness to work together toward
mutually beneficial trade, investment and economic development endeavors.
