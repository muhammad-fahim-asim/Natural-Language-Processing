BACKGROUND, DECLARATIONS AND CLAUSES:
BACKGROUND
a) The regional integration efforts put forth by the States of the Northeast Mexico Region,
through the signing of the Agreement for Sustainable Regional Development of the
Mexican Northeastern Region on March 6 of 2004.
b) The Agreement for Regional Progress signed by the Governors of the States of Coahuila,
Nuevo Leon and Tamaulipas from the Northeastern Mexico Region; and the Governor of
the State of Texas on June 22 of 2004.
DECLARATIONS
I.- Through their representatives, the States declare that:
The State of Chihuahua is interested in joining efforts to collaborate as part of the Agreement
for Regional Progresses as to benefit and strengthen, through cooperative programs and
actions, the competitiveness and the integral development of each state and the Northeast
Mexico- Texas Region as a whole.
The: States of Coahuila, Nuevo Leon, Tamaulipas and Texas welcome the State of Chihuahua
and accepti its inclusion for the benefit of the Northeastermn-Texas Region.
All parties agree that the terms and conditions established in the Agreement for Regional
Progress are binding as a part of this Addendum, , and that issues related to the intent of this
document and the Agreement that are: not expressly stated in this declarations, will be settled
by consensus as long as thej parties so determine.
