STATE OF TEXAS
OFFICE OF THE GOVERNOR
STATEMENT OF MUTUAL COOPERATION
BETWEEN
TEXAS OFFICE OF THE GOVERNOR,
ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT AND TOURISM DIVISION
AND
THE JAPAN BANK FOR INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION ("JBIC"), JAPAN
In the spirit of joint economic development cooperation and in recognition of the strong economic
and cultural ties between Japan and the State of" Texas of the United States of America ("Texas"), the
Texas Office of the Governor, Economic Development and Tourism Division, and JBIC, Japan
(hereinafter "the Parties"), hereby express their sincere desire to establish a relationship, with the
goals of encouraging collaboration, increasing prosperity in both regions, and contributing to the
further development of favorable relations between Texas and Japan. This relationship is subject to
the prevailing laws and regulations in the State of Texas, the United States of America, and Japan,
and founded as follows upon the principles of equality, openness, mutual respect, and benefit:
I. the Parties endeavor to collaborate in fields in Texas and Japan identified by both
Parties, which may include energy, mobility, digital connectivity, resilience, and
water, in the interest of the development of both regions;
II. the Parties endeavor to facilitate private sector exchange in Texas and Japan in a wide
range of areas, which may include banking and project financing, in the interest of the
development of both regions; and
III. the Parties endeavor to assist each other in understanding their respective region's
legal, regulatory, and investment environments, in thei interest ofmutual understanding
and ease of participation.
This Statement of Mutual Cooperation is solely an expression of the parties' desire to establish a
mutually beneficial relationship and creates no legally binding rights or obligations. This Statement
of Mutual Cooperation is intended to be in place until August 31, 2021.
Signed in Tokyo, Japan, on September 27, 2019, in recognition of the above articles.
