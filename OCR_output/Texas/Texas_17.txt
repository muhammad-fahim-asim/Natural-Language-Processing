Letter of Intent for the Establishment of
The Friendly Exchange Relations
Between
The State of Texas and Zhejiang Province
In accordance with the joint communiqué governing the establishment of diplomatic
relations between the United States of America and the People's Republic of China, the
State of Texas and Zhejiang Province, desiring to strengthen cooperation, to deepen
understanding and to promote friendship, believe it necessary to establish the friendly
exchange relations, and jointly agree upon the signing ofthis Letter of Intent for the
establishment of the friendly exchange relations between The State of Texas and
Zhejiang Province through amicable negotiation.
1. In accord with this Letter of Intent and based upon the spirit of equality and mutual
benefit, both parties will actively encourage and promote the expansion of cooperation
and exchange in the areas of trade, culture, education, science, health, sports and tourism
and to broaden the areas for exchange.
2. For the purpose of maintaining close contacts between the two signatories, the Texas
Economic Department and the Zhejiang Foreign Affairs Department are appointed
respectively as the liaison offices between the governments of the two signatories.
3. The governments of the two sides shall support exchange in the above-mentioned areas
as well as facilitate the exchange of delegation visits between the two signatories.
This agreement is written in duplicate in the English and the Chinese languages, both
texts being equally authentic. The agreement shall enter into force on the date ofits
signature.
