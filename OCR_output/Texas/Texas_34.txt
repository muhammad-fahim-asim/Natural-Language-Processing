Memorandum of Understanding
On Trade and Mutual Economic Co-operation
between
Texas Economic Development (TxED)
and the
Shangdong Department of Foreign Trade and Economic
Cooperation
(ShangdongDOFTEC)
Whereas TxED and ShangdongDOFTEC, in recognition of increased economic
cooperation have established an enduring and positive relationship based on many mutual
interests; and
Whereas TxED and ShangdongDOFTEC, wish to build on the experience of the Texas
Business Mission to China during the month of December 2002; and
Whereas TxED and ShangdongDOFTEC, recognize:
The dramatic increase in the value of trade between global partners;
The overall importance of trade and investment relationships between China and the
United States, and between ShangdongDOFTEC and Texas;
The increasing need for interrelationships in a global economy;
The advantages to be derived from the strengthening and broadening of mutually
beneficial trade and investment relations;
The value of close consultations and co-operation on trade issues and the need to ensure
open lines of communication and sharing of publicly available information; and
The importance of working together in matters of international trade significance.
Therefore, TxED and ShangdongDODTEC intend to work co-operatively to the fullest possible
extent consistent with law, existing treaties, and privacy requirements of their respective state or
province and nation to:
Promote and facilitate bilateral trade;
Co-ordinate activities in international development;
Promote the ongoing relationship
Therefore, according to their respective activities, TxED and ShangdongDOFTEC intend to work
co-operatively to the fullest possible extent consistent with law, existing treaties, and privacy
requirements of their respective state or province and nation to:
A. The commercial promotion, on an international level, of products, services and
technologies of' Texas and ShangdongDOFTEC; and
B. The development and delivery of international projects, initiatives and consulting
service for exporters and companies ready for export.



To carry out these objectives, the parties agree to promote the expansion of trade and business
relations between Texas and ShangdongDOFTEC. The parties will work together to identify
target sectors for joint initiatives, including but not limited to: oil and gas exploration, petroleum
refining, petrochemicals production, high-technology, bio-technology, medical field, agricultural
business, and service industries.
It is the intention of parties to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between TxED and
ShangdongDOFTEC. This Memorandum of Understanding between the two parties does not
represent a commitment by either party to any terms, conditions and obligations, but does
represent a willingness to work together toward mutually beneficial-trade, investment and
economic development endeavors.
