MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
ON TRADE, TOURISM AND MUTUAL
ECONOMIC CO-OPERATION
BETWEEN
THE STATE OF TEXAS
AND
THE PROVINCE OF MANITOBA

WHEREAS the State of Texas and the Province of Manitoba, in recognition of sharing the
Mid-Continent Trade and Transportation Corridor, extending border-to-border-to-border in the
Continent of North America, have established an enduring and positive relationship based on
mutual interests; and
WHEREAS Texas and Manitoba have a strong and growing trade relationship and wish to
build upon the experience of their participation in the Team Canada West Trade Mission to
Texas on November 27-29, 2001; and
WHEREAS Texas and Manitoba recognize:
the dramatic increase in the value of trade between all Mid-Continent Trade and
Transportation Corridor partners and the significant 78 percent increase in trade
between the state and the province over the last five years;
the growing overall importance of the Mid-Continent Trade and Transportation
Corridor trade relationships between Canada, the United States and Mexico and
between Texas and Manitoba;
the increasing need for interrelationships between all Mid-Continent trading partners;
the advantages to be derived from strengthening and broadening of mutually beneficial
Mid-Continent trade and investment relations;
the value of close consultations and cooperation on Mid-Continent Trade Corridor issues
and the need to ensure open lines of communication and sharing of publidy available
information; and
the importance of working together and in concert with their respective federal
governments in matters of international and Mid-Continent corridor trade significance.
Now, therefore, in the spirit of promoting closer relationships, the State of Texas and the Province
ofl Manitoba intend to work co-operatively to the fullest possible extent consistent with the law
and existing treaties between their respective nations to:
1. Promote and Facilitate Bilateral Trade
Bilateral trade between Texas and Manitoba now approaches US $480 million annually.
The Governments of Texas and Manitoba share a strong interest in expanding this economic
partnership and building on the shared potential of emerging export opportunities within the
Americas and across the globe.
The Governments of Texas and Manitoba, co-ordinated by Texas Economic Development and
the Department of Industry, Trade and Mines in Manitoba, therefore propose to:
1.1. work together to identify target sectors for joint initiatives including but not limited to
agribusiness, aerospace, component manufacturing, film, energy, biotechnology and
information and communication technologies;



1.2. work together to identify opportunities for strategic partnerships and alliances between
private sector companies in Texas and Manitoba which will strengthen their capabilities
to compete in international markets;
1.3. explore opportunities for joint trade promotions, beginning with the sharing of publicly
available information on priority markets and planned promotional activities, followed
by the identification of possible joint trade missions to targeted markets;
1.4. support regional and federal initiatives to facilitate the border-to-border-to-border
movement of goods and people;
1.5. share publicly available information on and learn from each other's best practices and
the world-class expertise found in both jurisdictions; and promote common goals
by encouraging co-operation and understanding between business, municipal,
state/provincial and federal governments, and citizens of' Texas and Manitoba.
2. Promote and Encourage Co-operation in Tourism
The Governments of Texas and Manitoba intend to work together to expand bilateral tourism
opportunities. The Governments senior tourism officials, who will operate under the direction
oft the Executive Director of Texas Economic Development and the Minister ofCulture, Heritage
and' Tourism in Manitoba, will explore a tourism strategy that will include:
2.1. the identification and sharing of mutually beneficial tourism marketing initiatives;
2.2. the facilitation of the development of travel packages that promote the advantages of
each jurisdiction.
3. Promote the Mid-Continent Trade and Transportation Corridor
Texas and Manitoba share the Mid-Continent Trade and Transportation Corridor which extends
border-to-border-to-border in the North American continent. Officials from Texas and Manitoba
will work together to:
3.1. establish a seamless transportation infrastructure which includes the harmonization of
regulations along the corridor and more efficient and effective border crossings; and,
3.2. asa result of the improvements made in the transportation infrastructure along the corridor,
create a more competitive environment for private sector companies in both Texas and
Manitoba and create an inducement to attract new inward investment to the state and the
province.
4. Coordinate Activities in International Development/Co-operation Agreements
The Governments of Texas and Manitoba agree to explore opportunities to co-ordinate their
efforts in furthering the goals and objectives of their respective co-operative agreements aimed at
strengthening trade and investment links and improving the effectiveness of government services.



PR
5. Promote the Ongoing Relationship
The Governments of Manitoba and Texas recognize the benefits of ongoing, regular contacts
between their respective governments to promote trade and economic development and identify
areas in which there are opportunities for joint formal or informal co-operation.
Therefore' Texas and Manitoba intend to:
5.1. monitor progress on the implementation of this Memorandum ofUnderstanding:
5.2. designate state and provincial government: representatives to serve as principal points
ofp policy contact and liaison to promote co-operation in areas of mutual interest;
5.3. encourage the executive departments of the State ofTexas and the Province of Manitoba
to work together to promote co-operative efforts within their respective areas of
responsibility; and,
5.4. explore other co-operative initiatives on matters of common interest, such as education,
cultural and sports exchanges, animal health, agricultural harmonization, and transportation,
that will be considered complementary to this Memorandum ofUnderstanding.
