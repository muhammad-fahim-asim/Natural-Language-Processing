Memorandum of Understanding
On Trade, Investment, and Mutual Economic Co-operation
Between the Texas Department of
Economic Development (TxED)
And the
Shanghai Municipal Foreign Economic Relations &
Trade Commission (SMERT)
Whereas TxED and SMERT, in recognition of increased economic cooperation have
established an enduring and positive relationship based on many mutual interests; and
Whereas TxED and SMERT wish to build on the experience of the Texas Business
Mission to China during the month of May 2002; and
Whereas TxED and SMERT: recognize:
The dramatic increase in the value of trade between global partners;
The overall importance of trade and investment relationships between China
and the United States, and between Shanghai and Texas;
The advantages to be derived from the strengthening and broadening of
mutually beneficial trade and investment relations;
The value of close consultations and co-operation on trade issues and the need
to ensure open lines of communication and sharing of publicly available
information; and
The importance of working together in matters of international trade
significance.
Therefore, in a spirit of promoting closer relations, TxED and SMERT: intend to work
co-operatively to the fullest possible extent consistent with law, existing treaties, and
privacy requirements of their respective state or municipality. and nation to:
Promote and facilitate bilateral trade
Promote mutual investment
Coordinate activities in international trade and investment development
Promote the ongoing cooperative relationship
Therefore TxED and SMERT s' primary objective guiding their respective activities
ate;
A. The commercial promotion, on an international level, of products,
services and technologies of Texas and Shanghai; and
B. The development and delivery of international projects and initiatives
for exporters and Companies ready for export.



Whereas, to carry out these objectives, the parties agree to promote the expansion of
trade investment and business relations between Texas and Shanghai. The parties will
work together to identify target sectors of mutual interest, including but not limited to:
oil and gas exploration, petroleum refining, petrochemicals production,
high-technology, bio-technology, medical field, agricultural business, and service
industries, etc.
It is the intention of parties to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between
TxED and SMERT. This Memorandum ofUnderstanding between the two parties does
not represent a commitment by either party to any terms, conditions and obligations,
but does represent a willingness to work together toward mutually beneficial trade and
investment, exchanges and communications and economic development endeavours.
Executed in Shanghai, People's Republic of China, as of the 28 of
nau
2002.
