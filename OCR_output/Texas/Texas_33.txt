MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING ON COOPERATION BETWEEN THE
STATE OF COAHUILA DE ZARAGOZA, THE UNITED MEXICAN STATES AND
THE TEXAS SECRETARY OF STATE, THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
THE STATE OF COAHUILA DE ZARAGOZA OF THE UNITED MEXICAN
STATES ("THE STATE OF COAHUILA") AND THE TEXAS SECRETARY OF
STATE, THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA ("TEXAS SOS"), which acting as a
whole shall be referred to as "Parties";
CONSIDERING the interest in deepening the bonds of friendship and cooperation
that unite both Parties;
RECOGNIZING that international cooperation is one of the factors that favorably
affects the solution of problems that affect their respective communities;
CONVINCED of the importance of establishing mechanisms that contribute to the
development and strengthening of the bilateral cooperation framework, as well as
the need to implement projects and actions that have an effective impact on the
economic and social progress of the Parties;
Set out the following intentions and understandings of the Parties:
Article 1
Objective
The purpose of this Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is to establish
mechanisms for bilateral cooperation relating to economic, social and cultural
development, between the State of Coahuila and the Texas SOS.
Article 2
Cooperation Areas
In order to achieve the objective of this MOU, the Parties intend to cooperate in the
following areas:
a) Commerce, investments, and business;
b) Energy;
c) Hydrocarbons;
d) Security;
e) Education;
f) Science and technology;
g) Culture;
h) Tourism;
Page 1 of 3



i) Environment; and
j) Any other cooperation area which the parties mutually identify.
Article 3
Financing
The Parties will finance any activities referred to in this MOU with the resources
allocated in their respective budgets, in accordance with their availability and the
provisions of their legislation. Each Party shall bear its own expenses related to its
participation in activities undertaken in the furtherance of the objectives of this
MOU.
Article 4
International Instruments
The cooperation referred to in this MOU will not affect any rights and obligations
that the Parties have acquired under any international instruments.
Article 5
Nature of the Memorandum of Understanding
This MOU serves only as a statement of intention of the Parties and is not intended
to create any legally binding obligations nor be construed as agreement on any
matters mentioned. This MOU does not create any relationship of employment,
agency, partnership, or joint venture between the Parties.
Any disagreement under this MOU shall be resolved through amicable
consultation, based on principles of mutual benefit, equality, cooperation, and trust.
Article 6
Term, Modification, and Termination
This Memorandum of Understanding will become effective as of the date of its
signature and will have a three (3) year term.
This Memorandum of Understanding may be modified by mutual consent of the
Parties, formalized through written communications, specifying the date of entry
into force of such modifications.
Notwithstanding the expiration of the validity of this MOU as provided above, this
MOU may be terminated by any of the Parties, upon written notification, thirty (30)
business days in advance.
