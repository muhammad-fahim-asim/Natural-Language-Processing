Confidentiality Undertaking
among members of the Supervisory College for
American International Group ("Institution")
1. The Participants and Observers identified in Annex A (each an - Authority" and together, the
"Authorities"), all of which are participants in the Supervisory College for the Institution ("SC")
have executed this Confidentiality Undertaking ("this Document") for exchanging confidential
information in connection with the activities of the SC. The exchange of such information in
connection with the SC will assist the Authorities in carrying out their respective
responsibilities.
2. This Document applies to confidential or proprietary information, including supervisory
information shared in connection with the SC. For purposes of this Document, "Confidential
Information" means any information that the Authority providing or producing the information
("Producing Authority") has not made public, including information that is confidential,
privileged or otherwise protected from disclosure under the laws applicable to the Producing
Authority.
3. The Federal Reserve will coordinate information sharing in connection with regular and ad hoc
SC meetings. The Authorities will maintain up-to-date contact lists, which will include contact
details for key senior and working-level staff, and the Federal Reserve will be responsible for
maintaining and facilitating access to those lists by all Authorities.
4. Each Authority will use any Confidential Information only for lawful purposes (including
ensuring compliance) related to the Authority's financial stability, supervisory, or safety and
soundness functions, including recovery and resolution planning and crisis management. In
addition, each Authority will hold confidential all Confidential Information it receives from a
Producing Authority to the fullest extent permitted by its respective laws and will not disclose it
except as follows:
(i)
Except as provided below in subparagraph (ii), before an Authority discloses any
Confidential Information to any other person, including any other Authority or
another governmental entity that is not a signatory to this Document, the Authority
seeking to disclose such Confidential Information will request and obtain prior
written consent from the Producing Authority.
(ii) In the event that an Authority is required by statute or legal process, including a
legally enforceable demand, to disclose Confidential Information, it will, to the
extent permitted by its respective law, inform the Producing Authority about such
possible compelled disclosure in advance of sharing such Confidential Information.
Ifthe Producing Authority does not consent to such disclosure, the Authority which
is required to make the disclosure will, to the extent possible and appropriate, take
reasonable steps to resist disclosure, including by employing legal means
(including asserting all appropriate legal exemptions or privileges that may be
available) to challenge an order to disclose Confidential Information and by
advising the requesting party of the possible negative consequences that such



disclosure might have on the future exchange of Confidential Information or other
non-public information with the Producing Authority.
(iii) Subject to the foregoing, access to Confidential Information at each Authority will
be restricted to those staff members who have a legitimate need for such
information, in connection with the receiving Authority's lawful activities, and
Confidential Information will be handled carefully and appropriately.
(iv) No privileges or confidentiality associated with information provided by any
Authority are waived as a result of sharing information as contemplated by this
Document. All Confidential Information, in whatever form, provided to an
Authority by a Producing Authority remains the property of the Producing
Authority.
(v) The Authorities acknowledge that an Authority's inability or failure to comply with
the foregoing limitations may result in that Authority having limited or no access
to Confidential Information in the future.
5. This Document sets forth the Authorities' intentions with regard to the exchange, use, and
protection of information, in connection with the SC to the extent permitted by their respective
law. It does not create legally binding obligations, confer any rights, or supersede or modify
domestic laws or regulations, and it does not give rise to any legal claim on behalf of any
Authority or third party.
6. The Authorities may have existing bilateral or multilateral information sharing arrangements,
understandings, or agreements ("agreements") or may execute such agreements in the future.
This Document is not intended to amend or supersede existing agreements or limit the terms of
any future agreements.
7. This Document may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original
and all ofwhich together are the same document.
