LETTER OF INTENT ON TRADE, TOURISM AND MUTUAL
ECONOMIC CO-OPERATION
Between
THE STATE OF TEXAS
and
THE STATE OF SAO PAULO
Considering
The promotion, on an international level, ofproducts, services and technologies of
Texas and Sao Paulo
The development and delivery ofinternational projects and initiatives for exporters
and companies ready to export
conclude the following agreement
The Secretary ofScience, Technology, Economic Development and Tourism and the Texas
Department ofl Economic Development agree to promote the expansion oftrade and
business relations between the State ofSao Paulo and the State ofTexas. The mode ofthis
co-operation is set out in the following articles.
ARTICLEI
The parties will use their best endeavours to promote trade and business relations between
the State ofSao Paulo and the State ofTexas and share information as is publicly available
on important economic and trade issues, including developments in their respective
economic and trade policies which might affect trade and business relations between the
State ofSao Paulo and the State ofTexas.
