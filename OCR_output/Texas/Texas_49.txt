STATEMENT OF MUTUAL COOPERATION
BETWEEN
THE TEXAS ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT AND TOURISM OFFICE
IN THE OFFICE OF THE TEXAS GOVERNOR
AND
THE MINISTRY FOR FOREIGN AFFAIRS OF FINLAND
In recognition of the strong economic ties between the State of Texas and the Republic of Finland
and in the spirit ofjoint economic development, the Ministry for Foreign Affairs of Finland and the
Texas Economic Development and Tourism Office in the Office oft the Texas Governor hereby
express their desires under this Statement ofMutual Cooperation (SMC) to establish a relationship
with the goals of encouraging collaboration, increasing prosperity in both regions, and contributing to
the further development of favorable relations between Texas and Finland. The participants endeavor
to work together to help facilitate the expansion of businesses, encourage the creation ofjobs, and
promote innovation by collaboration and joint research in areas including, but not limited to:
I. Connectivity and new and emerging technologies, such as 5G and 6G, as well as high-
performance computing, including artificial intelligence, quantum technologies, and
cybersecurity;
II. Aerospace and aviation endeavors;
III. Digital transformation and end-user applications, including health, energy, transportation
and logistics, manufacturing, and built environments;
IV. Energy security, reliability, and innovation, as well as smart grids and transportation
solutions: such as marine and electric vehicles; and
V. Energy-efficient materials, including processes in the built environment or building
technologies.
This relationship is subject to the prevailing laws and regulations of the State ofTexas, the United
States of America, and the Republic ofl Finland. This SMC is solely an expression of the participants'
desires to establish a mutually beneficial relationship and creates no legally binding rights or
obligations, nor does it constitute any financial commitment by either participant. Each participant
may conduct the cooperative activities described in this SMC at its discretion and subject to the
availability of funds and personnel. This SMC shall become effective on the date of last signature
below and shall remain in effect for a period of five years. The SMC can be renewed for successive
five-year periods by a mutual decision of the participants.
Thej participants may extend the terms of this SMC. Either participant may terminate this SMC upon
written notice to the other participant.
