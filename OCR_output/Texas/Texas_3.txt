At the invitation of the Honorable Gwyn Shea, Secretary of State, (hereinafter "State"), the
Honorable Tingsheng Lin, Vice Governor of Shandong Province (hereinafter "Province"), and
the Shandong Science, Technology and Economic Delegation, visited the State in April 2002.
The Vice Governor Tingsheng Lin participated in meeting with the Honorable Rick Perry,
Governor ofthe State of Texas, and other representatives from economic, industrial, educational,
scientific, and technology sectors.
Province and State leadership determined the interpersonal contacts and cooperation in the areas
ofbusiness, trade and education between Province and State have grown since the establishment
of the Friendship-Province: Agreement in 1993. State and Province leadership agreed that there
exists great potential to promote resources of both Province and State through continued
economic and social development. Accordingly, both governments agree:
1. To support and encourage their people and governments to further develop interpersonal
contacts and exchanges and cooperation between State and Province, and to promote
mutual understanding and friendship;
2. To support and encourage economic and trade cooperation between State and Province
based on equality and mutual benefit;
3. To promote exchange and training programs between universities and a friendly
relationship between State and Province;

The Shandong Provincial Department ofForeign Trade and Economic Cooperation and the
Texas Department ofEconomic Development agree to promote the expansion oftrade and
business relations between the Province of Shandong and the State ofTexas. The mode ofthis
co-operation is set out in the following articles.
ARTICLE I
The parties will use their best endeavours to promote trade and business relations between the
Province ofShandong and the State of Texas and share information us is publicly available on
important economic and trade issues, including developments in their respective economic and
trade; policies which might affect trade and business relations between the Province ofShandong
and the State ofTexas.

ARTICLE II
The parties will exchange trade enquiries from their respective home markets.
ARTICLE III
The parties agree to provide such current market information and business contacts to each
other upon request and availability.
ARTICLE IV
Each party will actively promote exchange visits ofbusinessmen in pursuit ofindustrial and
commercialjoint ventures. This assistance and support, however, can only be supplementary to
the efforts of Shandong Provincial Department ofForeign Trade and Economic Cooperation and
the Texas Department of Economic Development 's own representatives in Shandong and Texas,
respectively.
ARTICLE V
The parties will offer all reasonable assistance in respect of visiting trade missions, including
but not limited to, supplying relevant market information and facilitating the introduction of
visiting trade missions to representatives of relevant industries.
ARTICLE VI
This agreement shall enter into force on the date of its signature by the representatives ofboth
parties concerned and will remain valid for two years.
It will be extended automatically, by one, year at a time, unless written notice is given by one
party to the other for termination ofthe agreement not less than six months before the next date
ofexpiration. The agreement will be executed on a best effort basis and is not intended to create
any legal obligation. for either institution.
This agreement is signed in Austin, Texas, United States ofAmerica, on the 11th day ofApril, in
the year 2002.
