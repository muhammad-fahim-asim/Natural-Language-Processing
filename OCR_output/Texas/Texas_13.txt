The Texas Department of Public Safety presents its compliments to the Consulate General of France in
Houston and has the honor to inform him of the following:
The Department acknowledges that France has established comparable knowledge and driving skills
requirements which an applicant must demonstrate under comparable standards for testing knowledge
and driving skills before being issued a driver license.
The Department may allow a person over the age of eighteen, lawfully present in the United States, and
residing in Texas, who possesses a valid Category B French driver license to exchange that license for a
Texas Class C driver license for a nominal fee after passing an eyesight test, without, however, being
required to take the knowledge and skills test. All other requirements in Texas law and policy shall
apply.
The Department will require all applicants for a driver license to present proof of identity satisfactory to
the Department and will apply the same application standards, rules, and regulations in licensing
decisions as they would for any resident of Texas including a withdrawal or revocation of a driver
license already issued.
The Department may verify with France that the applicant has a valid French driver license and may
provide to France samples of driver licenses in circulation at the date of this correspondence and any
versions which may be introduced in the future to assist France recognize valid Texas driver licenses.
The requests for authentication shall be sent to the Department of the French National Register of

The Department will send French driver licenses exchanged for a Texas driver license to the French
Consulate in Houston for transmission to the competent French authority.
The Department may require a practical driving test and/or medical documentation attesting to physical
or mental capabilities only in situations where the Department has cause to believe that the person is not
physically or mentally qualified to operate a motor vehicle safely.
The Department may require an applicant to provide a translation of any documents presented as
identification and may require that any translations be limited to an approved list of translators.
The Department requests that France confirm that a person over the age of eighteen residing in France
who possesses a valid, unexpired Class C Texas driver license may exchange that license for a Category
B French license for a nominal fee after passing an eyesight test, without, however, being required to
take the knowledge and skills test. All other requirements in French law and policy shall apply.

The Consulate-General of France in Houston presents its compliments to
the Texas Department of Public Safety and has the honor to acknowledge receipt of
its letter of February 25 th, 2010 listing the conditions under which Texas shall
exchange driver licences issued by French authorities.
Furthermore, the Consulate-General of France in Houston has the honor to
inform the Texas Department of Public Safety that the French authorities shall
exchange driver's licenses issued by Texas for French driver's licenses, provided
that, and for as long as, the authorities of Texas exchange French driver's licenses
for licenses issued by Texas. The exchanges of driver's licenses issued by Texas
shall be carried out in accordance with the domestic laws and regulations of the
French Republic, as follows:
1- Any holder of a valid and unexpired Class C driver's license over the
age of 18 issued by Texas may exchange that license for a Category B driver's
license issued by France without being required to take a theory test or a practical
test provided that he/she is normally resident in France. The Conditions for this
exchange shall be defined by the laws and regulations applicable in this field in the
territory of the French Republic.
2- Driver's licenses issued by Texas and exchanged in France for a French
driver's license shall be kept by the competent French authority. They shall only be
returned to their holders in exchange for the French driver's license issued to them
by that same authority.
3- The competent French authority may verify with the competent Texas
authority the authenticity of any driver's s license presented for exchange, that is to
say the existence, the category, and the validity of the driver's license in question,
as well as the identity ofits holder.
