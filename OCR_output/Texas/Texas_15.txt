JOINT STATEMENT OF INTENT
BETWEEN
THE OFFICE OF THE TEXAS GOVERNOR, UNITED STATES OF
AMERICA,
AND
THE MINISTRY FOR EUROPE AND FOREIGN AFFAIRS OF THE
FRENCH REPUBLIC



In recognition ofthe strong economic ties between the State of Texas (hereafter "Texas") and the French
Republic (hereafter "France") and in the spirit of joint economic development, the Office oft the Texas
Governor and the Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs ofFrance express under this Joint Statement
ofl Intent their willingness to encourage collaboration, increase prosperity in both regions and contribute
to the further development of favorable relations between Texas and France. The participants will
endeavor to work together to help facilitate the expansion of businesses, encourage the creation of jobs
and promote innovation through collaborative efforts, including:
I. Encouraging investment, trade, and business expansion in key industry sectors, including aerospace
and aviation, film and music, digital media, energy, biotechnology, petrochemicals, energy transition,
advanced manufacturing, corporate operations, and information and communication technologies;
II. Developing a shared understanding of challenges and opportunities related to commercial and
economic development;
III. Exploring potential areas ofcooperation that enhance prospects for economic development and job
growth in Texas and France;
IV. Continually seeking opportunities to enhance trade and investments in each region; and
V. Supporting the development and discovery of new and emerging technologies.
This. Joint Statement of Intent is solely an expression oft the participants' desires to establish a mutually
beneficial relationship and creates no legally binding rights or obligations, nor does it constitute any
financial commitment by either participant. Each participant may conduct the cooperative activities
described in this Joint Statement of Intent at its discretion and subject to the availability of funds and
personnel and to its laws and regulations. The participants will embark on these endeavors for a period
of five years after the date it is signed by both signatories. Either participant may, at its discretion, end
its participation in this. Joint Statement of Intent at any time.
