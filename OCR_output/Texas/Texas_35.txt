Memorandum of Understanding
On Trade and Mutual Economic Co-operation
between
Texas Economic Development (TxED)
and
Tianjin Commission of Foreign Trade and Economic Cooperation
(TCFTEC)
Whereas TxED and TCFTEC, in recognition of increased economic cooperation have
established an enduring and positive relationship based on many mutual interests; and
Whereas TxED and TCFTEC, wish to build on the experience of the Texas Business
Mission to China during the month of March 2003; and
Whereas TxED and TCFTEC, recognize:
The dramatic increase in the value of trade between global partners;
The overall importance of trade and investment relationships between China and the
United States, and between TCFTEC and Texas;
The increasing need for interrelationships in a global economy;
The advantages to be derived from the strengthening and broadening of mutually
beneficial trade and investment relations;
The value of close consultations and co-operation on trade issues and the need to ensure
open lines of communication and sharing of publicly available information; and
The importance of working together in matters of international trade significance.
Therefore, TxED and TCFTEC intend to work co-operatively to the fullest possible extent
consistent with law, existing treaties, and privacy requirements of their respective state or
province and nation to:
Promote and facilitate bilateral trade;
Co-ordinate activities in international development;
Promote the ongoing relationship;
Commercially promote, on an international level, of products, services and technologies of
Texas and TCFTEC; and
Develop and deliver international projects, initiatives and consulting service for exporters
and companies ready for export.
To carry out these objectives, the parties agree to promote the expansion of trade and business
relations between Texas and TCFTEC. The parties will work together to identify target sectors for
joint initiatives, including but not limited to: oil and gas exploration, petroleum refining,
petrochemicals production, high technology, bio-technology, medical field, agricultural business,
and service industries.



It is the intention of parties to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between TxED and
TCFTEC. This Memorandum of Understanding between the two parties does not represent a
commitment by either party to any terms, conditions and obligations, but does represent a
willingness to work together toward mutually beneficial trade, investment and economic
development endeavours.
