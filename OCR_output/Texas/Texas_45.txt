MUTUAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE TEXAS DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC
SAFETY AND THE REPUBLIC OF CHINA (TAIWAN) THROUGH THE MINISTRY
OF TRANSPORTATION AND COMMUNICATIONS ON THE RECIPROCAL
ISSUANCE OF DRIVER LICENSES
This agreement is made between the Texas Department of Public Safety, represented by the
Assistant Director of the Driver License Division, and the Republic of China (Taiwan) through
the Ministry of Transportation and Communications, represented by the Director General of the
Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Houston, for reciprocity in issuing driver licenses for
persons who hold a driver license issued by the Texas Department of Public Safety legally
residing in the Republic of China (Taiwan) and to Taiwanese citizens who hold a driver license
issued by Ministry of Transportation and Communications, R.O.C. (Taiwan) legally residing in
the state of Texas. The Department of Public Safety is authorized to make this agreement
pursuant to Texas Transportation Code S 521.0305.
The Parties agree that their respective governments have established comparable knowledge and
driving skills requirements which must be demonstrated by a driver license applicant under
comparable standards for testing knowledge and driving skills prior to a driver license being
issued.
The Parties agree that a holder of a valid, unexpired driver license issued by Ministry of
Transportation and Communications, R.O.C. (Taiwan) residing in Texas shall be able to
exchange for driver license issued by the Texas Department of Public Safety. The applicant will
be required to pay all requisite fees and pass a vision test, but will not be required to submit to a
driving skills test or knowledge test. Correspondingly, a holder of a valid, unexpired driver
license issued by the Texas Department of Public Safety residing in the Republic of China
(Taiwan) shall be able to exchange for driver license issued by the Republic of China (Taiwan).
The applicant will be required to pay all requisite fees and pass a vision test, but will not be
required to submit to a driving skills test or knowledge test.
The Texas Department of Public Safety and the Republic of China (Taiwan) through the
Ministry of Transportation and Communications agree on the following:
1. Provide samples of driver license (or copies thereof) issued at the time of this Agreement
and thereafter.
2. A driving skills test or medical documentation may be required in situations where the
driving examiner has cause to believe that the applicant for a driver license is physically
or mentally not qualified to operate a motor vehicle.
3. This agreement only applies to holders aged 18 or older who possess unrestricted driver
licenses. (in State of Texas: Driver License Class C; in Republic of China(Taiwan): Class
B Driver's s License)



4. This agreement does not apply to commercial driver licenses or driver licenses to operate
a motorcycle.
5. For the decision on the issuance of a driver license, the same standards, rules, and
regulations as for any resident of the state of Texas or the Republic of China (Taiwan)
shall apply.
6. The new driver license shall be issued after processing the application for a driver
license.

license issued by the respective Party.
11. Any differences arising from the interpretation or implementation of this agreement will
be resolved through discussions between the Parties.
12. This agreement shall enter into force upon the signature of both Parties, and may be
cancelled or revoked in writing by either Party at any time and for any reason with 30 day
written notice, except in the case of misuse of data or a security breach, in which case
each Party may immediately terminate the agreement.
