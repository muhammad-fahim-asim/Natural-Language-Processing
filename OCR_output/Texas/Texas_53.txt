VIETNAM-TEXAS
JOINT COMMUNIQUE
The State of Texas highly values the many visits to Texas by the official delegations of Vietnam in the past
twelve months and is most honored by the present visit of the Deputy Prime Minister of the Socialist
Republic of Vietnam (SRVN), the Honorable Vu Khoan.
The SRVN presents its compliments to the State of Texas and expresses its appreciation to the Governor of
Texas, the Honorable Rick Perry, and the members of his administration for the warm welcome and
reception extended to the Vietnamese delegation during their current stay in the capitol city of Austin.
Based on the "Statement of Cooperation" signed on June 13, 2002 by the State of Texas and Vietnam's
Ministry of Planning and Investment, on the occasion of the visit to Texas by then Deputy Prime Minister
Nguyen Manh Cam, the purpose of the present "Joint Communique" is to put on record the following
understanding and initial work schedule as agreed to on this day between the State of Texas and the SRVN:
establish joint directives for the promotion of cooperation between the State of Texas and Vietnam as
outlined in the "Statement of Cooperation" of June 13, 2002, which is outlined below:
1. Promote mutual understanding and cooperation between the respective business communities of
the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the State of Texas in the fields of trade and investment.
2. Exchange and disseminate relevant business and economic, policy, law and regulation
information from the other country/state to businesses of each party's respective country/state.
3. Notify each other of trade exhibitions and investment seminars to be held in each party's
respective country/state, and to render appropriate and timely assistance when requested.
4. Promote exchanges of visits by delegations and businesses of each party's respective
country/state and extend cooperation and assistance to the visitors so sponsored by the other
party.
5. Advise each other of firms that have interest in joint venture or investment in each party's
respective country/state.
6. Encourage business and industrial communities of their respective country/state to form a
Business Association to consist of senior business executives, public officials, and civic leaders
in order to effectively facilitate and accelerate the activities mentioned above.
as circumstances permit, Texas and SRVN will meet within the next 12 months to review the
progress made on the directives outlined in this Joint Communique. This meeting will coincide with
a business delegation consisting of Texas and Vietnamese businesses for the benefit of business-to-
business relations.
This Joint Communique is issued between the two parties and does not commit either party to any terms,
conditions and obligations, but does represent a willingness to work together toward mutually beneficial
endeavors.
Signed on this 10th day of the month of December, 2003, in Austin, Texas.
