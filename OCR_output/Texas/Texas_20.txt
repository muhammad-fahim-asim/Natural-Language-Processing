Letter of Intentto sign a memorandum of understanding on Trade,
Tourism, and Mutual Economic Cooperation between the State of Texas, as
represented by the Texas Department of Economic Development and the
Province of Manitoba, as represented by the Manitoba Department of
Industry, Trade and Mines.
Whereas Texas and Manitoba, in recognition of sharing the Mid-Continent
and Transportation Corridor, extending border-to-bordertoborder in the Trade
Continent of North America, Texas Department of Economic Development and
Manitoba Department of Industry, Trade, and Mines have established an
enduring and positive relationship based on many mutual interests; and
Whereas Texas and Manitoba wish to build on the experience of the trade
relationship initiated by their participation in the Team Canada West Trade
Mission to Texas on November 28, 2001, and
Whereas Texas and Manitoba recognize:
the dramatic increase in value of trade between all Mid-Continent Trade
and Transportation Corridor partners;
the growing overall importance of The Mid-Continent Trade and
Transportation Corridor trading relationships between Canada and the
United States, and between Texas and Manitoba;
the increasing need for interrelationships between all Mid-Continent
trading partners;
the advantages to be derived from the strengthening and broadening of
mutually beneficial Mid-Continent trade and investment relations;
the value of close consultations and co-operation on Mid-Continent
Trade and Transportation Corridor issues and the need to ensure open
lines of communication and sharing of publicly available information; and
the importance of working together and in concert with their respective
federal governments in matters of international and Mid-Continent
Corridor trade and transportation significance.
Therefore, in a spirit of promoting closer relations, Texas and Manitoba intend to
work co-operatively to the fullest possible extent consistent with law, existing
treaties, and privacy requirements of their respective state or province and
nation.



Therefore Texas' and Manitoba's primary objectives guiding their respective
activities are:
A. the commercial promotion, on an international level, of products,
services and technologies of Texas and Manitoba; and
B. The development and delivery of international projects and initiatives
for exporters and companies ready for export.
Whereas to carry out these objectives the parties agree to promote the
expansion of trade and business relations between Texas and Manitoba. The
parties will work together to identify target sectors for joint initiatives, including
but not limited to agribusiness, aerospace, component manufacturers, film,
energy, biotechnology, and information and communication technologies. The
mode of this cooperation will be set forth in the terms of a Memorandum of
Understanding to be signed at a future date.
It is the intention of parties signing below to enter into a Memorandum of
Understanding between Texas and Manitoba. This Letter of Intent is issued
pending the development and execution of a final Memorandum of
Understanding between the two parties and does not represent a commitment by
either party to any terms, conditions and obligations, but does represent a
willingness to work together toward mutually beneficial trade, transportation and
economic, development endeavors. The parties do agree to discuss the terms of
a Memorandum of Understanding within 60 days of the date of this letter to
explore and further define specific steps toward carrying out the objectives of this
Letter of Agreement.
Executed in Dallas, Texas this 28th day of November 2001.
