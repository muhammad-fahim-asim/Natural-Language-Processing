MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
This Memorandum of Understanding is effective as of June 12, 2018 ("effective date")
BETWEEN:
THE DEPARTMENT OF ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT AND TRADE
THE GOVERNMENT OF ALBERTA
("ALBERTA")
And
OFFICE OF THE TEXAS SECRETARY OF STATE
("TEXAS")
Alberta and Texas (collectively, the "Participants") seek to facilitate and implement opportunities for a long
term collaborative relationship to advance the export development, investment attraction, solution
oriented research, commercialization and global competitiveness of the energy sector, advanced
technologies, and trade and investment in each respective jurisdiction.
Thel Participants share a common commitment to the pursuit of economic progress and prosperity.
The Participants are both recognized as centers of excellence and innovation in research and
development, agriculture, and energy within their respective countries.
The Participants are economically interdependent, share integrated supply chains, and enjoy al bilateral
trading relationship in excess of C$13.81 billion and US$10.6 billion (2017).
The Participants recognize the wish to enhance and strengthen their efforts to pursue common goals and
objectives in relation to stimulating economic growth.
This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") sets out the intentions and understandings of the
Participants: as follows:
1. Scope of Collaboration
The Participants, each working with other appropriate departments and agencies in their respective
governments, commit to ongoing dialogue and agree to work together to:
Endeavor to develop a mutual understanding of shared challenges and opportunities as they
relate to commercial and economic development;
ii. Explore potential areas of collaboration that enhance prospects for economic development and
job growth in Alberta and Texas;
iii. Seek opportunities to enhance trade and investment and to undertake collaborative innovations
and research and development around emerging technologies between. Alberta and Texas;
iv. Work with their respective federal governments to enhance regional and bi-national
competitiveness through the promotion of open trade practices and through the prevention and
elimination of tradel barriers;
V. Demonstrate sub-national leadership by jointly identifying areas of common interest that either
Party may! bring forward to the attention of the respective federal governments.
2. MOU Management Committee
Thel Participants will appoint an MOU Management Committee consisting of two senior officials from each
jurisdiction to oversee the implementation of the MOU.
Thel Management Committee may consider opportunities in the following sectors:
Oil and gas
Renewable energy
Agriculture and Agri-food
Life Sciences
Digital technology
Emerging technology
3. Costs
Each Participant will bear its own costs resulting from its participation in activities undertaken in the
furtherance of the objectives of this MOU.



4. Nature of the Memorandum of Understanding
This MOU serves only as a statement of intention of the Participants and is not intended to create any
legally binding obligations nor be construed as agreement on any matters mentioned. This MOU does not
create any relationship of employment, agency, partnership orj joint venturel between the Participants.
5. Exchange of Information: and Confidentiality
Participants will keep each other informed of progress and results in areas of common interest so as to
ensure that the activities of the Participants are coordinated. Participants recognize and acknowledge that
by the nature of their respective operations they will be involved with not only each other, but also with
third parties directly or indirectly, and that as a result of such involvement, confidential or classified
information may be generated or obtained from such third parties. Nothing in this MOU will be construed
as requiring the Participants to disclose to one another any confidential or classified information
generated or obtained. Participants will each have the right to place any restrictions, limitations and
conditions deemed appropriate upont the communications and cooperation contemplated by this MOU.
Texas acknowledges that this MOU, including the names of Participants and all content, may be subject
to disclosure pursuant to the Freedom of Information. and Protection of Privacy Act (Alberta) or by Alberta
if requested to table the MOUI before the Albertal Legislature.
Nothing in this MOU alters any obligations under Chapter 552 of the Texas Government Code (the "PIA"),
as interpreted by the Texas Office of the Attorney General's Open Records Division and the judiciary

7.Term
This MOU will be in effect for three (3) years from the date of signing, unless otherwise extended or
terminatedi in accordance with the provisions of this MOU.
8. Amendment
Either Participant may make a requesti in writing for an amendment to this MOU. Any amendment may
only be madel by mutual written agreement signed byl both Participants.
9. Termination
Either Participant mayt terminate thel MOU at any time, without cause, by providing thirty (30) days written
notice to the other Party.
