STATEMENT OF MUTUALCOOPERATION
between
the' Texas Economic Development and Tourism Office,
Office of the Texas Governor, United States of America,
and
KOTRA, the Korea Trade-Investment Promotion Agency
The Texas Economic Development and Tourism Office, Office of the Texas Governor, and the Korea Trade-
Investment Promotion Agency (KOTRA), will strive to engage in the following activities under this
Statement ofMutual Cooperation (SMC) for the purpose of helping small and medium-sized enterprises
(SMES) expandi into each party's market, promoting innovation through collaboration andj joint research, and
facilitating the creation ofj jobs.
1. ACTIVITIES
1) Encouraging investment activities and business expansion in Korea and Texas through, among
other approaches, providing foreign SMES with information about, and introducing them to,
agencies, institutions, and persons who can provide support;
2) Disseminating program information among relevant local and regional stakeholders; and
3) Participating and cooperating in economic development events, such as seminars and business
support programs.
2. DURATION OF OPERATION
This SMC will be applied from April 27, 2023, until March 31, 2025, and may be renewed by the parties.
3. FINALI PROVISIONS
1) This SMC is subject to the prevailing laws and regulations ofthe State of Texas, the United States
ofAmerica, and the Republic of Korea.
2) This SMC is solely an expression of the parties' desire to establish a mutually beneficial
relationship and creates no legally binding rights or obligations and does not constitute any
financial commitmentl by either party.
3) Any unspecified matters or differences that arise with respect to this SMC will be settled through
consultations betweent the Office ofthe Texas Governor and KOTRA.
4) This SMC does not eliminate pre-existing arrangements or statements of mutual cooperation
between the Office of the Texas Governor and KOTRA or the Republic of Korea, or those
concluded between their other agencies, bureaus, or divisions.
5) Each party may, in accordance with their respective laws and regulations, conduct the cooperative
activities outlined in this SMC at their discretion and subject to the availability of funds and
personnel.



This Statement of Mutual Cooperation was signed on April 27, 2023, in Austin, Texas, United States of
America in duplicate in Korean and English, with each version being equally valid for the recognition oft the
above articles.
