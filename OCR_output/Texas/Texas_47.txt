RECIPROCALAGREEMENT BETWEEN THE TEXAS DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC
SAFETY AND THE FEDERAL MINISTRY OF TRANSPORT, BUILDING AND URBAN
AFFAIRS OF THE FEDERALI REPUBLIC OF GERMANY FOR DRIVER LICENSE
RECIPROCITY
THIS INFORMAL AGREEMENT is made between the Texas Department of Public Safety,
represented by and the Federal Ministry ofTransport, Building and Urban Affairs of the
Federal Republic of Germany, represented by the competent Head of Division, to facilitate
the ease with which citizens from the US State ofTexas, residing in Germany, and German
citizens, residing in the State ofTexas, can obtain their driver license.
Each party acknowledges that the other has established comparable knowledge and driving
skills requirements which an applicant must demonstrate before being issued a driver license.
Each party is also satisfied that the other has established comparable standards to test the
knowledge and driving skills of an applicant for a driver license.
The parties hereto agree that a citizen of the US State of Texas, residing in Germany and
having a valid driver license ofthe State ofTexas, will be able to exchange that license for a
German driver license at the nominal conversion fee after having passed an eyesight test,
without, however, being required to take the knowledge and skills test, and without further
driver's education. Correspondingly, German citizens residing in the State ofTexas and
having a valid German driver license will be able to exchange that license for a license ofthe
State of Texas at the nominal conversion fee after having passed an eyesight test, without,
however, being required to take the knowledge and skills test and without further driver's
education.
The State of Texas and the Federal Republic of Germany agree to the following:
1. Provide samples of driver licenses (or copies thereof) being in circulation at the date of
this Agreement and any versions which may be introduced in the future, a definition of all
driver license classes, endorsements, and restrictions;
2. Require a practical driving test and/or medical documentation attesting to physical or
mental capabilities only in situations where the driver license examiner has cause to
believe that the person is not physically or mentally qualified to operate a motor vehicle;
3. This Agreement applies only to holders of unrestricted, non-commercial driver licenses
aged 18 or older and does not apply to making application to operate a commercial motor
