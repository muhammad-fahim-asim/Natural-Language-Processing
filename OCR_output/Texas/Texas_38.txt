Memorandum of Understanding
On Trade and Mutual Economic Co-operation
Between the Texas Department of
Economic Development (TXED)
And the
Suzhou Municipal Foreign Trade and
Economic Cooperation (SUMFTEC)
Whereas TxED and SUMFTEC, in recognition of increased economic cooperation have
established an enduring and positive relationship based on many mutual interests; and
Whereas TxED and SuMFTEC wish to build on the experience ofthe Texas Business
Mission to China during the month ofl May 2002; and
Whereas TxED and SuMFTEC recognize:
 The dramatic increase in the value of trade between global partners;
e The overall importance of trade and investment relationships between China and the
United States, and between Suzhou and Texas;
The increasing need for interrelationships in a global economy;
The advantages to be derived from the strengthening and broadening of mutually
beneficial trade and investment relations;
The value of close consultations and co-operation on trade issues and the need to ensure
open lines of communication and sharing of publicly available information; and
The importance or working together in matters of international trade significance.
Therefore, in a spirit of promoting closer relations, TxED and SUMFTEC intend to work CO-
operatively to the fullest possible extent consistent with law, existing treaties, and privacy
requirements of their respective state or province and nation to:
Promote and facilitate bilateral trade
Promote and encourage co-operation in tourism
Coordinate activities in international development
Promote the ongoing relationship
Therefore TxED and SUMFTEC 's primary objectives guiding their respective activities are:



A. The commercial promotion, on an international level, of products, services and
technologies of Texas and Suzhou; and
B. The development and delivery of international projects and initiatives for exporters
and companies ready for export.
Whereas, to carry out these objectives, the parties agree to promote the expansion of trade and
business relations between Texas and Suzhou. The parties will work together to identify target
sectors for joint initiatives, including but not limited to: oil and gas exploration, petroleum
refining, petrochemicals production, high-technology, bio-technology, medical field, agricultural
business, and service industries.
Iti is the intention of parties to enter into a Memorandum ofUnderstanding between TxED and
SUMFTEC. This Memorandum ofUnderstanding between the two parties does not represent a
commitment by either party to any terms, conditions and obligations, but does represent a
willingness to work together toward mutually beneficial trade, transportation and economic
development endeavours.
