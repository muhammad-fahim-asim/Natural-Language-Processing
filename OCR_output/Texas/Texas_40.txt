Memorandum of Understanding
On Trade, Tourism, and Mutual Economic Co-operation
Between
The Office of the Governor of Texas,
Economic Development & Tourism Division (EDT)
And
The Yangzhou Municipal City (YMC)
Whereas EDT and YMC, in recognition of increased economic cooperation have established an
enduring and positive relationship based on many mutual interests; and
Whereas EDT and YMC, wish to build on the experience of the Texas Business Mission to China
during the month of April 2005; and
Whereas EDT and YMC, recognize:
The dramatic increase in the value of trade between global partners;
The overall importance of trade and investment relationships between China and the United
States, and between Yangzhou and Texas;
The increasing need for interrelationships in a global economy;
The advantages to be derived from the strengthening and broadening of mutually beneficial trade
and investment relations;
The value of close consultations and co-operation on trade issues and the need to ensure open
lines of communication and sharing of publicly available information; and
The importance or working together in matters of international trade significance; and
Whereas to carry out these objectives, the two parties agree to promote the expansion of trade and
business relations between the State of Texas and Yangzhou City. The parties will work together to
identify target sectors for joint initiatives, including but not limited to the following sectors: oil and gas
exploration, petroleum refining, petrochemicals production, high-technology, bio-technology, medical
field, agricultural business, and service industries.
Now therefore, in a spirit of promoting closer relations, EDT and Yangzhou, intend to work CO-
operatively to the fullest possible extent consistent with law, existing treaties, and privacy requirements
of their respective state or province and nation to:



Promote and facilitate bilateral trade;
Promote and encourage co-operation in tourism;
Coordinate activities in international development;
Promote their ongoing relationship.
Therefore EDT and YMC's primary objectives guiding their respective activities are:
A. The commercial promotion, on an international level, of products, services and
technologies of' Texas and Yangzhou City: and
B. The development and delivery of international projects and initiatives for exporters and
companies ready for export.
It is the intention of parties to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding between EDT and Yangzhou.
This Memorandum ofUnderstanding between the two parties does not represent a commitment by either
party to any terms, conditions and obligations, but does represent a willingness to work together toward
mutually beneficial trade, transportation and economic development endeavours.
This agreement shall enter into force on the date of its signature by the representatives of both parties
concerned and will remain valid for two years.
It will be extended automatically, one year at a time, unless written notice is given by one party to the
other for termination of the agreement not less than six months before the next date of expiration. The
agreement will be executed on a best effort basis and is not intended to create any legal or financial
obligation for either institution.
Executed in Shanghai, People's Republic of China, as ofthe 2 of April, 2005,
