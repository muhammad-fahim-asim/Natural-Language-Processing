Memorandum of Understanding
Between
The State of Texas
Of the United States of America
And
The Shimane Prefecture
Of Japan
Whereas, Mr. Nobuyoshi Sumita, the Governor of Shimane Prefecture, Japan, visited the State of
Texas. from January 7 through 11, 2004, upon the official invitation from Mr. Rick Perry, the
Governor of the State of Texas, USA, and;
Whereas, Governor Sumita was warmly received by Governor Perry, Secretary of State Geoff
Connor, and representatives and citizens of the State of Texas, and Governor Sumita and
Governor Perry discussed and conferred together along with staff and interested parties, and;
Whereas, the Shimane Prefecture and the State of Texas intend to promote Industry and to create
new jobs in each region respectively;
Now, therefore, the Shimane Prefecture and the State of Texas conclude and agree to the
following points:
1. The Shimane Prefecture and the State of Texas agree to build a new Co-Creative
relationship based on Industrial and Advanced Technologies, such as Environmental,
Energy, Nano-Bio, Medical, etc.
2. As the first step of the Co-Creation, Shimane Prefecture and the State of Texas intend to
promote and encourage the exchange of personnel between academic and/or research
institutions! located in each region.
3. Shimane Prefecture and the State of Texas intend to establish a Task Force to formulate a
plan that will be submitted to the Governors by the end of Summer, 2004. This plan will
present concrete recommendations on the Co-Creative goals. The Task Force will be lead
in Shimane Prefecture by Dr. Tadashi Sasaki and in the State of Texas by Dr. Zvi Yaniv.
4. The Task Force will be open to ad hoc collaboration proposals that could be proposed from
time to time and will provide recommendations to both parties on how to proceed on a case-
by-case basis.
5. Governor Sumita invites Governor Perry to visit Shimane Prefecture during Fall, 2004, to
sign a final agreement of Co-Creation based on the recommendations of the Task Force.
6. This agreement is issued between the two parties on a best efforts basis and is not
intended to create any legal and/or financial obligation for either partyl but does represent an
intent to work together toward mutually beneficial endeavors.
7. In witness of the above, this agreement is executed in four official copies, two in English
and two in Japanese, by authorized representatives of each party and will retain one copy
each."
Upon confirming the above points, the Shimane Prefecture and the State of Texas affirm their
understanding by signing below on January 8, 2004.
