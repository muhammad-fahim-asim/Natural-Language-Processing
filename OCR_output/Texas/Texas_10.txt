COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE SECRETARIAT OF
INFRASTRUCTURE, COMMUNICATIONS AND TRANSPORTATION, AND
FEDERAL TOLL ROADS AND BRIDGES AND RELATED SERVICES AND
THE GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF TEXAS OF THE UNITED STATES
OF AMERICA, FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A SECOND TWIN
STRUCTURE OF THE INTERNATIONAL BRIDGE LOCATED IN OJINAGA -
PRESIDIO OVER THE RIO GRANDE RIVER, OR THE RIO BRAVO
The Secretariat of Infrastructure, Communications and Transportation ("the
Secretariat") and Federal Toll Roads and Bridges and Related Services
(CAPUFE), and the Government of the State of Texas of the United States of
America, through the Texas Department of Transportation (TXDOT), referred to
jointly as "the Parties".
DESIRING to perform all the necessary tasks and put forward their best efforts
to cooperate mutually in the construction of the second structure of the
International Bridge located in Ojinaga I Presidio, over the Rio Grande River or
Rio Bravo;
TAKING INTO ACCOUNT that the Parties possess all the technical, human,
economic, and material resources, as well as the experience and the appropriate
staffing to fulfill the objective of this Agreement;
THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:
ARTICLE 1
OBJECTIVE
The Parties shall perform in the territory of their respective countries, the work
associated with the construction of a second twin structure, one and a half spans
on the Mexican side, and five and a half spans on the United States side which
shall connect to the second structure of the Ojinaga-Presidio International Bridge
(hereinafter referred to as THE PROJECT).
In view of the previous, the Parties agree to execute THE PROJECT until its
completion, for which the United States possesses a Presidential Permit issued
by Acting Assistant Secretary of State for Oceans and International
Environmental and Scientific Affairs, Judith G. Garber, to the State of Texas on
May 30, 2017, and the Mexican party with the Title of Concession granted to
CAPUFE on March 18, 2003. The Parties agree to carry out THE PROJECT
according to the characteristics, specifications, project plans, schedule and
estimates approved by the Comision Internacional de Limites y Aguas of México
and the United States International Boundary and Water Commission, in July
2017, attached to and made a part of this Agreement as Appendix "A",
Executive Project, as an integral part of this Agreement.
It is expressly established that the Parties are obligated to only perform the work
corresponding to THE PROJECT in each side of the border, in accordance with
the characteristics, specifications, project plans, schedule and estimates in

ARTICLE 2
COST OF WORK
The total cost of THE PROJECT shall be the resulting from the bid amount for
THE PROJECT under each Party's letting process.
CAPUFE shall fund and construct the approach ramp located within the United
Mexican States territory and shall also construct the structure including the
substructure, superstructure, the parapet, lighting and all other installations and
additions in the extreme south end of the bridge.
TXDOT shall fund and construct the approach ramp located within the territory of
the United States of America and shall also construct the structure including the
substructure, superstructure, the parapet, lighting and all other installations and
additions in the extreme north end of the bridge.
ARTICLE: 3
CONSTRUCTION OF COMMON SPAN
The Parties agree to cooperate on the construction of Span No. 6 which is the
common span of THE PROJECT.
The common span is the portion of THE PROJECT between bent 6 on the United
States side and bent 7 on the Mexican side. Included in the common span section
are the parapet, lighting, monument and all other installations and accessories
that the Parties agree should be included in this portion of the span.
CAPUFE agrees to perform the corresponding work mentioned above for the
common span of THE PROJECT, which shall unite the section located on the
Mexican side with the section located on the United States side.
The Parties agree that if there is a cost increase during construction in the total
amount of the common span, it shall be jointly reviewed and approved by the
Parties' Supervisory Engineers, and where applicable, finally approved by the
Parties Designated Representatives.
ARTICLE 4
COST OF COMMON SPAN
The total cost of the common span, agreed to in this. Agreement, is the bid amount
for the common span at letting by CAPUFE and which cost the Parties shall share
equally. TXDOT's 50% (fifty percent) share of the total amount shall be paid to
CAPUFE upon completion of the common span mentioned as described in Article
3.

ARTICLE 5
AVAILABILITY OF THE PROPERTY AND ADMINISTRATIVE DOCUMENTS
The Parties agree to conduct the necessary coordination with their respective
immigration authorities to obtain free transit within the area of the work that is the
subject of this Agreement, for the time necessary to conduct the work in both
sides of each territory, including the delivery of materials and equipment and
access for workers, and technical and administrative personnel employed by the
Parties. Likewise, the Parties shall provide each other a list of all authorized
personnel for the construction of THE PROJECT with a reasonable time ahead
of the beginning of construction.
The work zone shall be enclosed, barricaded, and shall be made known to the
authorities of each country, with the understanding that the boundary line that
divides both countries continues to exist, as well as the jurisdiction of each
country over their respective territories. Each Party shall cover the cost for
establishing and maintaining free transit in their respective zones.
ARTICLE 6
SUPERVISION
The Parties shall assign, at their cost, an Engineer in Charge who will be
responsible for the performing the functions as described in Appendix B, which
is attached to and made a part of this Agreement.
ARTICLE 7
LOGBOOK
The Parties, through their Engineer in Charge, shall use and continually update
a logbook to include the most important daily matters and activities taking place
during the execution of this Agreement. This logbook must remain at the worksite,
so that requests for review by the Parties can take place at the worksite without
the need to remove the logbook from the work area.
The logbook shall at a minimum contain the requirements listed in Appendix B.

Each Party's Designated Representative should have the authority to act on
behalf of and represent each respective Party on everything related to this
Agreement. This broad and necessary authority shall be expressed in writing in

ARTICLE9
COMPLETION AND ACCEPTANCE OF WORK
Once the Parties consider THE PROJECT to be completed, the Engineer in
Charge from each Party shall determine the termination, and once it is duly
completed in accordance with the characteristics, specifications, project plans,
schedule, and estimates approved by The Parties, they shall issue a Certificate
of Completion where the completion of THE PROJECT will be noted (the
Certificate of Completion).
In the event that during inspection by each Party's Engineer in Charge it is
determined that outstanding items exist in accordance with the characteristics,
specifications, project plans, schedule, and estimates approved by The Parties,
without these affecting the functionality of the international Bridge, each Party's
Engineer in Charge shall issue a Certificate of Delivery that states the completion
of THE PROJECT, as well as the outstanding items and the deadline for
completion of such outstanding items.
ACCEPTANCE of the work, either total or partial, shall be conducted according
to the characteristics, specifications, project plans, schedule, and estimates as
established in this Agreement and its appendices. The Parties reserve the right
to demand that any unfinished or improperly performed be completed or redone.
The Parties may have partial deliveries of individual tasks related to THE
PROJECT before the established final completion when in their judgement the
tasks are completed and suitable for use.
The Parties shall receive the works related to THE PROJECT and the
corresponding Certificate shall be drawn up jointly.
The Certificate of Delivery shall become part of the documents considered for the
completion of construction of THE PROJECT and consequently, the Engineers
in Charge should proceed to issue a Certificate of Completion. The Certificate of
Delivery shall include the requirements listed in Appendix B.
The Certificate of Delivery must contain the signatures of the Designated
Representatives of the Parties as well as those of the Engineers in Charge.

ARTICLE 10
THE PARTIES' RESPONSIBILITIES
The Parties agree that the materials and equipment used for the execution of
THE PROJECT under this Agreement, shall comply with the quality standards
established by each Party shall be carried out in accordance with the previously
approved characteristics, specifications, project plans, schedule and estimates
as described in Appendix "A" of this Agreement. Each Party agrees that in the
event of flaws, defects, harm, and damages caused to third parties the
contractors hired by each party will take responsibility and shall assume the risk
in which case the guarantee granted for compliance with the Agreement
established by each of the Parties in their respective bridge spans shall be
effective.
The Parties agree that they will not be liable for damages that may arise as a
result of unforeseen causes, especially those that cause the suspension of the
activities contemplated under this Agreement, and once the causes that caused
such suspension have been overcome, the agreed activities will be resumed.
ARTICLE 11
TOLLS
CAPUFE shall prepare the necessary agreements with the Mexican Federal
Entity (the State of Chihuahua) and shall establish along with the Secretariat the
toll for vehicles and pedestrians for the right to cross from Mexico into the United
States. Crossing from the United States to Mexico shall remain toll free.
ARTICLE 12
MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITY
After the completion and acceptance date of THE PROJECT, each Party shall
operate, preserve, and maintain the portion of the bridge within the territory of its
country in good operational condition.

ARTICLE 14
FORTUITOUS EVENTS OR FORCE MAJEURE
In the event that the Parties are not able to fulfill the obligations of this Agreement
due to a Fortuitous Event or Force Majeure Event, they shall proceed as follows:
For the purposes of this Agreement, Fortuitous Events are natural phenomena
and, in general, those in which man's will do not directly intervene, including, but
not limited to: storms, cyclones, floods, lighting, earthquakes, snow storms, fire
and explosions.
For the purposes of this Agreement, Force Majeure Events are those in which
man's will can directly or indirectly intervene, including, but not limited to: acts of
war, civil disturbances, riots, uprisings, sabotage, sit-ins, acts off terrorism, strikes,
commercial embargoes against a country, transportation. accidents, either
maritime, railroad, land, or air, acts or omissions on behalf of the governmental
authority that were not voluntarily solicited or promoted by the affected Party, nor
caused by the non-compliance of the obligations of this Agreement or any
applicable law. Force Majeure includes the inability to timely and properly obtain
any permit needed so that the corresponding Party fulfills the obligations of this
Agreement, only when said Party has fulfilled each and all requirements
necessary for obtaining the permit according to the terms of applicable laws and
therefore carrying out all relevant actions.
In any case, the affected Party must notify the other Party within 72 (seventy-two)
hours after the existence of the Fortuitous Event or Force Majeure Event, for the
effects that may be applicable.
Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement or applicable laws, the Parties
shall not be responsible for the non-compliance of their obligations, as long as a
Fortuitous Event or Force Majeure Event has occurred or remains in force and
beginning at the moment in which the affected Party has notified the other Party
according to this Article.
For no reason, the occurrence of a Fortuitous Event or Force Majeure Event shall
be considered if any Party has contributed directly or indirectly to its existence.

DISPUTE RESOLUTION
The Parties agree to negotiate in good faith, directly and without a mediator in a
neutral location, be it El Paso, Texas, or in Mexico City, in a timely manner, and
in good faith regarding all disputes, litigation, conflicts or claims that could emerge

between the Parties based on interpretation, compliance, and execution of this
Agreement, with the objective of reaching an amicable arrangement. Any
agreement thati is reached between the Parties without the use of a mediator shall
be documented in writing and signed within a 30 (thirty) day period from the day
the meeting(s) took place, unless the Parties have agreed in writing to extend the
time to prepare and execute a written agreement resolving the matter. This
agreement shall be titled Memorandum of Agreement". Additionally, in the case
that a written agreement cannot be reached without a mediator, the Parties shall
proceed using a mediator as described below.
In case that the Parties use a mediator to resolve any dispute related to this
Agreement, the following guidelines shall be used:
The non-binding mediation shall take place in some neutral location, be it El Paso,
Texas or Mexico City. Any agreement that is reached between the Parties using
a mediator shall be documented in writing and signed within a 30 (thirty) day
period from the day the mediation session(s) took place, unless the Parties have
agreed in writing to extend the time to prepare and execute a written agreement
resolving the matter. This agreement shall be titled "Memorandum of Agreement."
Additionally, in the case that a written agreement cannot be reached with a
mediator, the Parties shall proceed using an alternate mediation process as
described below.
Each Party will select one mediator. Those two mediators so selected shall then
select a third mediator who has no interest or commitment to either Party. The
three mediators shall then begin a non-binding mediation process. The mediation
shall take place in some neutral location, be it El Paso, Texas or Mexico City. The
Parties shall evenly share the costs of the third mediator, and each shali pay for
the costs of its own selected mediator. Any agreement that is reached between
the Parties using an alternate mediation process shall be documented in writing
by the Parties at the end of the session. The final details of any agreed
arrangement shall be made in writing and the Parties should sign it within a 30
(thirty) day period from the day the mediation session(s) took place, unless the
Parties have agreed in writing to extend the time to prepare and execute a written
agreement resolving the matter. This agreement shall be titled "Memorandum of
Agreement".
The Parties agree that, in the event of not reaching any agreement in good faith,
directly after having exhausted the instances provided for in the preceding
paragraphs, the Parties will submit to the Courts of Mexico City, or where
appropriate, to the Courts of Travis County, Texas.

APPENDIX B
Engineer in Charge Responsibilities
1. General: The Engineer in Charge for each Party shall:
a) Supervise the execution and progress of THE PROJECT in its entirety.
The Engineer in Charge shall also report on irregularities encountered
during construction on the portion of THE PROJECT that is being
constructed by the other party.
b) Maintain THE PROJECT work log/record.
c) Ensure that the work is performed in accordance with this Agreement
and the previously approved characteristics, specifications, project
plans, schedule and estimates associated with THE PROJECT, as well
as any other requirements or special provisions contained in that
Party's construction contract.
d) Maintain plan updates.
e) Report on the progress and execution of THE PROJECT during weekly
construction meetings.
f) Confirm the completion of the work in this Agreement.
g) Provide a general overview on the terms and the manner in which the
work was executed, and;
h) Assist in the delivery and acceptance of THE PROJECT and sign the
corresponding documentation.
2. Daily Log: The Engineer in Charge for each Party shall ensure that the
Daily Log meets the following requirements:
a) The original pages and copies shall always be numbered and
reference the existing Agreement.
b) One original is prepared for both CAPUFE and TXDOT, and atl least
2 (two) copies.
c) The copies are detachable, whereas the originals are not
detachable; and
d) The content of each entry should specify number, classification,
date, description of the matter, and additionally, location, cause,
solution, prevention, economic consequence, responsibility (if any),
date addressed, as well as a reference to the issue it addresses.
