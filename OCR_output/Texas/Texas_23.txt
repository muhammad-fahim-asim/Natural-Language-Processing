Memorandum of Cooperation
Between
The State ofTexas, The United States of America
And
Tianjin Municipality, The People's Republic of China
The Tianjin Municipality, the People's Republic of China and the State of Texas, United
States of America (the Parties) recognizing the importance of developing and expanding ties of
cooperation through business, trade, investment, tourism, education, research and development, have
decided to establish "Tianjin Municipality and State of Texas Joint Working Group" (hereinafter
referred to as the "Working Group").
The Parties acknowledge the importance of commercial, scientific and cultural connections
to their respective economies. They undertake to expand existing links by exploring mutually
beneficial collaborative opportunities with a goal of increasing the exchange of information and
increasing trade and investment flows.
Consequently, the Parties have reached the following understanding of cooperation:
to explore the potential for enhancing business networks, alliances and collaborative projects
between their respective government agencies, industry organizations, and educational and
research institutes that will aim to deliver benefits to the public good ofTianjin Municipality
and the State ofTexas; and
to support ongoing staff collaboration to increase knowledge, enhance expertise and develop
best practices in relation to economic and industry development, policy development,
training, and project approval.
The forms of joint initiatives and collaborations between the Parties may include but are not limited
to:
regular trade and investment delegations;
research projects between academic institutions;
development of partnerships exploring joint industry and economic development initiatives;
collaborative industry-related training initiatives;
collaboration between government agencies and the private sectors of Tianjin Municipality
and US State ofTexas to increase industry-related competency levels and experience; and
promotion of industry-related events.
The Parties respectively designate the Tianjin Foreign Affairs Office and Office of the Governor of
Texas, Economic Development and Tourism Division as representatives of the business community
to join the Working Group and assume the functions and responsibilities of the Working Group



secretariat for daily communication, contact, information exchange, business project promotion and
business activities organization.
This Memorandum ofCooperation:
will commence on the date ofthe final execution;
may be modified by agreement in writing by the Parties;
can be terminated by either Party with written notice;
is a statement of intent of cooperation between the Parties, but is not intended as a legally
binding document and does not create legal obligations.
Any dispute arising out of the interpretation or implementation of this Memorandum of
shall be settled amicably by consultation and negotiation in good faith between the Parties. Cooperation
