Based on the "Statement of Cooperation" signed on June 13, 2002 by the State of Texas and Vietnam's Ministry Manh
of Planning and Investment, on the occasion of the visit to Texas by then Deputy Prime Minister Nguyen
Cam, the purpose of the present "Joint Communique II" is to put on record the following understanding as agreed
to on this day between the State of' Texas and the SRVN:
Establish joint directives for the cooperation between the State of Texas and Vietnam as outlined in the
"Statement of Cooperation" of June 13, 2002, outlined below:
Promote mutual understanding and cooperation between the respective business communities of the
Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the State of Texas in the fields of trade and investment.
Promote exchange of visits by business and official delegations of each party and to render
appropriate and timely assistance when requested
As circumstances permit, Texas and SRVN will meet within the next 12 months to review the progress made on
these directives. This meeting will coincide with a business delegation consisting of Texas and Vietnamese
businesses for the benefit of business-to-business relations.
This Joint Communique II is issued between the two parties and does not commit either party to any terms,
conditions and obligations, but does express a willingness by' Texas and the SRVN to work together on mutually
beneficial endeavors.
