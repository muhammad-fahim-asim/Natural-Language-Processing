The Indiana Department of Education, United States of America and the
Department of International and Cross-Strait Education, Ministry of Education,
Republic of China (Taiwan), hereinafter referred to as "the Parties", agree to
enter into a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) on education, in order to
strengthen education linkages between Indiana and Taiwan.
The Parties have: reached the following Agreement:
Article 1: Purposes
The Parties will pursue the formulation and implementation of work plans
through which they may achieve the following purposes:
to maintain and enhance bilateral co-operation in educational matters;
to promote mutual understanding; and
to realize common benefits and collaboration.
Article 2: Activities
The Parties agree to pursue, through this MOA, these activities:
(1) The promotion ofj joint projects between educators in Indiana and educators
in Taiwan.
(2) The encouragement of joint and collaborative efforts in the language
teaching field. These efforts may include assistance with the recruitment of
language teachers, exchange of curricular materials, and technical
assistance.
1



(3) Encouragement of educational and academic exchanges between
individuals and educational institutions in the State of Indiana and in
Taiwan.
Article 3: Coordination and Implementation
(1) Officials Responsible for Implementation
Responsibility for coordinating activities and communication concerning this
MOA will lie with the Superintendent of Public Instruction of the State of
Indiana, and with the Director General ofthe Department of International and
Cross-Strait Education of the Ministry of Education in Taiwan, and their
designated representatives.
(2) Working Groups
Each of the Parties will form a non-official Working Group for implementing
this MOA. The Working Groups will communicate with each other, as needed,
to implement work plans and will meet with each other in person or using
online electronic media at least once a year.
(3) Work Plans
Each of the Parties will formulate an annual work plan to coordinate the work
it is undertaking pursuant to this MOA. The nature and priority status of
specific areas of cooperation, the types of activities, and the facilitation and
promotion of the annual work plans being implemented under this MOA will
be determined by the Working Groups.
(4) Involvement of Other Organizations
The Parties will promote, facilitate, and encourage, as appropriate, the
development of direct contacts with and between related organizations, in
association with co-operative language teaching projects.
Article 4: Costs
Any questions regarding the expenses associated with any activity under this
MOA will be settled by negotiation between the participants involved prior to
the start oft that activity. Unless otherwise agreed between the Parties, each
2



party participating in any cooperative activity or initiative will be responsible
for all costs of its participation.
Article 5: Liability
When implementing any cooperative activities under this MOA, each of the
Parties will be responsible for its own acts or omissions and any consequences
thereof and will not be responsible for any acts or omissions ofthe other, nor
for any consequences thereof.
Article 6: Intellectual Property Rights
This MOA does not affect the intellectual property rights of either of the
Parties. Where it is foreseeable that questions related to intellectual property
rights might arise in connection with some proposed co-operative activities, the
Parties, in accordance with their national laws, will agree in advance as to the
effective protection and allocation of those intellectual property rights.
Article 7: General Clauses
This MOA will take effect on the date of the last signature by both ofthe
Parties and will remain in effect for a period of four years. This MOA may be
extended or amended with the written consent of both of the Parties.
This MOA may be terminated by either of the Parties at any time by giving
three months' written notice to the other. Any activities agreed upon and
initiated prior to the receipt of a termination notice may be completed in
accordance with the related activity agreement. The Parties may, however,
mutually agree to terminate an activity early as the result of a termination
notice.
In witness of the above, the undersigned duly authorized persons have signed
this Memorandum of Agreement.
There are two original copies in the Chinese language and two original
copies in the English language, the texts in each language having equal
standing.
