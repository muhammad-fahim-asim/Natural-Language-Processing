MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
ON AGREEMENT OF FRIENDSHIP AND MUTUAL COOPERATION
BETWEEN
THE INDIANA STATE GOVERNMENT, THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
AND
THE STATE OF KARNATAKA GOVERNMENT, INDIA
The State of Indiana of the United States of America, and the State ofl Karnataka Government ofl India (hereafter
"the Parties"), inspired by the strong desire to strengthen the ties of friendship and cooperation existing between
them,hereby express the goals ofincreasing economic, educational, and culturalties, thereby contributing to the
further development of favorable relations between the United States of America and India. Pursuant to the
prevailing laws and regulations in the Parties' respective states, and founded upon the principles of equality and
mutual benefit, the Parties declare that:
I.
The Parties shall strive to establish a close relationshipbased on respect, trust and mutual
benefit.
II.
The Parties shall cooperate to enhance the existing relationshipin those fields recognized by
the Parties to be mutually beneficial, including, in particular, economic, educational, and
cultural exchanges.
III. In the interest of development of both states, the Parties shall work to promote and intensify
economic cooperation between the State of Indiana and the State of Karnataka and between
their respective private sectors.
IV. The Parties share the intention to cooperate in the following areas and industries: economic
development and investment, workforce development, academic cooperation, information and
communications technologies, advanced manufacturing and materials, life sciences,
agriculture and agriculture technology, automotive (including smart/autonomous
transportation), and aerospace and aviation.
V.
The Parties will, on a regular basis, inform and consult with one another on matters of
common interest that represent opportunities for mutual benefit, consistent with this
Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU"), including a formation of a working group made
up of stakeholders chosen by the Parties.The foregoing working group will coordinate the
implementation of this MOU, as appropriate, with other commitments between entities in
Indiana and the State of Karnataka.
This memorandum of understanding is solely an expression of the Parties' desire to establish a mutually
beneficial relationship and creates no legally binding rights or obligations.
Signed inBengaluru, State ofl Karnataka, India on October 30, 2017 in recognition ofthe above articles.
