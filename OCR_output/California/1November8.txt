MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
TO ENHANCE COOPERATION ON CLIMATE. AND ENVIRONMENT
BETWEEN
THE MINISTRY OF ECOLOGY AND ENVIRONMENT OF THE PEOPLE'S
REPUBLIC OF CHINA
AND
THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
This MOU to Enhance Cooperation on Climate and Environment is entered into by the
Ministry of Ecology and Environment of the People's Republic of China (MEE) and the State
of California of the United States of America (California) and hereafter jointly referred to as
the "Parties", in order to further strengthen and coordinate efforts to combat climate
change, promote clean and efficient energy, while protecting public health, the environment
and natural resources.
ARTICLE 1 Areas of Cooperation
The Parties agree to cooperate in the following areas, on the basis of the principles of
equality and mutual benefit:
Activities to mitigate carbon emissions, to control carbon dioxide, methane and other
greenhouse gases with various efforts, to reduce air pollution while enabling
sustained economic growth;
Activities that enhance air pollution control strategies for industrial sectors and the
transportation sector, such as the pollution control strategies on Volatile - Organic
Compounds (VOCS), 03, PM2.5, and the development of exhaust emissions standards,
as well as providing policy recommendations and input for some environmental
protection action plans under China's Five Year Plan;
Activities to reduce short lived climate forcers (SLCFS), especially bilateral
cooperation in the field of improving cooling efficiency and strengthening black
carbon research and governance, and subnational collaboration around mitigating
Hydrofluorocarbons;
Activities that strengthen institutions and governance structures that oversee
implementation and enforcement of air pollution reduction programs, such as
providing advice for further legislation and law compliance and enforcement;
Activities to implement carbon emissions trading systems, emission trading system
and other market-based instruments;
Activities that reduce energy consumption and improve energy efficiency among
industrial, commercial, and residential sectors, such as cooling efficiency;
Activities that increase the usage of electrified transportation, such as supporting the
adoption of New Energy Vehicles;
Activities that support new and expanded markets of clean and efficient energy

technologies, including within energy-intensive industries and transportation, and
carry out technology exchange and promotion;
Activities that support subnational climate change leadership on low carbon
development and cities' low carbon development efforts;
Activities that support. joint-ventures, partner-agreements, and capital investments in
the design, construction and operation of clean and efficient energy and
infrastructure projects, reduce air pollution, and promote eco-civilization;
Activities that strengthen sharing and cooperation in research and practice of climate
change;
Activities to exchange on integrated management of industry park, including
pollution prevention of industry park, low carbon and recycling development; and
Other mutually agreed activities.
ARTICLE 2 Forms of Cooperation
Forms of cooperation may include the following and any other forms agreed by both Parties:
Sharing information and experiences regarding policies and programs to strengthen
low carbon development across economic sectors, and policies, programs and
incentives to reduce air pollution across economic sectors and regions;
Exchange and visits between parties' relevant personnel, including exchange using
conference calls and annual meetings;
Exchange and temporary assignments of personnel from one Party to the other;
Cooperative research on clean and efficient energy technologies, including
developing shared research, development and deployment projects;
Joint organization of symposia, seminars, workshops, exhibitions and training;
Activities to establish partnerships and exchange knowledge and experiences on low
carbon cities (towns), low carbon communities, and low carbon industry park
development; and
Any other mutually agreed forms of cooperation that contribute to the purpose of
this MOU.
ARTICLE 3 Implementation
The Parties shall, on a regular basis, inform and consult with the other on matters of
common interest that represent opportunities for mutual benefit consistent with this MOU.
In order to explore specifically how to collaborate through the framework of the MOU, the
Parties agree to form an MOU Working Group, co-chaired by the Director General level
officials of the Department of International Cooperation of the MEE and Secretary of the
California Environmental Protection Agency, Chairman of the California Air Resource Board,
and Chair of the California Energy Commission, or their designees, on the California side.
The Parties agree to designate a point (or Secretariat) that will serve as the primary liaison of

the cooperation and communication and to negotiate the specifics of cooperation under this
Agreement on a regular basis.
The Secretariats will co-lead the implementation of this MOU, as appropriate, with other
agreements between entities in California and the Ministry of Ecology and Environment of
the People's Republic of China, and will organize in-person meetings once annually and/or
by video or telephone conferences as often as necessary.
ARTICLE 4 Duration, Termination, and Modification
The MOU will remain in effort from the date of its signature by the parties for two years. It
may be amended upon the agreement by the parties in writing.
This MOU does not constitute or create any legally binding or enforceable rights or
obligations, expressed or implied.
