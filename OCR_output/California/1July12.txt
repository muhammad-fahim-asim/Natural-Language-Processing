LETTER OF INTENT, REGARDING ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION,
SUBSCRIBED BY THE GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA,
UNITED STATES OF AMERICA AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF
MEXICO, UNITED STATES OF MEXICO.
The State of California of the United States of America, represented by the
Honorable Governor, - Mr. Edmund G. Brown Jr., and the State of Mexico, of the
United States of Mexico, represented by the Constitutional Governor, Doctor
Eruviel Avila Villegas, aiming to strengthen their friendship, cooperation and
institutional ties, consider the following:
That based upon the findings of the United Nations Program for the Environment in
Mexico, the State of California was identified as having the best vehicle inspection
system in the world by virtue of the effectiveness of its control systems applied to
vehicle inspection stations, the transparency of its verification process and
consequently the improvement of air quality.
That in pursuance to. the. above, the Government of California counts on the
institutional support of the California Environmental Protection Agency, the
California Air Resources Board, and the Bureau of Automotive Repair of the
California Department of Consumer Affairs.
That the Government of the State of Mexico has made the firm commitment to
implement the enforcement of a modern environmental protection policy, that shall
be inclusive, effective and transparent, regarding the control of contaminant
emissions and vehicle inspection.
For the foregoing reasons, we manifest the intention of:
FIRST. To initiate a process of evaluation and implementation of mechanisms for
collaboration and exchange of experiences in the areas of technology transfer of
Verification Centers' Control Systems operated in California, reducing emissions of
contaminants and training public.officers in the State of Mexico for the use of such
systems.
SECOND. This Letter of Intent will be referred to hereinafter, for the purposes of
promoting and following up, as "The States of California and Mexico: Joined for
Environmental Protection".



THIRD. This Letter of Intent is signed in good faith and does not create any legal
obligations.
THE GOVERNOR
THE GOVERNOR
OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA
OF THE STATE OF MEXICO
