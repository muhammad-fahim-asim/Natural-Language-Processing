BACKGROUND AND RESOLUTION
This Memorandum of Understanding (hereafter "MOU") is entered into by the Ministry of
Education of Spain (hereafter referred to as the Ministry), represented by Mr. Xavier Gisbert da
Cruz, Counselor of Education of the Embassy of Spain to the United States, by approved signing
on behalf of the State Secretary of Education, Vocational Training and Universities; and the
California State Department of Education (hereafter referred to as the Department) represented by
Tom Torlakson, State Superintendent of Public Instruction and approved signing authority on its
behalf.
On July. 2, 1986, the California Department of Education and the Ministry of Education of Spain,
established a historic Memorandum of Understanding (MOU).
The initial MOU was expanded and accepted by both parties in May 1989. In February 1993,
January 1997, February 2000, and February 2005, the MOU was revised and newly signed. The
Department and the Ministry recognized the benefits derived from the collaborative efforts and
programs, and therefore expressed the desire to continue the collaboration.
In the past years, important changes in education have taken place both in California and in Spain.
This circumstance, while imposing new challenges and demanding new solutions to both the
Department and the Ministry, underscores the tremendous value of international collaboration.
The Department and the Ministry:

In recognition of the importance that the English and Spanish languages carry as means of
communication between individuals, and countries, and of the valuable role languages play in the
exchange of ideas in the fields of economy, science, and culture;
Driven by the desire to advance the goals of mutual understanding and mutual respect between the
citizens of their respective territories and to provide the key elements of multicultural education
and training to participants in California and Spain; and
Conscious of the results and the experiences gained over the past 27 years of collaborative work;
THEREFORE, resolve to establish a new Memorandum of Understanding (MOU).
This MOU is hereby signed in due form pursuant to the following Agreements:
I.- GENERAL PRINCIPLES
I.1.- The Ministry and the Department have established the following objectives for the continued
development and implementation of cooperative programs under this MOU for improving the
quality of teaching the Spanish language and culture in California and the English language and
North American culture in Spain:
a) To provide professional learning opportunities to teachers and administrators in
California and Spain;
b) To provide students with a world-class education that trains them to be bi-literate and
successful in a multilingual global economy and work force;
c) To foster knowledge and appreciation of the rich cultures shared between Spain and
California; and
d) To promote relationships between.the citizens of California and Spain.
I.2.- The MOU will cover the schools that teach Spanish and/or other subjects of the curriculum in
Spanish as defined in state and federal law, and will adhere to the curriculum frameworks and
common core standards developed by the Department and school districts to satisfy the demands
and needs of the school population.

II. COOPERATIVE PROGRAMS
The cooperation between the Ministry and the Department will be demonstrated through the
following programs:
II.1.- California/Spain Visiting Teachers Program
II.1.1.- The Department and the Ministry have been coordinating the California/Spain Visiting
Teachers Program (CSVTP) since 1986. Teachers from Spain have made important contributions
to the school communities and to the academic success of English learners. The purposes of the
agreement for the CSVTP are to:
II.1.2.- Place teachers from Spain in kindergarten through twelfth grade (K-12) public school
teaching positions for one to a maximum of three years where their language skills, professional
expertise, and experience can enrich the education of California's students;
II.1.3.- Provide the visiting teachers, as well as California students, teachers, administrators, and
the state's general public witha-cros-cultural experience-that-can fostermutual understanding and
respect; and
II.1.4.- Create opportunities for the visiting teachers to collaborate in professional development
that improves teacher and student performance and which allows for a better understanding of the
United States culture, society, and teaching practices.
II.1.5.- The Department, in collaboration with the Ministry:
a) Will promote the recruitment of highly qualified teachers from Spain to teach in school
districts that have Spanish as a Foreign Language courses and/or bilingual programs.
b) Agrees to invite school districts to take part in the program and will designate the districts that
will participate each year.
c) Agrees to assist school districts each year in the screening and placement of highly qualified
teachers from Spain who may be contracted for classroom teaching assignments for one to three

II.2.- In-Service Training Programs: Summer Courses in Spain for Teachers,

As part of the reciprocity with Spain and California, and in order to improve the Spanish

the Department will promote the participation in specially designed courses at fully-accredited
universities in Spain. These courses will include Spanish language and culture as well as effective
teaching methods and practices.
II.2.1.- Each year the Ministry will announce the summer courses in Spain.
II.2.2.- The Ministry may offer scholarships to defray all or part of the costs of the courses, subject
to the availability of funds
II.2.3.- The travel costs to the courses in Spain will be the responsibility of the participant in the
program or of the school district.

II.3.- International Spanish Academies
The Department and the Ministry will collaborate in the development of the network of
English/Spanish bilingual schools known as International Spanish Academies in California
schools. The main objective of this initiative is to enable the implementation of high quality
bilingual education with support from the Ministry and other Spanish institutions, like the
Cervantes Institute
II.3.1.- The purposes of the International Spanish Academies are to:
a) Enhance high levels of biliteracy which enables the students to express themselves in English
and Spanish in personal matters as well as in academic settings.
b) Facilitate and promote student access to centers of higher education of recognized academic
excellence in the United States as well as in Spain, Europe, and all Spanish-speaking countries in
the Americas.
c) Promote, with the collaboration of the Ministry, the establishment of sister schools in
California and Spain
d) Educate students in the values of multicultural education, which make possible international
communication and respect among cultures
e) Train students to become fully literate in English and Spanish in order to live and compete in a
globalized society.
II.3.2.- In addition to the participation in the programs mentioned in previous articles of this MOU.
the Ministry will offer the schools integrated in the International Spanish Academies network a
certificate of studies and the following services:
a) Facilitating the accreditation of the high school course of studies for the equivalent ones in the
Spanish educational system.
b) Collaboration in teacher training activities according to school needs.

II.4.- Spanish Resource Center
The Ministry has established a Spanish Resource Center at the University of Southern California in
Los Angeles. This Center contains books, written and audiovisual didactic materials, and computer
programs related to the teaching of Spanish language
The purposes of the Center are to:
a) Support the training of teachers of Spanish in California through various professional
development activities.
b) Supply pedagogical guidance on methods of instruction of Spanish as a foreign language.
c) Provide bibliographic resources about Spanish publications.
d) Organize cuitural and educational events on the Spanish language and culture, including those
of Spanish-speaking countries.
II.5.- Publications and Materials
The Ministry will assist California schools, universities, and school libraries in accessing
and audiovisual materials from Spain, which relate to the language and culture of
publications Spain and contribute to the development of the programs set forth by this MOU.

III. EVALUATION

IV.1.- This MOU is valid for four years and may be canceled on demand by either party due to
impossibility of execution. for breach of any of the clauses. or by mutual agreement, provided that
