WHEREAS the Parties to this Cap and Trade Information Sharing and Confidentiality
Agreement for the Purposes of Participating in the Western Climate Initiative, Inc.
("Agreement") are governmental entities oft the partner states or provinces participating in
the Western Climate Initiative, Inc. ("WCI, Inc."), a non-profit corporation providing
administrative and technical services to partner jurisdictions to facilitate the development
and implementation oft their respective cap and trade systems for greenhouse gas ("GHG")
emissions;
WHEREAS the Parties to this Agreement share a common interest and purpose in the
cooperative development oflegislation or regulations relating to the implementation in the
individual partner jurisdictions ofa GHG cap and trade system;
WHEREAS WCI, Inc. executed agreements with entities (each a "Contractor," collectively
"Contractors") to provide services supporting implementation oft the Parties' GHG cap and
trade systems ("Services") that are listed in Appendix A hereto;
WHEREAS, the Parties to this Agreement share a common interest in working together
and with WCI, Inc. and Contractors to further the cooperative development of legislation
or regulations relating to the implementation in the individual partner jurisdictions of a
GHG cap and trade system;
WHEREAS the Parties to this Agreement believe that sharing information related to the
Services, including communications by and with Contractors, whether expressed verbally
or in writing, and documents and technical information, such as design data and market
sensitive operational data, from Contractors or a Party that are marked or otherwise
identified as confidential and/or deliberative will further this common interest and purpose;
WHEREAS Canadian provincial law provides for the confidentiality of and attaches
applicable privileges to records that are subject to solicitor client privilege; records that
would reveal the substance of deliberations of the Executive Council, including draft
regulations and enactments; records that would reveal advice to ministers; and records the

disclosure of which could reasonably be expected to reveal information received in

WHEREAS California's public records law exempts records from disclosure when the
public interest served by not disclosing the record clearly outweighs the public interest in
disclosure, and California courts have found a public interest in maintaining the
confidentiality of agency deliberations, including drafts of regulations, and California's
public records law provides that exemptions are not waived by disclosure of the material
to any governmental agency that agrees to treat the disclosed material as confidential -
limiting distribution to persons authorized in writing by an agency head I and uses the
material for purposes consistent with existing law;
WHEREAS the Parties do not intend this Agreement to exempt from disclosure any
documents that are subject to disclosure under Canadian provincial or California law. The
Parties intend that sharing Shared Information pursuant to this Agreement shall not waive
any exemptions or privileges applicable to the Shared Information; and
WHEREAS the Parties intend to implement their cap and trade systems, the
implementation of which is based on the joint use of the Services, and it is imperative to
the success of this approach that the communications and implementation between the
Contractors providing necessary Services, collectively, and the Parties be streamlined;
NOW THEREFORE, in order to advance their mutual interests, the Parties to this
Agreement, hereby agree to the following:
1. Purpose: The purpose of this Agreement is to facilitate the confidential
deliberations within and between the Parties in the interest of development and
implementation of GHG cap and trade legislation and/or regulations in the
individual partner jurisdictions. In furtherance of this purpose, the Parties wish to
share information related to the Services ("the Shared Information") including, but
not limited to communications by and with WCI, Inc. and Contractors, whether
expressed verbally or in writing, and documents or technical information, such as
design data and market sensitive operational data, from Contractors or Parties that
are: marked or otherwise identified as confidential and/or deliberative.
2. Confidentiality Statement: All Shared Information that is shared pursuant to this
Agreement is shared with the express intent that it be kept confidential and
privileged to the maximum extent allowable under law. Pursuant to this Agreement,
the confidences and privileges attached to the Shared Information have not and will
not be waived except as provided in paragraph 4 ofthis Agreement. Any disclosure
of the Shared Information, regardless of the intent of such disclosure, that is
inconsistent with this Agreement shall not waive the confidentiality and privileges
otherwise applicable to the Shared Information or any other information referenced
by, based upon, or related to such shared information.

3. Handling of Shared Information: The Shared Information is to be used by each
Party: solely to further the development and implementation by each Party ofa GHG
cap and trade system. The Parties may share information with one another and
within their agencies, departments, or ministries in a manner that maintains the
confidentiality and privilege of such Shared Information to the maximum extent
allowable under law. Any Party receiving Shared Information shall ensure that each
ofits agents, contractors or employees authorized to access the Shared Information
is informed that they are subject to and must comply with the requirements ofthis
Agreement. A receiving Party shall notify the disclosing Party immediately upon
the discovery of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the disclosing Party's
information or of any other breach of this Agreement, and will cooperate in every
reasonable way to help the disclosing Party prevent further unauthorized disclosure
or use ofits Shared Information.
4. Non-disclosure: A receiving party of Shared Information that originated, in whole
or in part, from another Party or a contractor of another Party may not disclose
Shared Information to any non-Party, except WCI, Inc. and Contractor(s), and
Shared Information may not be used in any other matter or proceeding without prior
written waiver from the disclosing Party, an order by a court or tribunal of
competent jurisdiction, prior public disclosure of the Shared Information by the
disclosing Party or a determination by the receiving party that disclosure of
information is required by applicable laws. A Party shall provide written notice
within ten working days to other Parties of any request by a non-party, except a
request from WCI, Inc. or Contractor(s), for access to the Shared Information. In
the event that the Party determines that the disclosure of the Shared Information
that originated, in whole or in part, from another Party or a contractor of another
Party to a non-party is required by applicable law, it will provide written notice to
the other Parties to this Agreement at least ten working days prior to such
disclosure. In the event that a decision about the requirements of disclosure of
Shared Information in a given Party's jurisdiction is subject to a deliberative
proceeding such as a hearing, the Parties shall, where appropriate and allowed by
law, be provided with an opportunity to participate in that proceeding.
5. Modification. Any modification to this Agreement shall be in writing and signed
by all Parties. The inclusion ofadditional parties to this Agreement: from among the
partner states or provinces of WCI, Inc. shall not be considered a modification and
shall be accomplished by having the new party execute an addendum and
distributing a copy to all Parties. None ofthe provisions ofthis Agreement shall be
deemed to have been waived by any act or acquiescence on the part of any Party,
its agents, contractors or employees, but only by an instrument in writing signed by
an authorized representative of the Party. No waiver of any provisions of this
Agreement shall constitute a waiver of any other provision(s) ofthe same provision
on another occasion.
6. Termination. Any Party to this Agreement may terminate its participation in this
Agreement upon thirty (30) days written notice to the other Parties. In such

7. Nondisqualification Agreement. No Party or counsel for any Party to this
Agreement will attempt to use, in any other legal proceeding or cause, either the
existence ofthis Agreement or any Shared Information or other information learned
as a result of this Agreement as a reason to disqualify any other person or firm
acting as counsel for a Party in connection with participating in WCI, Inc. from
acting as counsel in any other matter or proceeding. The provisions of this
paragraph shall survive the termination ofthis Agreement.
8. Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is determined by a court of
competent jurisdiction to be invalid in whole or in part, such determination shall
not affect the validity of other provisions off this Agreement.
9. Memorialization of Prior Understanding. This Agreement memorializes the
earlier oral understanding among the Parties according to which Shared
Information may have been shared among the Parties. This Agreement shall apply
to such previously Shared Information.
10. No Third Party Beneficiary. The Parties do not intend any provision of this
Agreement to benefit or to confer any legal right or obligation on any non-Party,
including Contractors, hereto.
11. Execution in Counterparts: This Agreement and any amendments or waivers
thereof may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which shall be
deemed an original but all ofwhich shall constitute one instrument. This Agreement
will be effective when at least one counterpart has been executed by each Party (the
"Effective Date"). Faxed or emailed signatures shall constitute original signatures
binding on the signing Party. Within 10 days ofthe Effective Date, counsel for each
ofthe Parties shall return an original signed version of this Agreement to the other
Parties' counsel.

ADDENDUM TO THE CAP-AND-TRADE INFORMATION SHARING AND CONFIDENTIALITY
AGREEMENT FOR THE PURPOSES OF PARTICIPATING IN THE
WESTERN CLIMATE INITIATIVE INC.
The Washington State Department of Ecology. (Ecology), a member of WCI Inc. since
December 20, 2021, hereby accepts to join the Cap-and-Trade Information Sharing and
Confidentiality Agreement for the purposes of participating in the Western Climate Initiative
Inc. signed in 2018 by the California Air Resources Board, the province of Nova Scotia and the
Gouvernement du Québec, and to abide by its provisions.
Consistent with the provisions of this Agreement, Ecology does not intend this Agreement to
exempt from disclosure any documents that are subject to disclosure under Washington
state law. Washington's public records law provides for access to public records in
furtherance of its public policy to ensure the people of Washington remain informed, but
also exempts from disclosure certain records or portions thereof when information
contained in such records derives actual or potential economic value from not being
generally known; when disclosure of information such as valuable formulae and research
data would produce private gain and public loss; when disclosure of pre-decisional opinions
or recommendations would be injurious to the agency's deliberative process; and when
information contained in such records is determined by Ecology to be financial, proprietary,
or market-sensitive.
