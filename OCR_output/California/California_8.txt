AGREEMENT OF COLABORATION
BETWEEN THE SECRETARY OF PUBLIC EDUCATION OF THE UNITED
STATES OF MEXICO AND THE CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION,
OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
The Secretary of Public Education, of the United States of Mexico
(Secretaria de Educacion Publica, SEP) and the California Department of
Education, of the United States of America, herein after referred to as "the Parties";
CONSIDERING the achievements obtained by both parties in the
development of a series of educational and training programs for American and
Mexican educators working with Spanish-speaking populations, especially those of
Mexican origin;
RECOGNIZING the importance of establishing collaborative programs to
attend the mutual interests and needs;
CONSIDERING that California has 6.2 million students enrolled in grades K-
12, of which 45% are Hispanic, mostly of Mexican origin. There are 1.3 million
students who speak Spanish only;
JU.
HAVING IN MIND the dispositions of the addendum for school districts of
the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) relating to educational exchange
between the Govemment of Mexico and the Government of the United States of
America, as of November 25, 2002;
The parties have agreed to the following:



ARTICLE I
Objective
The current Agreement has as its objective to continue the mutual
cooperation between both Parties in order to expand the framework of knowledge
of both educational systems and to support the Mexican student population and
school community by means of the Visiting Teachers Program.
ARTICLE II
Activities of Cooperation
In order to accomplish the objectives of the present Agreement, both Parties
will carry out the following activities of cooperation:
a.
strengthen the bilateral, educative, and cultural cooperation between
both educational systems, as well as the mutual understanding and
respect between the communities of California and Mexico;
b.
promote the educational and cultural exchange with the school
districts in the State of California;
C.
support activities that will permit participating teachers to become
acquainted with pedagogic innovations and techniques that will
J.0.
enrich their different teaching styles;
d.
support projects that will benefit and enrich the cultural diversity and
educational investigations in both Mexico and the State of California,
and
e.
agree on the terms of the Request for Teacher Applications and the
dates of the selection process for the Visiting Teachers Program
between Mexico and California.

ARTICLE IV
Costs and Expenses
The financial contributions that are required for the execution of the activities
referred to in this Agreement are subject to the availability of funds by both Parties.
Each party is responsible for its own travel expenses, and specific programs will be
conducted as long as funding is available.
!



ARTICLE V
Working Relations
The designated employees for each Party will operate under the direction
and dependence of his/her institution. As a result, there should not be other
employment arrangements with the Other party that would be considered to be a
change of supervisor.
The employees sent by each Party to the Other will abide by the current
national legislation of the receiving Party as well as the laws, terms and conditions
of his/her institution. This designated employee should not dedicate him/herself to
any other activity outside the established agreement, nor should helshe receive
any other form of payment outside those already established without the previous
authorization of the authorized supervisor.
Each one of the Parties will be responsible for the labor accidents that the
designated employee may suffer or the damages to his/her property, regardless of
the place they may occur. Additionally, neither party will file suit against the other
Party.
The Mexican teachers who in the Exchange Visitor Program are employed
in a California school district are required to return to Mexico for a minimum of two
(2) years following completion of their three (3) year program.
JO,
ARTICLE VI
Admittance and Departure of Employees
The receptor Party will provide all the necessary facilities for the admittance
and departure of the participants who labor in cooperative projects that derive from
this agreement. These participants will be subject to any migratory, fiscal,



customary, sanitary, and security procedures that are in effect in the receptor
country and will not carry out any other duties different from the functions already
established for them without the authorization of the authorized party in this matter.
The participants will leave the receptor country in accordance with the laws
established by the receptor country.
ARTICLE VIII
Solution of Controversies
Both parties through mutual agreement should resolve any differences that
may arise from the interpretation or application of this Agreement.
ARTICLE VIII
Final Dispositions
This Agreement will take effect on the date signed and will be valid for five
(5) years. The Agreement is automatically renewed for periods of the same
duration unless cancelled by either Party. Either Party upon notice of termination
may terminate this agreement provided that at least six (6) months written notice is
given.
JO
Both Parties can modify this Agreement through written consent. Such
modifications will take effect the date such document is signed.
The early termination of this Agreement will not affect the conclusion of the
cooperative actions that had been formalized during the time the Agreement was
valid.
