Letter of Intent
This Letter of Intend ("Lol") is made and signed in duplicate on the 12th of November 2019 (Commencement
Date), by and between:
The Netherlands Vehicle and Driving Licence Registration Authority, hereafter referred to as RDW, office
address, Europaweg 205, 2711 ER, Zoetermeer, The Netherlands
and
the Californial Department ofMotor Vehicles, hereafter referredi to as DMV, office address, 2415 First Avenue,
Sacramento, CA 95818, United States of America
RDW and DMVI have come to the following understanding;
1. Purpose
The purpose of this Letter of Intenti is to express the mutual intention to utilize the knowledge and experience
of the two Parties in the areas related to the automotive industry and (smart)mobility. The subjects of mutual
interesti are innovation related to electric vehicles, autonomous vehicles, vehicle type-approval Vs. self
certification and enforcement, guidelines experimentation, registration benchmarking, vehicle identification,
data and data sharing and innovations related to self-driving functionalities and the infrastructures needed to
provide sustainable and quality support. By exchanging knowledge and best practices, a mutually beneficial
cooperation can arise. However, nothing in this Lol obliges any party to adopt any law or standard of, or
proposed by, the other party.
2. Areas of interest and proposals
1. Areas of Interest
RDW and DMV investigate the possibilities to extend cooperation by the exchange of
knowledge/experiences, for example the possibility of al RDW 'foothold', / presence in California.
RDW and DMV deliver a joined effort to investigate and facilitate California based automotive manufacturers
that want to obtain (European) Type Approvals/Certification fort the European market.
2. Areas of Cooperation
Exchange of knowledge/experiences can take place in any of the following fields of common interest:
a. Innovative developments in the automotive on safety and security and the RDW's Vehicle Safety and
Security Framework (VSSF)
b. Emissions
C. Enforcement
d. Data use and data sharing
e. Cybersecurity



3. General understanding
1. Duration of Lol: This Lol shall be operational upon signing by both parties, will be deemed to commence on
the Commencement Date stated above, and will have an initial duration of 12 months. All activities
conducted throughout this term, and within the scope of the joint collaboration will be deemed to fall
under this Lol. A general evaluation of this cooperation and its future will be conducted and reported upon
completing 12 months from the date of signing the Lol.
2. Termination of Lol: The cooperation covered by this Lol shall terminate upon completion of the agreed
upon tasks or automatically after completing 12 months from the date of signing, whichever comes first.
The Lol may also be terminated with a one month's written notice from either party.
3. Extension of Lol: The Lol will not: automatically be extended. Upon completion both parties will evaluate
the mutual benefits of the Lol.. A positive evaluation of the Lol leads to the intention of both parties to
conclude a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU).
4. Coordination: In order to carry out and fulfil the aims of this Lol, each party will appoint an (the)
appropriate person(s) to represent its organization and to coordinate the implementation of activities.
RDW and DMV representatives will maintain contact at regular intervals to discuss progress and plan
activities.
RDW Contact Person: Mr. Gerard Doll - Director' Vehicle Regulation and Admission Division
DMV Contact Person: Dr. Bernard C. Soriano I Deputy Director, Registration Operations Division
5. RDW and DMV will pay the costs and expenses incurred by it in connection with the negotiation, entering
into and completion of this Letter of Intent and any further agreements, including the costs of own
advisors and consultants.
6. This Lol does not create any rights or obligations under international law.
