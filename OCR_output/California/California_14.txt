FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT
BETWEEN
THE GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA
AND
THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA
THE GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA AND THE GOVERNMENT OF
THE REPUBLIC OF ARMENIA (hereinafter "the Parties") RECOGNIZE THAT:
WHEREAS, the Republic of Armenia and the State of California share close ties and
California is home to the largest Armenian Community in the United States; and
WHEREAS, the Parties seek to expand the current level of cooperation between Armenia
and California in, but not limited to, the fields of trade and commerce, alternative
energy, environmental technology, information technologies, health, food and
agriculture, tourism, higher education and science and other technology-based industry
sectors, business innovation, research and development; and
WHEREAS, the State of California's University of California was instrumental in the
founding of the American University of Armenia and has had an ongoing, longstanding,
deep and beneficial relationship with the American University of Armenia since its earliest
days; and
WHEREAS, the State of California is home to the largest public university system in the
United States and the largest in-state innovation network in the United States, which offer
educational templates and technology-commercialization opportunities for the Republic of
Armenia.
Have agreed upon the following:
Article 1
Objective
The Parties hereby sign this Framework Agreement for the purposes of fostering
economic cooperation and development, facilitating joint research and development, and
enhancing business relationships and educational opportunities that will, in turn, foster
job creation and incubate global solutions from joint Armenia-California innovation
initiatives.

Article 2
Areas of Cooperation
The Parties plan to facilitate collaborations between Armenian and Californian educators,
entrepreneurs and researchers through the encouragement of exhibitions, seminars and
visits.
The Parties intend to support and encourage further development of interpersonal
contacts and exchanges, and promotion of mutual cooperation, understanding, and
friendship through ad hoc academic collaboration, trade delegations, and professional
exchanges between Armenia and California in key sectors including, but not limited to:
a.) Water Conservation and Management
b.) Alternative Energy and Related Clean Technologies
c.) Health and Biotechnology
d.) Information Technologies
e.) Trade and Commerce
f.) Arts and Culture
g.) Higher Education
h.) Agricultural Technologies
i.) Tourism
j.) Emergency Preparedness and Response
Article 3
Specific Activities
The Parties intend to encourage the creation of public-private partnerships between
Armenian and Californian entities in the areas of higher education, economic
development, social entrepreneurship, or academic research related to the above
activities.
The Parties intend to encourage the strengthening of sister city programs between
Armenia and California cities, which are designed to enhance opportunities for people-to-
people cultural and educational exchanges, economic partnerships, and social
entrepreneurship on the local level.
The Parties intend to encourage continued collaboration between Armenia and California
universities, public and private research institutions.
The Parties intend to welcome trade and tourism promotion officials and delegations. To
this end, the State of California intends to establish a Trade and Services Desk in
Armenia to be utilized on an as-needed basis by California's trade representative for
Europe and Eurasia to promote and encourage two-way trade and investment by
Californian and Armenian businesses and enterprises. The Trade and Services desk



would serve as a platform to provide information and contacts for entrepreneurs from
both California and Armenia who wish to connect with counterparts in order to engage in
trade and/or commercial activities in either goods and/or services or both.
The Parties intend to encourage the work of non-governmental organizations in Armenia
and will seek to help them to develop capabilities and build support through exchanges
with similarly oriented non-governmental organizations in California.
Article 4
Interpretation and Implementation
This Framework Agreement serves only as a record of the Parties' intentions and does
not constitute or create any legally binding or enforceable rights or obligations, implied
or expressed.
This Framework Agreement does not involve the exchange of funds, nor does it
represent any obligation of funds by the Parties. All costs that may be undertaken
pursuant to this Framework Agreement will be assumed by the Parties that incurs them,
unless otherwise agreed by future written agreement. All activities referenced in this
Framework Agreement are subject to the availability of the required resources on the
part of Parties, and may be subject to appropriation by the relevant legislative body of
the respective Party.
Any personnel designated or assigned to implement activities referenced in this
Framework Agreement shall work under the direction and responsibility of the
organization or institution by whom they are employed or contractually retained and will
at all times maintain their employment or contractual relationship with that organization
or institution. Their work on activities to implement this Framework Agreement will not
create an employer-employee relationship with any other organization or institution or
with the Parties, and in no case shall any other organization or institution be considered
as a substitute or joint employer of the designated or assigned personnel.
All activities undertaken pursuant to this Framework Agreement, and all personnel
designated or assigned to implement activities referenced in this Framework Agreement
are subject to the applicable laws of the receiving country.
The respective Points of Contact for each Party concerning all communications or
information exchanges for this Framework Agreement shall be the following:
For California:
Senior Advisor for International Affairs and Trade
Governor's Office of Business and Economic Development



For Armenia:
Advisor to the Minister of Economy
Nothing in this Framework Agreement limits the right of either Party to establish similar
agreements with other entities, nor does cooperation pursuant to this Framework
Agreement affect the rights and/or obligations of the Parties under other international
agreements.
Article 5
Final Provisions
This Framework Agreement shall become effective upon its signature, and shall remain in
effect for an indefinite duration, subject to the provisions below.
This Framework Agreement may be modified at any time by mutual consent of the
Parties. Any modification shall be made in writing and specify the date on which such
modification is to become effective.
Either Party may withdraw from this Framework Agreement at any time by providing
thirty (30) days advance written notice to the other Party.
Signed in New York on September 24th, 2019, in two original copies, each in the
Armenian and English languages. Both texts are equally authentic.
