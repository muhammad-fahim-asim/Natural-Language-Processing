MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
between
State of California
and
INTERNATIONAL BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION DEVELOPMENT,

November 11, 2016



This Memorandum of Understanding ("Memorandum") represents a statement of
intent by the State of California ("California"), and the International Bank for Reconstruction
and Development (IBRD) and the International Development Association (IDA) (collectively,
the "World Bank" or the "Bank") (each a "Party" and together, the "Parties") to collaborate in
the achievement of common objectives as described and on the basis outlined below.
WHEREAS:
(A) The World Bank's overarching mission is to reduce poverty, improve living
conditions, and promote sustainable and comprehensive development in its developing
member countries. It has established two ambitious goals to anchor its mission: end
extreme poverty within a generation and boost shared prosperity. The World Bank will
achieve these goals by providing loans, concessional financing, technical assistance,
and knowledge sharing services to its developing member countries and through
partnerships with other organizations.
(B) California faces significant impacts from climate change, including rising sea levels,
diminishing snow pack, increased size and intensity of wildfire, and greater impact
from drought. As a result, California has taken a world-leading role in response to
climate change and in working with jurisdictions around the world in promoting more
aggressive action to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and build resilience to climate
change.
NOW THEREFORE the Parties intend as follows:
A.
Objective
Consistent with their respective mandates, the Parties desire to cooperate on sharing
knowledge and good practice with other territories and cities on sustainable, low
carbon development strategies, tools, and good practices consistent with the goal of
limiting global warming well below 2 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels and
to pursue efforts to limit the temperature increase even further to 1.5 degrees Celsius,
Collaboration will cover multiple sectors and programs, focusing on shared and
necessary goals to achieve greater benefits.
B.
Collaboration
(a) To further the achievement of their common objectives, the Parties intend to
collaborate in the following manner ("*Collaborative Activities"):
(i) share knowledge, ideas and lessons learned;
(ii) plan joint activities in areas of common interest;
(ili) pool efforts and expertise;
(iv) collaborate on the promotion, preparation and organization of information
exchanges, action plans, and implementation strategies;
(v) collaborate on the organization and execution of case studies, best
practices, and templates;
2



(vi) collaborate on ways to engage other entities to bring further support to the
activities pursued under this Memorandum;
(vii) collaborate on the dissemination of model policies and programs through
publications, seminars, workshops, conferences and other easily accessible
means; and
(b) The Collaborative Activities will be delivered through various initiatives,
including the Global Platform for Sustainable Cities (GPSC), a knowledge sharing
program that will provide access to cutting-edge tools and promote an integrated
approach to sustainable urban planning and financing coordinated by the World
Bank, for which the California will act as a technical partner. Initial Collaborate
Activities may include:
(i)
Carbon inventory methodology, including establishment of a database
and development of related templates;
(ii) Decarbonization pathway to 2050, including cataloging of cross sector
methods and consideration of policy interactions across sectors;
guidance on technical and political processes for emission target
setting; identification and sharing of resources for implementation,
including market and non-market means; tracking and analysis of
indicators for progress towards goals;
(iii) Capacity building, including model policies and training programs for
other regions, development of templates, case studies analysis, tool and
resource sharing, and analysis of governance structures in specific
regions
(c) As part of this collaboration, the Parties will, from the outset, work in partnership
with the Under 2 Coalition and its Secretariat and the Climate Group, and seek
other partnerships as appropriate.
(d) The Parties will periodically evaluate the effectiveness of working in collaboration,
with reference to their respective mandates and priorities.
C.
Contacts
Each party hereby designates and appoints below its representative with overall
responsibility for implementing this Memorandum. The Parties may, by written notice to the
other. Party, designate additional or different persons as points of contact but the Parties expect
to have only one person at a time designated as the person with overall responsibility for all
activities undertaken pursuant to this Memorandum.

D.
General
1.
This Memorandum does not constitute an agreement or commitment by either Party to
enter into or provide support for any specific activity or project.
2.
The Parties expect that this Memorandum and information with respect to the
collaborative activities contemplated herein will be publicly disclosed by the Bank.
Such disclosure will be made in accordance with the Parties' respective disclosure
policies. The Parties plan to consult with each other concerning the manner and form
of any acknowledgement of the other Party's support regarding the activities.
3.
This Memorandum shall become effective upon signature by both Parties on the date
set forth below. It will remain in effect until November 10, 2019.
