MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
To Establish a Partnership between the following Federal States
The State of California, United States of America
AND
the Land Baden-Wurttemberg, Federal Republic of Germany.
Preamble
In 2015, the Minister Prasident Winfried Kretschmann of Baden-Wurtteberg and
Governor Jerry Brown of California shared thoughts on the occasion of a delegation
visit to California on a potential collaboration between Baden-Wurttemberg and
California in the economic, environmental and climate field as well as in science,
literature, and art. During a visit to Stuttgart in 2017, Minister President Kretschmann
and Governor Brown discussed the expansion of the collaboration between technology
and science centers and how to enhance the exchange and friendship between Baden-
Wurttemberg and California. In May of 2018, the California State Senate passed a
Resolution that supports expanding the bilateral relationship that exists between Baden-
Wurttemberg and California and encouraged the efforts to establish a sister state
relationship.
Memorandum of Understanding
The State of California in the United States of America and the Land Baden-
Wurttemberg in the Federal Republic of Germany (in the following referred to as "the
partners") are leading in the sector of innovation and climate protection. They both
hereby express the intent to share as partners the vision of sustainable change by
combining economic prosperity with the indispensable protection and preservation of
our natural heritages. They are both convinced that concrete action locally and close
cooperation between each other will contribute substantially to sustainable change.
In 2015, Baden-Warttemberg and California jointly launched the Under2 Coalition as an
initiative for climate protection. It has now developed into a worldwide movement of
subnational governments that fight climate change by implementing concrete policies
and measures. At present, more than 200 countries, regions and municipalities are
members of the initiative. Both Baden-Warttemberg and California are establishing
ambitious policies and programs to achieve their climate protection goals by 2030.
These include the promotion of smart and environmentally-fiendly mobility, energy
efficiency and the comprehensive utililization of renewable energy.
Other important contributions are made by industry, the academic community and
research with innovative technologies and sustainable solutions. The Ministry for
Science, Research and the Arts of Baden-Wurttemberg and the California State



University have enjoyed a cooperation since 1995 as well as an exchange program that
was renewed in 2015.
The Partners have come to the following understanding:
1. The Land Baden-Warttemberg and the State of California express their intention of
cooperating in the future in the following areas within the scope of respective
competences, functions and means:
CLIMATE, ENERGY AND ENVIRONMENTAL POLICIES, including measures to
improve resilience in the face of climate change and consequent natural events
TRAFFIC TRANSFORMATION, including initiatives and measures for sustainable
and modern mobility
URBAN INFRASTRUCTURE
ECONOMIC COOPERATION, specifically in the field of digital transformation and
in collaboration with start-ups from Silicon Valley
INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY IN RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT AND THE
BUSINESS SECTOR
SCIENCE, RESEARCH, ARTS AND CULTURE
2. The following activities should be specifically conducted within the scope of the State
Partnership:
Exchange of information on energy storage, utilization of renewable energy and
measures for energy efficiency
Exchange of experience and information for the promotion of sustainable mobility
and the creation of required infrastructure (Strategy Dialogue Automotive
Industry)
Exchange of experts and information on best practices in cybersecurity,
Exchange of experts and job shadowing in science, research, arts and culture as
well as in the state agencies for energy (KEA and California Energy
Commission), mobility (e-mobil BW and California Air Resources Board) and
environment (LUBW and California Enivronmentaly Protection Agency)
Establishment of joint, innovative research approaches on climate change (as a
partnership among government, academia, industry, and civil society)
Exchange of expert knowledge in the film and media industry in partnership with
the California Film Commission and the Media and Film Society Baden
Wurttemberg
Collaboration in the field of literature and intellectual history such as provenance
research, starting with a workshop in California (June 2018) on the subject
"Thomas Mann's American Library"
Exchange of experts and cooperation in the field of digital transformation,
specifically within the scope of the project "Innovation Camp BW Silicon Valley"
(www.innovationcampbw.de)



Exchange and collaboration regarding start-ups and networking of start-up
ecosystems, specifically through partnership with the California Innovation Hub
(iHub) Networ
Sharing and transfer of know-how and potential project concerning
comprehensive, publically accessible electric charging infrastructure
Expansion and optimization of public transport and consequently the necessity of
a marked increase in transport capacity in public transport
Exchange of experience, specifically for the rapid installation of infrastructure,
procurement of vehicles, funding and new forms of public transport with
autonomous vehicles, connectivity of transport modes and carriers, new mobility
services providers, sharing concepts (ride sharing, car sharing, bike sharing) to
achieve zero emissions in inner cities and livable urban quarters as well as
promoting pedestrian and bicycle transport in order to increase their share
significantly and systematically on all routes. Exchange of information on
respective urban and traffic planning.
3. None of the provisions contained in this Memorandum of Understanding should entail
any legally binding or financial obligations for any one of the Partners. The Partners can
withdraw from this MoU at any time and should provide 60 days notice prior to doing SO.
This Memorandum of Understanding was signed at San Francisco on September 15th,
2018 in two copies of the same content in the English and German language.
