Memorandum ofUnderstanding
to Enhance Cooperation on Low Carbon
between the Beijing Municipal Commission of
Development and Reform
and
the California Governor's Office
The Beijing Municipal Commission of Development and
Reform and the California Governor's Office (hereinafter referred to
as "the Participants") recognize the following:
Enhancing regional cooperation between the Beijing and
California is critical to combating climate change, supporting the
intended action in bilateral countries determined contributions on
global change, controlling the emissions of carbon dioxide and other
GHG, and protecting the environment and natural resources.
In order to achieve the sustainable development and response to
climate change, The Beijing Municipal Commission of
Development and Reform and the California Governor's Office have
therefore reached the following understandings:



I. Purpose
The purpose of this Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is
to commit to agreement of U.S.-China Joint Statement on Climate
Change, promote to long-term transformation of low carbon, livable,
ecological and environment protection society.
Toward this end, the participants intend to share the best
practice demonstration, display the leadership on controlling carbon
dioxide emissions, and strength the capacity of mitigation and
adaptation in climate change.
The priority area for cooperation will be on developing the
sustainable, low carbon and livable city, sharing the good experience
of demonstration projects, exploring the sustainable pattern, and
working together on other areas through policy dialogue, capacity
building, joint research and expertise application.
II.Cooperation Fields
These include, but are not limit to:
1. Low carbon legislation and low carbon standard;
2. Comprehensive and integer theory on low carbon planning
and its technical means;
-2-



3. Clean energy and sustainable energy;
4. Carbon emission trading;
5. Low carbon transportation and relative technology (such as
electric vehicle)
6. Smart grid and relative technology;
7. Capacity building (training, lecture, workshop, etc);
8. Public information and education;
9. Other area upon mutual agreement as set forth in Appendix
A;
III. Ways of Cooperation
The participants may choose to cooperate in the following
ways:
1. Organize seminars, meetings, and conferences on topics of
common interest, and invite relevant experts, scholars, and
entrepreneurs to attend;
2. Organize study groups in both cities for study, investigation,
or professional training;
3. Carry out joint demonstration projects including individual
low-carbon technology demonstration projects and
-3-



integrated demonstration projects;
4. Support exchanges and joint researches between the mutual
research institutions;
5. Organize and issue bilingual publications jointly in both
Chinese and English on low-carbon;
6. Engage in other cooperative opportunities.
IV. Organization
The participants agree to set up a joint working group that is
responsible for planning and coordinating relevant work involving
this MOU.
The working group is responsible to encourage the cooperation
projects on both planning and construction/building departments,
relevant research institutions, and enterprises.
V.Dispute Settlement
The participants shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any
dispute in the interpretation and application of this MOU. This MOU
is not intended to give rise to rights or obligation under international
law, and does not authorize any rights to either side or any third
party, nor shall it impose any liability on either side or any third
-4-



party.
VI. Execution and Validity
This MOU is executed on June 7, 2016, in Beijing, China. The
MOU is executed in duplicate in Chinese and English; both versions
are equally authentic.
This MOU shall come into force upon execution by both
participants and is valid for three years. If any party wishes to
terminate this MOU, it shall notify the other party in writing six
months in advance, and the solution shall be negotiated jointly by
the participants. The termination of this MOU shall not affect

bilateral cooperation projects undertaken and obligations fulfilled by
the participants before written notice to terminate the MOU is given.
Appendix A:
Beijing Development and Reform Commission (BDRC) will
support the California's low carbon cooperation programs under the
framework of the Global Climate Leadership Memorandum of
Understanding.
California Governor's Office will support Beijing to set its
goals of reducing greenhouse emissions according to its own

situation and circumstances, and will actively engage in the areas of
improving policies and measures to reduce energy consumption,
improving energy efficiency, promoting clean energy and reducing
carbon emissions.
