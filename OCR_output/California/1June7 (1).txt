Memorandum of Understanding on Environment Cooperation
The California Environmental Protection Agency
And
The Beijing Municipal Environmental Protection Bureau
The intent of this Memorandum of Understanding on Environment Cooperation is to renew
and strengthen the cooperation relationship between the California Environmental
Protection Agency ("CalEPA") and the Beijing Environmental Protection Bureau (*Beijing
EPB"), who signed the original MOU in 2005, and updated it in 2013, in order to promote
cooperation and collaboration on science, technology, and policies in the field of
environmental protection. This is to update the MOU again.
Article I
Both sides will carry out cooperation in the areas of environmental protection on the basis
of equality and mutual benefit.
Article II
The main areas of cooperation and of common interest are as follows:
Environmental management legislation, policy and regulation.
Air quality management (air quality monitoring and assessment, vehicle emission
control, advanced vehicle technologies, industrial pollution control, pollution source
management, regional approaches to air quality management, compliance and
enforcement, etc.);
Water management and services (surface and ground water protection, water body
ecology, water pollution control, waste water treatment, etc.);
Solid waste management and recycling;
Capacity building (training, lectures, workshops etc.);
Public education.
Cooperation may also be undertaken in other areas as agreed by the Parties.
Article III
Cooperation under this MOU includes the following:
Exchange of scientific and technical information through bi-lateral visits and
1



meetings;
Collaborative research on pollution prevention and control;
Best policy practice and knowledge, and experience sharing;
Capacity building through study tours, lab comparisons, in-depth technical trainings,
and invited lectures;
Exchange ofi information on environmental technologies, goods, and services;
Any other forms of cooperation deemed necessary by the Parties.
Article IV
The Parties will draw up annual work plan concerning specific issues of cooperation under
this agreement.
The Deputy Secretary for Border and Intergovernmental Relations of CalEPA will be
appointed as California's liaison. CalEPA may designate California Universities to support
cooperation under this agreement.
The Deputy Director of Beijing EPB for International Cooperation will be appointed as
Beijing's liaison. The Beijing Research Academy of Environmental Sciences and Beijing
Municipal Environmental Monitoring Center may support cooperation under this
agreement.
Article V
The parties will allocate financial and human resources as necessary for the implementation
of the annual plan. The Energy Foundation-Beijing Office and other non-profit
organizations and think-tanks may participate in the development of the annual work plan,
and may provide financial support on implementing the plan.
Article VI
This agreement may be amended as agreed by the Parties in writing.
Article VII
Unless otherwise extended in writing, this agreement shall be in full force and effect for a
period of four years. Any party may withdraw from the agreement 45 days after written
notice to the other party.

Article VII
The present agreement will become effective upon signature. This agreement is signed, in
