Declaration of Intent
Ministry of Climate and Environment Norway and California Environmental
Protection Agency
California and Norway are equally committed to significant climate change action and share the long-
term goal of becoming low emission societies by 2050. Norway has a target to reduce emission by at
least 50 percent up towards 55 percent by 2030. California has a target to achieve carbon neutrality by
2045. These targets have resulted in ambitious policies for zero emission transportation on land and sea,
energy innovation, clean power generation, and zero waste initiatives;
California's increasing share of renewable energy and Norway's unique position with 99 percent
renewable electricity make California and Norway natural partners to share experiences.
A strong partnership currently exists between Norway and California. This Declaration of Intent serves to
formalize the partnership by pledging to strive toward measurable and complementary outcomes. This
Declaration of Intent builds upon a prior declaration of intent in effect from August 2, 2017 to August 2,
2019.
Through this Declaration of Intent, Norway, through its Ministry of Climate and Environment, and
California, through its Environmental Protection Agency, each of which may be referred to as a
"Participant," seek to promote climate action at all levels. Our partnership builds on the following
Common objectives:
Confirm our commitment to holding the increase in global warming to well below 2'C and
pursuing efforts to limit the temperature increase to 1.5'C above pre-industrial levels.
Undertake ambitious emission reductions based on the recognition that climate action is
beneficial to the economy at large, while also building climate resilience.
Promote the perspective that we need to implement climate action at all levels of government
and involve all sectors and stakeholders.
Support efforts to achieve necessary near- and long-term emission reductions and showcase
results and experiences at all levels of government to demonstrate the benefits of ambitious
partnerships and practical collaboration.
Support the implementation of the Sustainable Development Goals, as that term is defined in
the United Nation's 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, as the basis for sustainable, low
emission and climate resilient societies, with special focus on Sustainable Development Goal 13,
Climate Action.
Promote the economic and social benefits created by the transition to low emission, climate
resilient societies.
Support a just transition that can support low emission community development and help
transition workers to jobs for the zero carbon economy.
Foster competitiveness and job creation, and promote a positive interaction between policy and
low emission technology development.
To accomplish these goals and advance climate action, California and Norway will endeavour to share
strategies, experiences and insights in the following priority areas:
1



a)
Natural climate solutions
Conserving and strengthening the carbon storage of natural ecosystems to realize the potential
of providing one third of the global mitigation necessary to meet the 1.5'C target.
Conserving, restoring and sustainably managing the world's forests to protect biodiversity,
enhance wildfire resilience and ecosystem resilience, and provide long-term climate benefits and
ensure economic and food security.
Working with partners, including the Governors' Climate and Forests Task Force, to facilitate and
pilot the long-term financing of, and incentives for, reduced deforestation, including through the
use of state-of-the art standards that ensure social and environmental safeguards are addressed
and respected.
Working with partners in tropical countries to enhance relationships between jurisdictional forest
and climate programs and the private sector, in order to promote deforestation-free supply
chains at significant scale.
b)
Zero emission transportation
Providing experiences in innovation and advancing business models, policy development, and
technological solutions to catalyze the transition to zero emission, efficient transportation that
meet society's needs.
Continuing to work through the Zero Emission Vehicle Alliance.
Driving innovation on the global scale to meet the International Maritime Organization's goal of
reducing emissions from international shipping by 50 percent by 2050 through:
o Sharing experiences and analyses on policy options and policy implementation.
o Promoting dialogue between private sector actors in the growing markets for zero
emission transportation.
o Sharing lessons learned with other governments and private sector actors.
Employing forward-looking spatial planning to build a zero emission transport system that
includes mass transit solutions and zero emission technologies for road and ship transport.
Ensuring paired growth in renewable electricity deployment with electrification of the transport
system. California's increasing share of renewable energy and Norway's unique position with 99
percent renewable electricity make California and Norway natural partners to share experiences.
C
The climate economy: carbon markets and carbon valuation
Sharing best practices in carbon pricing and market mechanisms.
2



Continuing to share lessons learned, experiences and analytical insights from the development
and application of market-based programs, carbon pricing instruments in different sectors, and
the added perspectives from updating and improving estimates of the social cost of carbon.
Sharing experiences from implementation of California's Cap-and-Trade Program and Norway's
participation in the European Union Emissions Trading System and supplementary carbon pricing
instruments, to provide insights that may assist in the assessment of market mechanisms in other
venues.
d)
Achieving a carbon neutral economy
Analyzing approaches to reduce consumption of fossil fuels to achieve a carbon neutral economy.
Fostering research, innovation, and collaboration to support the development of new solutions
that aim to help Norway and California achieve their low emission goals.
Sharing best practices to foster connections between employers, workers, and communities to
identify the best areas for economic and workforce development for a carbon neutral economy.
Continuing expert dialogue, shared information and experiences in developing and implementing
carbon capture and sequestration.
Points of contact, responsibilities, and revision of the Declaration of Intent
For each year of this Declaration of Intent, the Participants will develop an annual work plan ("Work
Plan") identifying activities for that year. These activities could include, among other things, plans for
exchanges and information sharing, training, and events.
An annual working level meeting will assess the status of implementation of most current Work Plan. This
Declaration of Intent is effective from the date that it is fully executed and will remain in effect for a
period of two years. The Participants agree that, at least six months prior to the expiration of this
Declaration of Intent, they will meet in good faith to discuss whether to extend or enter into a new
declaration of intent.
For Norway, the Climate and Environment Counselor at Norway's embassy to the US will be the point of
contact for this Declaration of Intent.
For California, the Deputy Secretary for Climate Policy at the California Environmental Protection Agency
will be the point of contact for this Declaration of Intent.
The designated points of contact will facilitate expert dialogue, information sharing, facilitate visits if
applicable, and otherwise serve as partners in promoting the intents of cooperation between Norway
and California on climate change.
This Declaration of Intent is a voluntary initiative. It does not create any legally binding rights or
obligations and creates no legally cognizable or enforceable rights or remedies, legal or equitable, in any
form whatsoever. In addition, the pledges in this Declaration of Intent are not conditioned upon
3



reciprocal actions by other Participants; each Participant retains full discretion over implementation of
its pledges in light of the Participant's individual circumstances, laws, and policies.
This Declaration of Intent does not involve the exchange of funds, nor does it represent any obligation of
funds by either Participant. All costs that may arise from activities covered by, mentioned in, or pursuant
to this Declaration of Intent will be assumed by the Participant who incurs them, unless otherwise
expressly agreed in a future written arrangement in accordance with applicable laws. All activities
undertaken pursuant to this Declaration of Intent are subject to the availability of funds, personnel and
other resources of each Participant.
The personnel designated by a party for the execution of this Declaration of Intent will work under the
orders and responsibility of that party and any other organization or institution to which the personnel
already belongs, at all times maintaining any preexisting employment relationship only with that party
and organization or institution, and not with any other Participant.
This Declaration of Intent shall be construed consistent with all applicable laws, and activities
undertaken in connection with this Declaration of Intent shall be subject to, and shall be undertaken in a
manner consistent with, all otherwise-applicable laws.
Any difference that may arise in relation to the interpretation or application of this Declaration of Intent
will be resolved through consultations between the Participants, who will endeavor in good faith to
resolve such differences.
This Declaration of Intent may be modified at any time by mutual consent off the Participants. Any
modification shall be made in writing and specify the date on which such modification is to become
effective.
Either Participant may, at any time, withdraw from this Declaration of Intent by providing a written
notice to the other Participant. A Participant who intends to withdraw from this Declaration of Intent
shall endeavor to provide notice of such withdrawal to the other Participant 30 days in advance. The
termination of this Declaration of Intent shall not affect when activities initiated while this Declaration of
Intent is in effect shall conclude, unless the Participants mutually decide otherwise.
