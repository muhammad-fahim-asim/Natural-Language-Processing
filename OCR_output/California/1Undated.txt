Memorandum of Understanding
between
The State of California
and
The Province of British Columbia
The California.Air Resources Board and the British Columbia Ministry of
Environment:
Acting on the commitment in the Memorandum ofUnderstanding between The Province
ofBritish Columbia and The State ofCalifornia on Pacific Coast Collaboration to
Protect Our Shared Climate and Ocean ofMay 2007 to work together to reduce
greenhouse gases from the transportation sector through the adoption of greenhouse gas
vehicle emissions standards in British Columbia consistent with California's laws and
regulations;
Pursuant to the Memorandum to Establish the Pacific Coast Collaborative of June 2008
to which both British Columbia and California are signatories, and as provided for in
Article 6;
Resolved to see decisive and immediate action taken to address greenhouse gas
emissions from the transportation sector through the implementation ofvehicle emissions
standards - the Pavley standards - that progressively reduce the intensity of GHG
emissions from vehicles in our jurisdictions;
Committed to maximize the impact of our joint actions in implementing vehicle
emission standards by ensuring consistency in the application of the standards in
California and British Columbia;
Agreed that collaboration and resource sharing in the implementation of greenhouse gas
vehicle emission standards should be pursued wherever possible to ensure success;
Now therefore commit to move forward in partnership in the development of
greenhouse gas vehicle emission standards as follows:
2



I. Coordinate efforts to ensure complete coverage
A. The British Columbia. Ministry ofl Environment and California Air Resources
Board may share resources to support the consistent application of vehicle
emission standards to vehicle models available to citizens in California and
British Columbia, including cooperation on vehicle test data and determination of
carbon dioxide equivalent values for vehicle models available to the market in
either jurisdiction.
B. The California. Air Resources Board agrees in principle to support the Ministry of
Environment, British Columbia, in the implementation of its Vehicle Emission
Standards Regulation by providing technical and policy guidance to assist British
Columbia Ministry of Environment in making determinations for vehicle models
or systems delivered for sale in British Columbia.
II. Information Sharing
A. California Air Resources Board and the British Columbia Ministry of
Environment will work to implement information sharing processes to ensure
the timely communication of proposed and enacted regulatory amendments by
the California Air Resources Board, policy determinations and approvals as
part of the implementation of the California Code of Regulations, Title 13 by
the Executive Officer; and,
B. Wherever possible, the rationale for said policy determinations and approvals by
the Executive Officer that may impact the application ofthe California Code
of Regulations, Title 13, in BC, be made available to British Columbia
Ministry of Environment; and,
C. The categorization of manufacturers as large volume, intermediate volume,
independent low volume and small volume manufacturers under the
California Code of Regulations, Title 13, will be periodically determined and
made public by California Air Resources Board; and,
D. The constituent values that determine carbon dioxide equivalent values for
emissions from specific vehicle test groups be made available to British
Columbia Ministry of Environment in such a: manner as can be shared with
regulated entities in British Columbia.
III.Referencing
Regulations made under British Columbia's Greenhouse Gas Reductions (Vehicle
Emissions Standards) Act may specifically reference and make use, in whole or part, of
determinations oft the California. Air Resources Board, including California Air Resources
Board Executive Orders under the California Code of Regulations, Title 13, that
determine the carbon dioxide equivalent values for emissions from specific vehicle test
groups.
3



IV.Limitations
This Memorandum of Understanding is not intended to be legally binding or to impose
legal obligations on either California. Air Resources Board or the British Columbia
Ministry of Environment and will have no legal effect. Neither California Air Resources
Board nor British Columbia Ministry ofl Environment is responsible for the actions of
third parties or associates who may be involved in activities outlined in this
Memorandum ofUnderstanding.
