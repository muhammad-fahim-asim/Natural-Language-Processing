Memorandum of Understanding
on the Friendly Exchanges and Cooperation between
the State of California of the United States of America and
Guangdong Province of the People's Republic of China
The State of California of the United States of America and Guangdong
Province of the People's Republic of China,
Considering the importance of enhancing the state-province friendship
and mutual understanding,
Believing that a mutually beneficial partnership should be promoted to
work effectively and continually,
Pursuant to The Three Joint Communiqués and other agreements
between the United States of America and the People's Republic of
China,
Have agreed, through the state-province contacts and connections, to
strengthen the cooperation in areas including but not limited to:
- Two-way trade and investment
I Science and technological innovation
I Environment conservation
I Renewable and sustainable energy
I- Education and human resources
I Culture
I Tourism
The State Government of California and the People's Government of
Guangdong Province have designated the following organisations as the
primary liaison offices for both sides respectively.



California: California Governor's Office of Business and Economic
Development
Guangdong: The Foreign Affairs Office of Guangdong Province
The liaison offices will keep in touch or meet to discuss the progress of
ongoing programmes in the above-mentioned areas when necessary.
This MoU shall come into force on the day of signing by the official
representatives of both governments.
Signed in Guangzhou on the fifteenth day of April 2013, this MoU is
written in English and Chinese languages, both in duplicate and deemed
equally authentic.
Zhu Xiaodan,
Edmund G. Brown,
Governor of Guangdong Province,
Governor of the State of California,
The People's Republic of China
The United States of America
