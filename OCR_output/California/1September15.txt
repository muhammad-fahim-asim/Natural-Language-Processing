Purposes
1
The MOU is designed to support the Participants' efforts to address
climate change issues and to realize. the goals of low carbon development.
On the basis of equality and mutual benefits, the MOU provides a
framework for the Participants to carry out practical exchanges and
cooperation, but does not authorize any rights to either side or any third
party, nor shall it impose any liability on either side or any third party.
