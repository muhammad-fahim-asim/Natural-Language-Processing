Confidentiality Agreement
College of Supervisors of The Travelers Companies, Inc.
The Connecticut Insurance Department ("CID"), as lead regulator ofThe Travelers Companies,
Inc. ("The Travelers") insurance holding company system and other regulators of The Travelers
and its regulated affiliates (collectively "The Travelers Regulators"; each "The Travelers
Regulator") enter into this Confidentiality Agreement ("Agreement") for exchanging confidential
information pertaining to The Travelers and its regulated and non-regulated affiliates on a
reciprocal basis within the framework ofthe Supervisory College of The Travelers.
This Agreement applies to confidential information shared in preparation for the meetings ofthe
Supervisory College of The Travelers or as a result of such meetings. For the purpose ofthis
Agreement, confidential information means any information regarded as confidential, privileged
or otherwise protected from disclosure under the laws applicable to the insurance regulator that is
the source of the documents, materials or information.
The CID and The Travelers Regulators who are parties to this Agreement acknowledge that this
Agreement does not create any legally binding obligations, confer any rights, modify, or supersede
any domestic laws or regulatory requirements in force in, or applying to, their respective
jurisdiction and does not confer upon any person the right or ability, directly or indirectly, to
obtain, suppress or exclude any information. This Agreement is not intended to affect any
arrangements under any other agreements or memoranda of understanding in existence to which
any ofThe Travelers Regulators is a party. All confidential information, in whatever form,
furnished under this Agreement remains the property ofthe party that provided the information.
By signing below the CID and each The Travelers Regulator confirm (1) their agreement to use
any confidential supervisory information received in connection with the Supervisory College of
The Travelers only for lawful supervisory purposes, (2) their authority and intent, under the laws
oftheir respective jurisdiction, to protect from disclosure and otherwise preserve the confidential
and/or privileged nature of any confidential supervisory information received in connection with
the Supervisory College of The Travelers and (3) their authority to share confidential information
relating to The Travelers and its affiliates with each The Travelers Regulator. The copy ofthe law
establishing such authority for each signatory is attached hereto as Exhibit A.
Before a party to this Agreement discloses confidential information obtained under this
Agreement, the party disclosing the information will seek to obtain prior consent from the party
that provided the information. Ifthere is a legally enforceable demand to disclose non-public
information obtained from the providing party, the other party will (1) notify the providing party
prior to complying with the demand, and seek to obtain prior consent and, ifconsent to such
disclosure is not given, (2) use all reasonable legal means to resist such a demand or protect the
confidentiality ofthe information, including to assert such appropriate legal exemptions or
privileges with respect to such information as may be available and consent to any application by
the providing party to intervene in any action to preserve the confidentiality of the information.
Notice shall be given to the appropriate contact person(s) by email and by mail at the address
specified in the Exhibit B.
