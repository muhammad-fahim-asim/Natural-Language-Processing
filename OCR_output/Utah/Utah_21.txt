This Memorandum of Understanding advances the relationship between Gyeonggi Province of the
Republic of Korea and the State ofUtah ofthe United States of America (hereinafter collectively referred
to as "parties")
Commemorating the 35th year of Sisterhood relations and recognizing that more enhanced exchanges will
benefit their residents, both parties have agreed upon the following:
1. Both parties will promote bilateral trade and commerce SO as to ensure economic advancements in their
respective regions by encouraging the following:
a) Organizing investment and business meetings in their respective regions in tandem with visits by
trade delegations;
b) Participating in G-Fair Korea and the Utah Global Forum and similar events.
2. In commemoration of their deep and enduring friendship, and in recognition of the State ofUtah as the
first Sister Region of Gyeonggi Province, both parties will consider participation by the State of Utah in
the "Gyeonggi World Garden" (tentatively named) to be launched in 2019 in Gyeonggi Province.
3. Both regions will promote educational exchanges to provide their students and residents with
opportunities to understand the history, culture and language ofthe other region.
4. Both regions will encourage cultural exchanges through reciprocal visits of traditional cultural
performance groups in order to enhance mutual understanding and further strengthen inter-regional
relations.
5. Both parties express their commitment and willingness to further develop and deepen their relationship
based on this MOU. This MOU is not intended to create any legally binding obligations between the two
parties.
Signed on the 18th of September in Suwon City of Gyeonggi Province in the Republic of Korea, this
Memorandum of Understanding is executed in duplicate in English and Korean, with both texts being
equally authentic.
