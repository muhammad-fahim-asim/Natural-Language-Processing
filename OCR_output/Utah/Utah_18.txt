BACKGROUND AND RESOLUTION
This Memorandum of Understanding is entered into by the Ministry of Education and Science of
Spain (hereafter referred to as the Ministry), and the Utah State Office of Education (hereafter
referred to as the USOE),
In recognition of the importance that the English and Spanish languages carry as means of
communication between individuals as well as countries, and oft the valuable role they play in the
exchange ofi ideas in the fields of economics, science and culture;
Driven by the same desire to strengthen ties between the citizens of their respective territories
and to cooperate in the efforts that both agencies are exerting to design the educational model of
this new millennium, of which interculturalism will be an essential element;
Conscious of the possibilities that the collaboration of both agencies can offer in the field of
education;
Therefore: a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is hereby signed in due form and agreeing
to the contents established in the following Agreements:

I. GENERAL PRINCIPLES
I.1. - The Ministry and the USOE have established as the main objectives for the cooperative
programs that will be developed under this MOU the improvement of the quality and extent of
the teaching of Spanish language and culture in Utah, and the English language and North
American culture in Spain, in order to:
a) Promote relationships between citizens ofUtah and Spain;
b) Foster knowledge and appreciation oft the culture shared by the two communities;
Provide students who enrol in these programs with a bilingual and multicultural
education that trains them for future personal, academic and professional success;
and
d) Provide professional growth opportunities to teachers ofUtah and Spain.
1.2.-All plans and programs subject to this MOU will be implemented in order to obtain quality
standards in English as well as Spanish.
II. COOPERATIVE PROGRAMS
The basic means of cooperation between the Ministry and the USOE will be implemented
through the following programs:
II.1.- Visiting Teachers.
Each school year, the USOE and the school districts will cooperate with the Ministry in the
selection of a number of qualified teachers from Spain who may be hired under local contract in
elementary, middle and/or secondary classrooms in school districts in Utah.
II.1.1. - The USOE, in collaboration with the Ministry, will promote the assignment of teachers
from Spain who are specially qualified to teach in bilingual programs and/or teach Spanish
language and culture.

II.2. - In-Service' Training Programs for Teachers and Administrators.
II.2.1. - The Ministry will promote the enrolment of teachers and administrators of Utah in
courses provided by Spanish universities for the teaching of Spanish as a second language.
Scholarships will be offered to defray part of the costs of said courses.
II.2.2. - The USOE will take steps to recognize the above cited courses taken at accredited
Spanish universities.
II.3. - International Spanish Academies.
The USOE and the Ministry may collaborate in the creation of a network of English/Spanish
bilingual schools known as International Spanish Academies. The main objective of this
initiative would be to enable the implementation ofl high quality bilingual education with support
from the Ministry and other Spanish institutions, such as Instituto Cervantes, dedicated to the
promotion of Spanish.
In addition to the participation in the programs mentioned in previous clauses of this
Memorandum, the Ministry will offer to the schools integrated in this network the following:
access to dual academic certification, facilitating the accreditation of their studies for their
equivalent ones in the Spanish educational system, specific teacher training activities in the
United States, sister schools in Spain, pedagogical guidance, development of educational projects
with Spanish schools using Internet support and, when available, classroom and curricular
materials and resources.
II.4. -Other ongoing initiatives:
II.4.1. - Internet - World Wide Web: The Ministry and the USOE agree to discuss the
development of e-mail exchanges among students and educators in classrooms in Utah and
Spain, along with the maintenance of a web site for the program.
II.4.2. - Students/Educators Exchange Program: The Ministry and the USOE agree to discuss
the establishment of exchanges between schools in Utah and Spain for groups of up to 15
students accompanied by a teacher, for a period of time of about three weeks, to occur at such
time as to allow each group to attend regular school in the host country, apart from participating
in other activities organized by the receiving school.

application of this program. This report will suggest proposals for improvement.
