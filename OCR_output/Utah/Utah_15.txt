THE MINISTRY OF EDUCATION AND VOCATIONAL TRAINING OF THE KINGDOM OF SPAIN,
(hereinafter referred to as the Ministry), through the Education Office of the Embassy of Spain in
the United States and Canada (hereinafter referred to as the Education Office) represented by the
Counselor of Education in the United States and Canada, Dr. Jesùs Fernandez Gonzalez, by
means of delegation of signature from the Deputy Minister of Education and Vocational Training,
(art 13.3 of the order EFP/43/2021, of January 21, 2021, on delegation of authority)
And,
THE UTAH STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION (hereinafter referred to as the Board), represented
by the State Superintendent of Public Instruction Dr. Sydnee Dickson, in accordance with the
powers vested in her under the rules of the Board, is authorized to act on behalf of the Utah State
Board of Education in making decisions and signing documents.
Both organizations mutually acknowledge that they have sufficient legal capacity to enter into this
Memorandum of Understanding.
Purpose of the Agreement
The Ministry and the Board of Education, hereinafter referred to as the "Signatories", have agreed
as follows:
Recognizing the importance of the English and Spanish languages, both as a means of
communication between individuals as well as countries, and of the valuable role that they play in
the exchange of ideas in the fields of education, economics, science and culture;
Equally determined to strengthen ties between the citizens ofthe Kingdom of Spain and the State
of Utah and willing to cooperate in the endeavors that both administrations are dedicating to the
design of educational models for this century, of which interculturalism will be an essential
element; the Signatories will seek to promote collaborative opportunities that both organizations
can offer in the field of education.
Both Signatories STATE:
The Ministry is responsible for promoting cooperative actions and international relations in the field
of education, in coordination with the Ministry ofl Foreign Affairs, European Union and Cooperation.
That the Board is responsible for pursuing and implementing advances that will enhance student
performance and ultimately generate the kind of educated, highly skilled workforce that will bring
further and improved jobs to the state of Utah.

Both Signatories agree to sign this Memorandum, the purpose of which is to foster and consolidate
a collaborative relationship between the Board and the Ministry in order to promote the Spanish
language and culture, through the following objectives and activities.
1. General Objectives
a. Encourage intercultural exchanges between the citizens of the State of Utah and Spain.
b. Advance the understanding and appreciation of the culture of both countries.
C. Strengthen bilateral ties in education and amplify the presence of the education of Spain in
the United States.
d. Improve the quality of education in both countries by providing bilingual and multicultural
education which prepares students who participate in these programs to enable personal,
academic, and professional success.
e. Provide professional development opportunities to educators and other professionals in the
field of education of Utah and Spain.
2. General Actions
In orderto pursue the aforementioned objectives between thel Ministry and the Board, the following
programs and actions will be engaged in.
a. Encouraging the study of Spanish and the implementation of nigh-performance dual
language programs in Spanish and English in the state of Utah.
b. Facilitating the recruitment of Visiting Teachers from Spain by the schools of the state of
Utah.
C. Disseminating information about the multiple programs promoted by ofthe Education Office
of Spain, such as the Spanish Language Assistants program and the International Spanish
Academies (ISA), as appropriate.
d. Offering quality professional development for educators and administrators.
e. Promoting the participation in conferences or meetings of mutual interest that the
Signatories could encourage.
f. Developing exchanges and networks between students and educators from Spain and Utah.
g. Organizing joint activities that promote the Spanish language and culture targeted at
professionals, educators and students.
h. Disseminating information on cultural activities, professional development, educational
opportunities and other initiatives in Spain and in the State of Utah that may contribute to
the goals of this Memorandum of Understanding.
i. Exchanging regulations and best practices pertaining to educational leadership in
administration and management and bilingual education, as well as in the research towards
the development of specific programs for language educators.
j. Exchanging written instructional materials related to the language and culture of Spain.

3. Economic Obligations
The expenses that may stem from the implementation of this memorandum will be subject to the
yearly budgetary availability of the Signatories, in accordance to the laws and regulations of the
respective Signatories and are non-binding.
4. Evaluations, Monitoring and Observation of the Memorandum
a. So as to monitor, supervise, and observe the fulfilment of this memorandum and the
commitments undertaken by the Signatories, a committee may be set up with equal number
of representatives from each of the Signatories, appointed by the Board and the Ministry,
respectively. The committee shall meet at the request of either party.
b. Any disputes regarding the interpretation and implementation ofthis Memorandum that may
perhaps arise, will be resolved by consulting and conversations between the Signatories,
and may also be resolved by the said committee.
5. Legal Nature and Governing Law
This memorandum is not legally binding nor is it subject to International Law.
6. Duration
a. This memorandum is effective from the date of its signature and has a duration of four years
and will be renewed for a period of up: to four additional years by express agreement ofthe
signatories.
b. This memorandum may be annulled by either party provided that at least a three-month
written notice is given.
C. During the term of the memorandum, the possible provisions for its amendment and/or
improvement may be adjoined when agreed upon in writing by both Signatories. Either
signatory may recommend future modifications in writing that will only go into effect once
said modifications or amendments are signed by both parties.
