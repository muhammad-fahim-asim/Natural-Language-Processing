BACKGROUND AND RESOLUTION
The Utah State Board of Education (the Board) and the Camdes, Instituto da Cooperaçao e da
Lingua, I.P., (the Camoes, I.P.):
4 Recognize the importance that the English and Portuguese languages carry as a
means of communication between individuals as well as countries, and the
valuable role they play in the exchange of ideas in the fields of economy, science,
history and culture.
Are driven by the same desire to strengthen ties between the citizens of their
respective communities andto cooperate.
4 Are conscious of the possibilities that the collaboration of both agencies can
offer to the field of education.
Therefore, a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is hereby signed in due form
and agreeing to the contents established in the following Agreements:
I. GENERAL PRINCIPLES
The objective of this Memorandum of Understanding is to establish the terms of cooperation in
the development ofjoint educational activities in order to:
Support the teaching and learning of their respective languages and cultures;

Promote a better knowledge and understanding of respective educational systems;
Provide professional growth opportunities to their respective teachers and administrators;
Establish collaboration between schools and encourage their collaboration on common
projects.
The programs in this Memorandum of Understanding shall apply to all levels of education
under the supervision of the signatories of this agreement.
Respect for existing programs in both regions is paramount.
II. COOPERATIVE PROGRAMS
II.1 School Connections
The Board and the Camdes, I.P., will create a process for connecting schools to
engage in specific pedagogical projects, sharing resources or findings, creating
teaching materials, or other activities covering all subject areas, both academic and
cultural. The individual schools will be responsible for carrying out the projects,
which may be internet-based exchanges, site visits, etc.
II.2 Guest Teacher Program
The Camdes, I.P. agree to develop a visiting program for teachers of elementary and
secondary education to come to Utah to teach Portuguese Dual Language Immersion.
II.2.A
The Camdes, I.P., will assist the Board in establishing a visiting teachers'
program and in the identification and selection of the visiting teachers. These
should be qualified, locally selected and currently teaching in accredited
education institutions.
II.2.B
Teachers from Portugal or other Portuguese-speaking countries with
appropriate legal status will be considered international guest teachers and hired
under local education agency contracts in Utah for a minimum of one school year
and a maximum of five school years.
II.2.C
The Board will collaborate with the Camoes, I.P., in preparing
professional learning activities for visiting teachers.
II.3 Professional Development
The Board and the Camdes, I.P., agree:
II.3.A
To allow teachers, administrators, other educational personnel in each partner
region to participate in seminars on pedagogy, methodology or other fields of

interests, by means of new technologies or with one trainer going to each partner
region with the objective of disseminating information on teaching practices to a
wide range of teachers in the respective regions
II.3.B
To promote reflection among educators from both regions, on themes jointly
chosen, through study groups held when possible in each region, or via distance
learning, to promote an exchange of educational ideas.
II.3.C
To encourage programs among educators from both regions, such as teacher
mentoring, teacher teaming, and class observations that improve instructional
skills and cultural understanding.
The purpose of the professional development is to improve the quality of education and
encourage exchange of information, knowledge and educational expertise for the benefit
of both parties. In order to reduce the costs and multiply the number of trainers, these
activities will use new technologies (internet exchanges, video conferencing, etc.) to the
extent possible. The Board and the Camdes, I.P., will be responsible for their own
expenses.
III. EVALUATION
Representatives ofthe Board and the Camoes, I.P. will evaluate the activities and results
pertinent to the program development and implementation developed under this MOU.
IV.DURATION
IVI
The term of this MOU will be five (5) years, beginning on the date of signing by parties.
At the end of the agreement, it is renewable by tacit agreement for further one-year
periods.
IV.2
This MOU may be terminated by either the Board or the Camdes, I.P., for any reason
with six (6) months' notice to the other party.
IV.3
During the time the MOU is in effect, if both parties agree, both parties may enter into
addenda that improve its effectiveness.
