Memorandum of Understanding on Economic Cooperation
Between Liaoning Provincial Bureau of Economic Cooperation and
Utah Governor's Office of Economic Development
1. This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is signed between Liaoning
Provincial Bureau of Economic Cooperation, China ("Liaoning BOEC") and Utah
Governor's Office of Economic Development ("UGOED"), collectively referred to
herein as the "parties, 22 aiming to boost mutual understanding and friendship and
economic cooperation between the people of Liaoning Province, China and the
State of Utah, USA,, and enhance both sides' global publicity and competitiveness,
and promote common development.
2. This MOU is based on the following factors:
Liaoning is located in a key geographical location, with a solid industrial
foundation and relatively developed economy. Therefore, it is considered to be one
of China's provinces with the greatest economic growth potential. Liaoning BOEC,
as the government agency responsible for Liaoning's foreign economic
cooperation, is committed to driving forward the province's industrial restructuring,
product upgrading and sustainable and sound growth of its foreign-oriented
economy.
The State of Utah is centrally located in the rapidly growing U.S. west, and
has been recognized as a leader in economic growth. As a government agency,
UGOED aims to promote economic ties with Utah and attract foreign direct
investment into Utah. UGOED has deep experience in experience in promoting
cooperation between governments, organizations and enterprises between China
and the United States.
3. On the principle of realizing mutual development by exerting their respective
interests and advantages, the parties desire to work towards the following:



3.1 The parties will work together in the spirit of friendship and cooperation to the
advantage of one another's businesses and other common interests.
3.2 The parties will maintain close contacts with one another.
3.3 The parties will explore cooperation/exchange in fields such as economy, trade,
technology, culture, education, and health, based on equality and mutual benefit to
develop mutual understanding, friendship and common growth.
3.4 The parties will encourage exchange and cooperation between companies from
both Liaoning and Utah, maintain a platform for communication, and assist in
facilitating exchanges and bilateral trade.
3.5 The parties will establish a mechanism for regular exchange of information to
propose projects and exchanges where mutually beneficial, address concerns, and
generally strengthen friendship.
3.6 The parties will recognize this relationship as a cooperative platform, using
resources and connections to actively promote each other, providing conditions to
support each other's work aiming at a win-win result.
3.7 The parties will abide by the principle of confidentiality, as permitted by their
respective laws, rules, and policies.
3.8 In case of any new problems in their cooperation, the parties may revise or
supplement this MOU.
3.9 It is fully understood that this MOU is a guiding document on principles
without commitments or restrictions. Rather, this MOU is freely entered into in the
spirit of friendship and cooperation. All activities under this MOU are voluntarily
undertaken in that same spirit, with sincere desires for mutual benefit and success.



4. This MOU takes effect from February 24, 2016, concluded in duplicate, with
each of the two parties holding one copy. It can be terminated by any party upon
written notice.
5.This MOU is written in both English and Chinese, and both texts are equally
authentic and effective. It is effective as of the date of the signature.
