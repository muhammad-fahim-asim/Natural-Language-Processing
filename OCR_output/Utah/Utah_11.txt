MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN
THE HASHEMITE KINGDOM OF JORDAN AND THE STATE OF UTAH
The Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan and the State of Utah who both share key objectives of securing a
supply of reliable, efficient, and cost-effective energy and water through the effective use of energy
and water resources express their intent to promote cooperation in the water and energy
infrastructure sectors.
THEREFORE, The Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan through the Ministry of Water and Irrigation and the
Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources, and the State of Utah through the Governor's Office of
Energy Development, express their intention:
1. To seek to expand cooperation in, but not limited to, the development of new technologies
and processes for the efficient use, treatment and reuse of water, integration of renewable
energy: sources, oil and gas exploration and production, and smart grids.
2. To explore the possibility of promoting cooperation between organizations in the water and
energy sectors in the State of Utah and those in the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan.
3. To encourage collaboration in research and development in the energy sphere between
Jordanian and Utah universities as well as, public and private research institutions building
on Utah's Energy Research Triangle Program that addresses Utah specific energy and natural
resource challenges identified in the Governor of Utah Ten Year Energy Plan.
4. To promote the exchange of ideas between various water and energy infrastructure
institutions within The Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan and the State of Utah in support of the
key areas above.
5. To promote the exchange of visits by government and industry representatives, engineers,
scientists and other specialists in the areas of energy and water resources.
This Memorandum of Understanding will be effective from the date of its signatureand does not
create any legally binding rights or obligations for either side.Signed in the city of Amman, Jordan on
22 October, 2017.
