ARTICLE I
Objectives
The present Memorandum of Understanding has as its objectives the
establishment of cooperation between the Parties to further the development of mutual
knowledge of both educational systems; and to support the linguistic and academic
development of Spanish-speaking students, including Mexican students in the state of
Utah, and other students participating in the Utah Alternative Language Services
Programs, through the Visiting Teacher's Program.
ARTICLE II
Activities of Cooperation
For the attainment of the objective in the present Agreement of Collaboration, the
Parties will carry out the following activities of cooperation:
a.
strengthen the bilateral, educational and cultural cooperation between both
educational systems, as well as the mutual understanding and respect between
the communities of the State of Utah and the United States of Mexico;
b.
promote the educational and cultural exchange of teachers with Utah school
districts;
C.
strengthen the linguistic and academic development, the knowledge of history,
culture, values and traditions for the Mexican students, Mexican-Americans,
Hispanics and other participants in the Utah Alternative Language Services
Programs in order to strengthen their identity and to improve their education;
d.
promote appropriate mechanisms for the carrying out of joint projects that will
increase quality of teaching staff;
-2-



e.
support activities that allow the participating teachers to become aware of the
pedagogical innovations and techniques that enrich different areas of teaching;
f.
support research projects that equally benefit and enrich the cultural diversity and
educational systems of Mexico and Utah;
g.
foster a permanent communication between Utah and Mexican teachers. This
exchange will contribute to enriching the linguistic competencies of Utah students
at the preschool, elementary and secondary levels;
h.
reinforce the teaching/learning process in the Spanish language, which is
recognized as a core curriculum course offering in Utan;
i.
agree to the terms of the Memorandum between Mexico and Utah and to the
selection process for the Visiting Teacher's Program;
j.
initiate a visiting program of Mexican teachers specialized in Language
Acquisition, with the capacity to teach subject areas such as mathematics, natural
sciences, humanities, history, Spanish language and special education and other
subjects; and
k.
encourage Utah school districts to participate with the Utah State Office of
Education to offer to place Mexican teachers in Utah school districts to provide
language acquisition at the preschool, primary and secondary levels in Utah
public school districts as part of the state approved Alternative
Services education programs previously designated by the Utah State Languages Office of
Education.

ARTICLE IV
Expenses and Costs
The financial contributions that are required for the execution of the activities that
the present Agreement will be subject to the budgetary availability of both Parties.
At the arrival of the Mexican teachers, neither the State of Utah nor the Utah
State Office of Education is obligated to cover any costs of transportation for the
participating teachers, nor expenses relative to lodging, food, local transportation,
utilities and other personal expenses.
At the arrival of the Mexican teachers, they are responsible for their expenses
related to airfare, housing, utilities, meals, and other personal expenses.
ARTICLE V
Medical Insurance
The Parties will ensure that the visiting teachers are informed that they must
obtain their own medical insurance, at their own expense, until the teachers are covered
by the insurance offered by the Utah public school district employing the teacher.
ARTICLE VI
Entry of Personnel
The Utah State Office of Education will work with participating Utah school
districts to facilitate reasonable activities for the arrival, residence, and departure of
participants in joint projects, which are part of the present Agreement Visiting teachers
will adhere to the applicable laws and rules of the U.S. Home Land Security. Visiting

teachers shall not engage in any other compensated activities outside of the scope of

State Office of Education. Visiting teachers will conform to the receiving state's laws

allowed to remain in this State for a period not to exceed three (3) years.

ARTICLE VII
Resolving Controversies
Any difference derived through the interpretation or application of this common
agreement will be resolved by the parties by mutual agreement.
ARTICLE VIII
Final Provisions
The present Agreement will take effect as of the date of its signing and will have a
term of three (3) years, which can be extended for periods of equal duration upon
execution of a written agreement by the Parties. Either Party may terminate this
Agreement upon one-hundred eighty (180) days' written notice to the other Party.
The present Agreement may be modified by means of the written consent of both I
Parties. These modifications will take effect on the date of their signing.
The termination of the present Agreement will not affect the conclusion of the joint
actions that will have been formalized during the term of this Agreement.
