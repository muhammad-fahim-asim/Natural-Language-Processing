DECLARATION OF INTENT
BETWEEN THE STATE OF UTAH
AND THE: STATE OF ISRAEL
The: State of Utah and the State of Israel (hereinafter referred to as "Sides") share key objectives of
securing a supply of reliable, efficient, and cost-effective energy and water through the effective use of
energy and water resources express theiri intent to promote cooperation in the water and energy
infrastructure sectors.
THEREFORE, the State of Utah through the Governor's Office of Energy Development and the State of
Israel through the Ministry of Energy express theiri intention:
1. To: seeki to expand cooperation in, but not limited to, the development of new technologies and
processes for the efficient use, treatment and reuse of water, integration of renewable energy
sources into the electricity grid, oil and gas exploration and production, and smart grids.
2. To explore the possibility of promoting cooperation between organizations in the water and
energy sectors in Utah and those in Israel.
3. To encourage collaboration in research and development in the energy sphere between Utah
and Israeli universities as well as, public and private research institutions.
4. To promote the exchange ofi ideas between various water and energy infrastructure institutions
within the two States in support of thel key areas above.
5. To promote the exchange of visits by government and industry representatives, engineers,
scientists and other specialists in the areas of energy and water resources.
This Declaration of Intent will be effective from the date of its signature and does not create any legally
binding rights or obligations for either Side. Signed in the city of Jerusglem, this 26 day of lo 2017,
the 6o day of Hechvah5778, according to the Hebrew calendar.
