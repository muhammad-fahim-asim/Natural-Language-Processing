MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
BETWEEN
UTAH STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION
AND
SENAT VON BERLIN
The following points of agreement concern a program of exchange and cooperation
between the Utah State Board of Education and the Senat von Berlin, represented by
the Agency in charge of schools in the federal state of Berlin (SenBildJugFam,
Senatsverwaltung fur Bildung, Jugend und Familie). The exchange program provides
teaching opportunities for qualified German teachers to teach in Utah schools and
training opportunities for qualified Utah educators to participate in an educational
enrichment program in Berlin or other states of the Federal Republic of Germany.
(1) Both the Utah State Board of Education and SenBildJugFam are convinced
that this exchange is of great mutual benefit to both sides.
(2) For each contract offered to a teacher from Germany new to Utah by a
participating school district, one educator from that school or district will
receive one scholarship to attend an Educational Enrichment Program (EEP)
in Berlin the same summer. The EEP will be two weeks long. An educator may
be an administrator at the school board or state level; a principal or assistant
principal; or a teacher. It has to be an American citizen, born and raised in the
United States.
(3) The scholarship mentioned above covers all expenses in connection with the
program occurring in Berlin, i.e. housing, local transportation, a per diem,
planning and other program costs.
(4) The scholarship does not include the cost of transportation to and from Berlin.
(5) SenBildJugFam will implement its responsibilities and the management of the
exchange program through a non-governmental institution. Currently the
program is operated by Checkpoint Charlie Foundation.
The Utah State Board of Education can also assign an institution to handle the
necessary arrangements concerning this program but is not required to do so.
(6) SenBildJugFam will cooperate closely with other German authorities, and the
other German states.

(7) Responsibilities of SenBildJugFam and/or the institution assigned will be the
general management of the program, pre-screening of German exchange
applicants, presentation of their educational levels, certification procedures,
time scheduling, job fair organization in Germany, facilitation of communication
and the coordination of all steps necessary to guarantee the highest possible
success of the program.
(8) Both parties to this agreement shall not be held responsible for any elements
of contracts signed between participating school districts and selected German
teachers or for consequences thereof. The engagement and payment of the
teachers will be undertaken by the school district on the basis of an evaluation
of their qualifications by SenBildJugFam and/or the designated German
institution, the Utah State Board of Education and the employing Utah school
district.
German exchange teachers will serve as fully participating members of the
school district with duties and responsibilities as well as privileges like those of
their colleagues who are regular teachers in that district.
(9) The Utah State Board of Education and/or its designee will cooperate with
SenBildJugFam and/or the designated institution in Germany for acceptable
program planning, providing bulletins and other information materials about the
program to Utah schools and districts, supplying data about needed subject
areas and job placement and coordinating all necessary steps.
(10) In addition to the legal situation described above, both parties accept a
general responsibility for making this exchange a success for the participating
school districts and the German teachers as well as the Utah educators who
come to Berlin for the EEP.
(11) Both sides will promote and strengthen a friendly and cooperative partnership.
All aspects regarding the future development of the program will be arranged
through close association between the Utah State Board of Education and
SenBildJugFam.
(12) This agreement will take immediate effect upon being signed by both parties. It
will remain in effect until either side gives notice by no later than September 1st
of the academic year prior to the academic year for which it wishes to cancel
the agreement.
(13) This agreement shall be construed under the laws of the State of Utah (USA).
