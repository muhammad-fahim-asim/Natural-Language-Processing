BACKGROUND AND RESOLUTION
The Utah State Board of Education and the Académie de Créteil,
+ Recognize the importance that the English and French languages carry as a means of
communication between individuals as well as countries, and the valuable role they
play in the exchange of ideas in the fields of economy, science, history and culture.
* Are driven by the same desire to strengthen ties between the citizens of their
respective communities and to cooperate.
+ Are conscious ofthe possibilities that the collaboration ofl both agencies can offer
to the field of education.
Therefore, a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is hereby signed in due form and
agreeing to the contents established in the following Agreements:
I. GENERAL PRINCIPLES
The objective of this Memorandum of Understanding is to establish the terms of
cooperation on the development of a range ofjoint educational activities in order to:
a. Support the teaching and learning oftheir respective languages and cultures.
b. Promote a better knowledge and understanding oftheir respective
educational systems.
C. Provide professional growth opportunities to teachers of the State of Utah and the
Académie de Créteil.
d. Establish collaboration between schools and encourage their teaming through common
projects
e. Provide the possibility to participate in exchange programs to students of the State of
Utah and the Académie de Créteil in order to develop their skills, especially their
language skills.



The programs in this Memorandum of Understanding shall apply to all levels of education under the
administrative supervision ofthe signatories ofthis partnership agreement.
Under the terms ofthis MOU, the highest level of quality in academic areas and education is sought
after for the students involved, both in English as well as in French programs.
Respect for existing programs in both regions is a prerequisite.
I. COOPERATIVE PROGRAMS
II.1 Connections between schools
The Utah State Board of Education (the Board) and the Académie de Créteil will
create a process for connecting schools (sister schools, internet based exchanges...)
around specific pedagogical projects, sharing resources or findings, creating teaching
materials, or other activities covering all subject areas, both academic and non-
academic or technical. The individual schools will be responsible for carrying out the
projects.
II.2 Teacher Exchanges
The Board and the Académie de Créteil agree to develop through the Jules Verne
Program or equivalent, exchange programs of two to three years for French teachers of
Elementary Education to come to Utah to teach French Dual Language Immersion.
The number of teachers involved and the exchange duration will be agreed by both
parties. In addition to their teaching mission, the USBE will offer French teachers
every opportunity to improve their pedagogical skills in the field of English teaching,
particularly in immersion, by facilitating access to working groups, class observations,
seminars, and pedagogical workshops.
II.3 Language Assistants
Student teaching assistants ofl both English and French from each region (Académie de
Créteil and Utah) are encouraged to be candidates for similar posts in the partner
region according to the regulations of each region.
II.4 Vocational and Language Training for Students
The Board and the Académie de Créteil agree to facilitate the stay of students in
institutions of Utah and the Académie de Créteil (individually or in small groups) and
develop activities to promote general education, as well as technical or professional
education. These may include:
II.4.1
Individual short-term exchanges (two weeks to three months) for students within
the administrative oversight oft the signatory parties during the school year and
2



during the holidays. The Parties will encourage similar kinds of exchanges at the
university level.
II 4.2
Internships in companies in Utah and the Académie de Créteil for students in technical and
vocational education.
Objective: to promote better language skills and enable students to increase their experience
and intercultural skills in order to gain a better understanding of the world around them.
II.5 Professional Development
The Board and the Académie de Créteil agree:
II.5.1
To allow teachers, administrators, other educational personnel in each partner region to
participate in seminars on pedagogy, methodology or other fields of interest, by means of
new technologies or with one trainer going to each partner region with the objective of
disseminating information on teaching practices to a wide of range of teachers in each region.
II.5.2
To encourage programs, such as teacher mentoring, teacher teaming, and class observations,
for teachers or student teachers and administrators.
Objectives: to promote the quality of education and encourage exchange of information,
knowledge, and educational expertise of common interest to both parties for the benefit of all.
In order to reduce the costs and multiply the number of trainers, these activities will be based
on new technologies (internet exchanges, video conferencing) as much as possible.
III. Evaluation
Representatives of the Utah State Board of Education and the Académie de Créteil will
assess the progress regarding the implementation ofthe program and write a report. This
report may propose changes and improvements if necessary.
A copy of this report will be sent to the French cultural services as the Embassy of France in
Washington. D.C. and to the Consulate of France in San Francisco, as well as to the Utah
State Board of Education and to the Académie de Créteil.
IV. Duration
IV.1:
The term of this MOU will be four (4) years, beginning on the date of signing by parties. It
is renewable by tacit agreement for further one-year periods.
3



IV.2:
This MOU may be terminated by either the Utah State Board of Education or the Académie
de Créteil if such a decision is reported to the other signatory by written notice six (6) months
in advance.
IV.3:
During the time that the MOU is in effect both parties may execute addenda that improve its
effectiveness.
IV.4:
Unless otherwise stipulated, and addendum to this MOU will take effect when signed by the
Utah State Board of Education and the Académie de Créteil.
This MOU is equally valid in the State of Utah and in the Académie de Créteil.
