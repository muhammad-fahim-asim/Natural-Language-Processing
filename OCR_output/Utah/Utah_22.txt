Memorandum Recognizing the Sister State Relationship between
the State of Utah, U.S.A. and the Jiangxi Province, P.R.C.
To recognize the Sister State relationship between the State of Utah of the United States of
America and the Jiangxi Province of the People's Republic of China, and to promote concrete
exchanges and co-operations in various fields. Through friendly consultations, the two sides have
reached the following consensus ahead of Jiangxi Province's Executive Vice Governor's visit to
the State ofUtah:
I.
Enhancing regular mutual visits of senior officials to develop exchanges in various fields.
The Foreign Affairs and Overseas Chinese Affairs Office of Jiangxi and the Governor's
Office of Economic Development of Utah shall confirm an exchange plan for the 2018
Jiangxi Province Government Delegation and for future visits.
II.
Encouraging enterprises of both sides to exchange and to cooperate directly. Both
governments shall lay foundations for mutual promotion meetings on tourism, culture,
trade and investment.
III. Strengthening cooperation in education promoting exchanges among institutions ofhigher
learning, middle schools, and primary schools.
IV. The Jiangxi Province wishes to hold a calligraphy and painting exhibition, porcelain
exhibition, and porcelain music show in Utah during the 2018 visit of the Jiangxi
Province Government Delegation.
V.
Promoting overall cooperation in tourism for the Jiangxi Province and the State of Utah
with their respective, relevant agencies.
VI. Cooperation in mutually agreed upon business projects resulting from this visit shall be
given priority in the near future.
VII. The Sister States shall keep regular contacts to facilitate consultations on the exchanges
and cooperation on matters of common concern.
Signed in duplicate in Salt Lake City, Utah on June 22nd, 2017 in Chinese and English languages,
with corroborating texts being equally authentic.
