MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE CONFUCIUS INSTITUTE HEADQUARTERS (KNOWN AS "HANBAN"),
THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA, AND THE UTAH STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION,
(KNOWN AS "BOARD OF EDUCATION"), STATE OF UTAH, UNITED STATES OF
AMERICA. 1
This Memorandum of Understanding (hereafter "MOU") is entered into by Hanban,
represented by
of Hanban and approved signing authority on its behalf; and
the BOARD OF EDUCATION of State of Utah represented by Sydnee Dickson and
approved signing authority on its behalf, hereinafter referred to as "the Parties".
Hanban and the BOARD OF EDUCATION seek to promote cultural and
educational relations beneficial to citizens of both countries.
RECOGNIZING the importance the English and Chinese languages carry as means of
cross-cultural communication, and of the valuable role they play in the people to
people exchanges between the US and China;
DRIVEN by the same desire to strengthen ties between the citizens of their
respective territories and to cooperate in the education, business, and technology
efforts both parties are exerting to design the teaching model of this new age;
CONSCIOUS of the possibilities the collaboration of both parties can offer to the field
of education;
ACKNOWLEDGING the grand vision of dual language education in the State of Utah
as indicated in State International Initiatives (Senate Bill 41-2008 and Senate Bill 117-
2018), the Governor's Language Summit, and the Governor's World Language
Council.
CONSIDERING demands for high-quality dual language Chinese immersion education
in the State of Utah have been increasing;
TAKING INTO ACCOUNT that Hanban is in a unique position to offer financial and
educational resources and assistance in strengthening Chinese language teaching in
Utah public schools; therefore the Parties agree as follows:
ARTICLE I
Objectives
This MOU affirms the intention of the establishment of cooperation between the

Parties to further the development of mutual understanding and to support the
linguistic and academic development of Chinese-speaking students. It will also
support the shared belief that the next generation of Utah citizens would benefit
from developing deep knowledge of the Chinese language and culture to increase
their opportunities in the growing global economy, through educational collaboration.
The cooperative programs which will be supported under this MOU are intended to
facilitate the improvement of the quality and extent of the teaching of the Chinese
language immersion program in the State of Utah, in order to:
Cultivate intercultural competence through global educational experience;
Promote mutual understanding and mutual respect between the two cultures;
Provide students who enroll in these programs with a high level of bilingual
and multicultural education for the student's personal, academic and
professional success in the future;
Offer professional growth opportunities to teachers of the State of Utah and
of The People's Republic of China; and
Advance the scholarship of teaching and learning in Chinese language
immersion language programs through intellectual discussions, conferences,
practicum, and teaching resources development efforts.
ARTICLE II
Program Components
The basic means of cooperation between Hanban and the BOARD OF EDUCATION will
be implemented through the following possible programs upon further negotiations
on each:
1) Establishing Confucius Institutes and Classrooms;
2 Selecting and inviting qualified visiting teachers, and volunteer teaching
assistants from China;
- Developing and acquiring teaching materials suitable for Chinese language
immersion teaching in Utah;
4 Offering scholarships for students with high academic achievements in
Chinese language learning;
5) Providing student immersive summer camp experiences in China;
6) Facilitating educators and school administrators' visiting professional
development programs in Utah and China;
7) Conducting educator professional learning under the direction of Utah State
Board of Education;
8) Organizing Chinese language immersion conference to advance the
scholarship of teaching and learning;
9) Collaborating on exchanges with visiting Chinese students and educators;
10) Coordinating efforts on additional initiatives as mutually agreed upon.

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is not intended to create any legally
binding obligations on either institution but, rather, is intended to facilitate
discussions regarding general areas of cooperation.
The Parties agree to commence good faith discussions with the goal of executing a
final written agreement covering agreed upon activities of cooperation, conditions,
provisions and obligations of the Parties.
The present MOU will take effect as of the date of its signing and will continue until
the final written agreement has been entered into. This MOU can be canceled by
either party on demand when written notice is given. This MOU may be modified by
means of the written consent of the Parties. These modifications will take effect on
the date of their signing.
The parties must give all notices under this MOU in writing via one of the following
methods: (a) confirmed facsimile transmission; (b) postage prepaid registered or
certified mail, return receipt requested; or (c) commercial overnight carrier. All
communications must be sent to the addresses set forth below or to such other
address designated by the parties by written notice. Notices are effective upon
receipt.
