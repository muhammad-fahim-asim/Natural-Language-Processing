MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN
TAIWAN EXTERNAL TRADE DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL
AND
UTAH GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT



This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is entered into between the Taiwan
External Trade Development Council ("TAITRA") and the Utah Governor's Office
of Economic Development ("GOED").
Whereas it has been established that there exists great mutual interests in trade,
investment and economic development of the public and private sectors
oft the contracting parties and that it is the fostering of such mutual
interests that are most desired for the benefit of all parties concerned;
and
Whereas TAITRA and GOED, each representing the private and/or public sectors
of its side and believing firmly in the system of private and public
initiatives for economic development,
it is now, therefore, understood between TAITRA, represented by Walter M.S. Yeh,
President & CEO of TAITRA, and GOED, represented by Brett W.
Heimburger, Director of GOED International Trade and Diplomacy,
that the parties will strive:
1.To take the initiative to exchange trade, investment, market and economic
information and to disseminate to the businessmen of both sides information on
goods, services and partnership possibilities that may be available from either or
both parties;
2. To provide local assistance to the representatives of the other party in matters of
this MOU, especially in regard to the local establishment of representative offices;
3. To notify each other of and assist each other in trade exhibitions, fairs and similar
events promoting the products ofl both parties;
4. To sponsor and promote trade and industrial missions between the two parties to
extend joint-ventures in both parties;
5. To organize meetings and seminars in both parties to inform their businesses about
possibilities of investment, joint-ventures and advantageous trade opportunities for
both parties;
6. To undertake together market studies on sectors presenting potential interests for



both parties;
7. To take steps as may be necessary and feasible to promote trade and economic
cooperation between the two parties;
8. To hold joint meetings when necessary during the course of implementing the
MOU.
This MOU takes effect from the date of signing and can be terminated by any party
upon written notice. All activities under this agreement are voluntarily undertaken
and the parties agree that no part of this agreement may be enforced as binding
by one party upon the other. Rather, this agreement is freely entered into in the spirit
of friendship and cooperation, with sincere desires for mutual benefit and success.
Signed in Taipei, Taiwan on this the 23rd day of March, 2017.
