BACKGROUND AND RESOLUTION
The Utah State Board of Education and the Parana State for its Secretary of Science,
Technology and Higher Education,
Recognize the importance off the English and Portuguese languages as a means of
communication between individuals as well as countries, and of the valuable role
the languages play in the exchange of ideas in the fields of economy, science,
history and culture;
Are driven by the mutual desire to strengthen ties between the citizens of their
respective communities: and to cooperate in the efforts ofl both agencies to design
the educational model of this new millennium; interculturalism will be an
essential element;
Are conscious of the possibilities that the collaboration of both agencies offer in
the field of education;
Therefore: a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is hereby signed in due form and
both parties accept the following Agreements:
I. GENERAL PRINCIPLES
The objective of this Memorandum of Understanding is to establish the terms of
cooperation in the development of joint educational activities in order to:
a. Support the teaching and learning of the respective languages and cultures;
b. Promote a better knowledge and understanding of respective educational
systems;

The programs in this Memorandum of Understanding shall apply to all levels of
education under the supervision of the signatories of this agreement.
Respect for existing. programs in both regions is paramount.
II. COOPERATIVE PROGRAMS
11.1 Connections between schools
The Utah State Board of Education and the Parana State Secretary of Science,
Technology and Higher Education will create a process for connecting schools
(twin schools, internet based exchange) around specific pedagogical projects,
sharing resources or findings, creating teaching materials, or other activities
covering all subject areas, both academic and non-academic/technical. The
individual schools will be responsible for carrying out the projects,
11.2 Teacher and administrator exchanges
The Board and the Parana State Secretary of Science, Technology and
Higher Education agree to develop two year exchange programs for
Brazilian teachers of Elementary Education to come to Utah to teach
Portuguese Dual Language Immersion. Teachers from other fields of studies
and educational administrators may be included in these programs or other
exchange seminars.
11.3 Language assistants
English and Portuguese student teaching assistants from each region (Parana
and Utah) are encouraged to be candidates for similar posts in the partner
region consistent with the regulations of each region,
11.4 Vocational and Language Training for Students
The Board and the Parana State Secretary of Science, Technology and Higher
Education agree to facilitate student educational visits or experiences to
schools in Utah and Parana (individually or in small groups) and develop
activities to promote general education and technical or professional
education. These experiences may include:

summer vacations. The Parties will encourage similar kinds of exchanges at

III. EVALUATION
III.1
Annually, representatives of the Board and the Parana State Secretary of Science,
Technology and Higher Education will assess the progress of this program and
write a report. This report may propose changes and improvements. A copy of
this annual report will be sent to the Parana State Secretary of Science,
Technology and Higher Education, as well as to the Board.
III.2
The Board and the Parana State Secretary of Science, Technology and Higher
Education agree that representatives of both administrations will evaluate the
programs under this Memorandum at meetings which will alternate between
the parties' primary locations.
11I.3
In order to achieve this term there will be no transfer of financial or material
resources by the signatories of this adjustment.
111.4
The execution of this term does not give rise to any responsibilities, whether civil,
labor or other, reciprocally to the signatories.
IV. DURATION
IV.I
The term of this MOU will be five (5) years, beginning on the date of signing by
parties. At the end of the agreement, terms of renewal will be discussed. If
desired, either party may forward a letter of non-renewal: 30 days priortot the date
of expiration to the other party.



IV.2
This MOU may be terminated by either the Board or the Parana State Secretary
of Science, Technology and Higher Education for any reason with six (6) months
notice to the other party.
IV.3
During the time the MOU is in effect; if both parties are in agreement, may enter
into additives with a view to improving their effectiveness.
IV.4
Except as otherwise provided, any addendum to this Memorandum of
Understanding shall enter into force upon signature by the Utah State Board of
Education (USBE) and the Secretariat of Science, Technology, and Higher
Education of the State of Parana.
This MOU is equally valid in the State of Utah and in the State of Parana.
