MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN
HANOI PEOPLES COMMITTEE
THE SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM
AND
THE GOVERNMENT OF UTAH
THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
ON
THE ESTABLISHMENT OF
FRIENDSHIP AND COOPERATION



PREAMBLE
WHEREAS the Two Parties hope to establish better goodwill, friendship,
cooperation and create mechanisms for such within the framework of their
respective jurisdiction on the basis of mutual respect and mutual benefit, in
conformity with the laws and policies of the two countries and the two localities.
The State of Utah of the United States and the Hanoi People's Committee ofthe
Socialist Republic of Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as "the Two Parties"),
through friendly consultation, have reached the following understanding:
Article I: Purpose
The objectives of this Memorandum of Understanding are to establish and
expand bilateral cooperation, strengthen communication, enhance trust and
mutual understanding between the city ofl Hanoi and the state of Utah in various
areas, as well as promote friendship between people-to-people exchanges,
contributing to the achievements of Comprehensive Partnership established
between the Unites Stated and the Socialist Republic of Vietnam as reflected in
the Joint Statement by The President of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and
The President ofthe United States of America on July 25, 2013.
Article II: General Principles
The Two Parties agree to enhance and expand cooperative efforts according to
the following general principles:
1. The Two Parties will encourage and develop cooperation and exchanges
on the basis of equality, mutual benefit, and reciprocity.
2. Such exchanges and cooperation will be subject to the constitutions and
applicable laws and regulations of the respective countries. Within this
framework, the Parties will make every effort to promote favorable
conditions for the fulfillment ofthis cooperation and these exchanges.
3. The cooperation provided for in this Memorandum is not intended to
affect relations currently established between the state of Utah and/or the
city of Hanoi with any partners. Rather, the Two Parties will attempt to
identify new areas for joint activities, or, where deemed appropriate by all
concerned, to strengthen or expand specific programs.



Article III: Forms of Cooperation
The Two Parties are committed to creating an enabling environment for and
providing necessary assistance to each others. Cooperation may be conducted in
the following ways:
1. To exchange experience on regular basis and discover and work in a
timely manner to solve concerns and difficulties the Two Parties have in
cooperation;
2. To exchange information on a regular basis about the areas of cooperation
and collaborated events;
3. To promote key projects and programs of trade and economic
cooperation;
4. To exchange high-level delegations and support companies/enterprises in
seeking business opportunities and further cooperation;
5. To organize delegations of entrepreneurs to attend main events held in the
state ofUtah and the city ofHanoi;
6. To promote the cities/localities of Utah to develop friendly cooperation
relations with the city ofl Hanoi.
Article IV: Areas of Cooperation
The Two Parties will cooperate in the following key sectors:
General education and staff training;
Promote technology transfer and research development.
Transportation infrastructure building;
Information technology;
Pharmaceutics and healthcare using advanced technologies;
High-tech agriculture;
Energy;
Manufacturing;
Tourism;
e Environmental Protection.



Article V: Authorized Contact Points
The Two Parties respectively designate Hanoi Department of Foreign Affairs
and the Utah Governor's Office of Economic Development as contact points for
communication and information exchange, and recipients of any notice required
to be given under this MOU.
Article VI: Effectiveness and Duration
This MOU will become effective on the date of signature. Either of the Two
Parties may terminate this MOU upon written notice. All activities under this
agreement are voluntarily undertaken and the parties agree that no part of this
agreement may be enforced as binding by one party upon the other. Rather, this
agreement is freely entered into in the spirit of friendship and cooperation, with
sincere desires for mutual benefit and success.
Signed in Hanoi on August 30t, 2017 in duplicate in the English and
Vietnamese languages; both texts being equally authentic.
