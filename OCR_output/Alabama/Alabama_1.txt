Agreement Between The State of Alabama, Alabama Development Office (ADO)
and
The State of Brandenburg
Ministry of Economics, Small and Medium Business, and Technology (MWMT)
Whereas:
It is agreed by and between the ADO and the MWMT that the
prospects for expanded economic cooperation between the two
states are excellent and as a result the economic structure of both
states will mutually benefit;
Whereas:
the ADO and MWMT each have important responsibilities for
encouraging the growth of trade and investment relations within
the framework of a free enterprise economy;
Now, Therefore, It is agreed between ADO and MWMT
1.
To improve the trade relationships of the entrepreneurs of each of
the two states.
2.
To support and facilitate cooperation between entrepreneurs of
the two states.
3.
To facilitate investment of entrepreneurs of the two states for the
mutual benefit of both states.
4.
To encourage and expand the transfer of technology between the
two states to the mutual benefit of both states.
5.
To promote tourism and travel opportunities for and between the
peoples of both states.
6.
To promote the exchange of ideas between various businesses,
trade associations, business agencies, and commercial institutions
within both states.
7.
To cooperate in the fields of post-secondary education and
industrial training.
8.
To notify each other of trade fairs and exhibitions, investment
seminars, and other business related conferences to be held in
each party's respective state and to render appropriate and timely



assistance when requested.
9.
To promote the exchange of visits of entrepreneurs, engineers,
scientists, and other specialists.
10.
To establish an addresse and point of contact to be utilized by
visting entrepreneurs SO they may obtain assistance when visiting
the other state.
The above points are provided especially for the benefit for small and medium
businesses in each of the two states.
