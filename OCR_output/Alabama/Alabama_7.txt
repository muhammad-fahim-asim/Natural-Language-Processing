Memorandum of Understanding on Friendly Cooperation
Between the State of Alabama, U.S.A.
and the Province of Hubei, P.R.C.
From July 10th to 12th, 2006, At the invitation of the Honorable Luo Quingquan,
Governor of Hubei Province, the Honorable Bob Riley, Governor oft the State of Alabama,
led a business mission to Hubei Province for an official visit. During the trip, The
Honorable Luo Quingquan, Governor of Hubei Province held productive discussions
with the Honorable Bob Riley, Governor of the State of Alabama. Through friendly
discussion, both parties have thus decided to enter into this Memorandum of
Understanding with the goal of enhancing the state-province friendly relations of
cooperation between Hubei and Alabama,:
Both parties share a common desire to promote multi-level and multi-form
cooperation in various fields on the basis of the existing relations of cooperation.
Both parties will further strengthen the cooperation in the fields of agriculture,
forestry, animal husbandry, fishery, pulp and paper, bio-technology and carry out
personal and information exchanges through consultation.
Both parties will actively promote the friendly exchanges between Hubei
Provincial People's Congress and the Alabama State Legislature to establish the
mechanism of long-term exchanges.
Both parties designate Hubei Provincial Foreign Affairs Office and Alabama
Development Office respectively as liaisons responsible for the state-province
friendly exchanges.
As the supplement to the Sister-State Friendship Agreement, this document serves to
promote and expand effective and mutually beneficial cooperation and development in
both states within the limits of their financial, material and personal services capabilities
and in conformity with the prevailing laws and regulations in their respective countries
and states.
IN WITNESS HEREOF the undersigned, being authorized by their respective
Governments, do sign this Memorandum of Understanding on Friendly Cooperation in Wuhan
City, Hubei Province, P.R.C. on the 11th day of July in the year 2006, in duplicate in
Chinese and English, both texts being equally authentic.
