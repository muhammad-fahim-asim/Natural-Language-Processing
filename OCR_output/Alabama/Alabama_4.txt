MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING AND
COOPERATION BETWEEN THE
ALABAMA DEVELOPMENT OFFICE
AND THE
WIRTSCHAFTSFORDERUNG BRANDENBURG
GMBH
Potsdam, January 14th, 1994



Whereas:
It is agreed by and between the Alabama
Development Office (hereinafter referred to as
ADO)
and
the
WirtschaftsfOrderung
Brandenburg GmbH (hereinafter referred to
as WFB), the parties hereto, that the prospects
for expanded ecenomic cooperation between
Brandenburg and Alabama are excellent and
give promise of yielding substantial mutual
benefits based on the complentary economic
structure of the two states; and
Whereas:
the ADO and the WFB each bear important
responsibilities for encouraging the growth of
trade and investment relations within the
frameworks of a free enterprise economy;
Now, Therefore,
It is agreed between ADO and WFB that each
will take necessary steps to:
1.
Promote
mutual
understanding
and
cooperation between the respective private
sector business communities of Alabama and
Brandenburg in the fields of trade, technology
transfer and investment relations.
2.
Exchange and disseminate relevant business
and economic information, on goods and
services that are available from the other
country to businessmen of their respective
countries.
3.
Notify each other of trade exhibitions and
investment seminars to be held in each party's
respective state, and render appropriate and
timely assistance when requested.



4,
Sponsor and promote exchanges of visits by
delegations and businessmen of the two states
and extend cooperation and assistance to the
visitors SO sponsored by the other state.
5.
Additional items may be added to this
document as mutually agreed upon by both
parties.
