MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
STATE OF ALABAMA, THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
AND
JIANGSU PROVINCE, THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA
Background
Based on the bonds of friendship and in the spirit of common development, the State of
Alabama and Jiangsu Province have henceforth agreed to enter into the Memorandum
of Understanding. This document serves to promote and expand effective and mutually
beneficial cooperation and exchanges between the two territories.
Objective
The goals of the agreement are as follows:
1. To facilitate trade between the industrial and services sectors of Alabama and
Jiangsu, promote investments, expand technology transfers, and advance research
and development;
2. To advocate the exchange of ideas between various businesses, trade associations,
business agencies, and commercial institutions by maintaining information channels
between. Alabama and Jiangsu;
3. To gain reciprocal understanding of emerging industries in Alabama and Jiangsu,
such as biotechnology, agricultural technology, information technologies, etc.;
4. To identify economic needs in both territories and how these needs can be met
through bilateral cooperation.
Agreed Areas of Cooperation
Both parties agree to advance cooperation in the following key fields
1. Advanced manufacturing, especially auto
2. Life sciences, including biotechnology, bio medicine and research
3. High technology, including information technology
4. Higher education, including, joint training and degree programs
5. Energy
