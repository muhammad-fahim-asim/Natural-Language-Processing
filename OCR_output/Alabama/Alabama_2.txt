JOINT DECLARATION
FOR THE PURPOSE OF ESTABLISHING
THE SEUS-CANADIAN PROVINCES ALLIANCE
This Joint Declaration affirms and announces our intention, by and on behalf
of our respective jurisdictions, to establish the Southeastern United States-
Central and Eastern Canadian Provinces Alliance, hereinafter called "SEUS-
Canadian Provinces Alliance".
The relationship between Canada and the United States of America is marked
by generations of peaceful coexistence and extensive economic interaction
across the longest shared border of any two countries in the world.
In this cooperative spirit, the undersigned representatives of the Central and
Eastern Canadian Provinces and the Southeastern United States recognize and
believe that the establishment of the SEUS-Canadian Provinces Alliance will
be in the best interests of our citizens and economies. The SEUS-Canadian
Provinces Alliance will present the opportunity for businesses to enhance
commercial exchanges, promote two-way investment and encourage
technological and scientific exchanges between Provinces and States.
For the designated Southeastern United States, the Canadian market is already
of primary international importance. For the Central and Eastern Canadian
Provinces, the Southeast constitutes one of the fastest growing regions in the
United States and presents opportunities to enhance existing business
relationships and to develop new ones. The SEUS-Canadian Provinces
Alliance promises to bring focus to these relationships and to expand
opportunities in underdeveloped sectors and markets through new
partnerships.
Through this Joint Declaration, we signal our desire to sustain and improve
our long-standing friendship and to pursue mutually beneficial economic
outcomes.
THEREFORE, we endorse the establishment of the SEUS-Canadian
Provinces Alliance, the focus on which shall be on strengthening existing
economic ties while creating a fertile environment for businesses of all sizes to
succeed, and fostering opportunities for technological collaboration.

The SEUS-Canadian Provinces Alliance will function within the framework
of the general relations between the Governments of Canada and the United
States of America and by the proper observation of corresponding laws and
policies of both countries, Provinces and States.
We the undersigned do hereby support the establishment the SEUS-Canadian
Provinces Alliance and do pledge the best efforts of our respective
Governments to ensure the mutual benefits and practical results cited herein.
Done at Montréal, this 16th day of November 2007, in thirteen copies, in the
English and French languages.
