MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING FOR THE STRENGTHENING OF THE
COOPERATION BETWEEN THE GOVERNMENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF
PERU AND THE GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF ALABAMA
The Government of the Republic of Peru and the Government of the State of
Alabama, hereinafter "the Parties";
Reaffirming the excellent relations between the Parties and being determined to
establish a framework of cooperation through this Memorandum of
Understanding;
Acknowledging the vast opportunities to strengthen bilateral trade and
investment as key components for economic and social development and in
favor of all sectors of our societies;
Recognizing the willingness of both Parties to develop mutually beneficial
programs and projects in areas of common interest, such as agriculture and
aquaculture, education and cultural affairs, research, forestry and innovation,
among others;
Have reached the following understandings:
ARTICLEI
PURPOSE
This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is based on the following common
objectives of the Parties:
a) To engage in mutually beneficial economic, commercial, educational,
research, cultural and environmental activities;
b) To promote the organization of trade and investment missions; facilitating
the identification of economic opportunities for the Parties;
C To foster academic-to-academic partnerships among educational
institutions and business-to-business activities between private entities;
and
d) To organize visits and exchange of experts and researchers and consider
opportunities for joint projects, conferences, internships, and training.



ARTICLE II
AREAS OF COOPERATION
The Parties intend to cooperate and coordinate efforts, including and with special
emphasis on, but not limited to, the following priority action areas:
Trade and Investment
Agriculture, Poultry and Livestock
Aquaculture
Science and Technology
Education and Cultural Affairs
Forestry
ARTICLE III
IMPLEMENTATION
In order to facilitate the implementation of this MOU, officials from the Embassy
of Peru in the United States and the Government of the State of Alabama will
address periodically specific cooperation action plans in order to achieve the
objectives established in this Memorandum and coordinating the involvement of
their own institutions.
The Parties will encourage their corresponding agencies, public and private
institutions, education and research institutions, companies and citizens to
actively participate in the above-mentioned areas, thus contributing toward the
successful implementation of this Memorandum of Understanding.
ARTICLE IV
ENTRY INTO FORCE
This Memorandum of Understanding is effective on the date it is signed and does
not create legally binding rights or obligations for the Parties.
ARTICLE V
MODIFICATIONS
This Memorandum of Understanding may be modified at any time by mutual
consent of the Parties.



ARTICLE VI
DURATION
This Memorandum of Understanding is indefinite. However, any Party may
withdraw from the Memorandum of Understanding by written notification to the
other party 30 days in advance. Such notification will not have effect on the
activities in progress unless the Parties mutually decide otherwise.
SIGNED this sixteenth day of January, 2015 at Montgomery, Alabama in
duplicate in the English and Spanish languages, each text being equally valid.
